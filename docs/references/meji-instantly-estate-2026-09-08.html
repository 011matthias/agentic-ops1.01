At a glance: two eras

The account splits cleanly in two. Five campaigns predate our engagement and were built around a different product mix. Four are ours, built as the three-piece pilot in June 2026.

Inherited  Nov 2025, before us

  • Spans three different businesses: the Christmas party product, the MejiAI chatbot line, and Banter
  • Two are stopped with bounce rates above 5 percent; one is paused; one is complete; one is nominally active but has not sent since Feb 2026

Ours  Jun 2026, the three-piece pilot

  • 4 campaigns, 2,499 leads, 5,512 emails, 117 replies, 17 tagged interested

  • Strict mailbox-to-piece routing, no cross-wiring, enforced by a validator hook
  • More replies from a third of the send volume
The number that matters for the end-of-September decision

Corporate cold has never produced a tagged opportunity. Across all three corporate campaigns ever run (the inherited Big Companies list and our A/B split), that is 3,906 emails to 1,903 people, 9 replies, 0 tagged interested. By contrast the warm re-engagement list replies at 4.2 percent and the Christmas cold list at 0.9 percent. Caveat worth holding: "interested" is a manual tag, so 0 could mean nobody was interested or it could mean the 9 replies were never tagged. The API cannot separate those two.

Reading the two "Stopped" campaigns

Big Companies in UK and MejiAI Construction both sit in the same non-active state, and both are the only two campaigns in the account above a 5 percent bounce rate (5.9 and 5.6). That is the band where Instantly's bounce protection halts a campaign, so the cause is near certain. The precise meaning of the underlying status code is not verified against Instantly's docs, so it is reported here as "stopped" rather than given a label the API has not confirmed.

How it got here

  • Oct to Nov 2025
    Account built out before our engagement
    12 mailboxes created across mejimedia.co, mejievent.com, mejiai.com and banterexp.com. Five campaigns launched inside three weeks, covering three separate businesses.
  • Nov 2025 to Jan 2026
    Two campaigns stop themselves on bounce rate
    MejiAI Construction runs for a single day. Big Companies in UK stops with 258 people never emailed and 9 frozen part-way through the sequence. Neither was restarted.
  • Feb 2026
    Christmas Bookers goes quiet
    Last send 5 Feb 2026. The campaign still reads as active, but its three sending mailboxes are on a domain that no longer resolves.
  • 1 Jun 2026
    We diagnose mejimedia.co as NXDOMAIN
    The domain does not exist in public DNS: no A record, no MX. Instantly had auto-disabled all three mailboxes for degrading the warmup pool. Reactivation is structurally impossible, since the reactivation code is sent to an address that cannot receive mail. Piece 1 is rerouted to a single working mailbox.
  • 6 to 8 Jun 2026
    The three-piece pilot goes live
    Piece 2 splits corporate cold into Decision-Makers and Organisers. Piece 1 launches warm re-engagement to past attendees on gurmej@mejimedia.com alone.
  • 9 Jun 2026
    Double-send bug caught and fixed the same day
    All three live campaigns were sending touch 1 and its follow-up about 20 minutes apart. Instantly's per-step delay is the gap before the next email, and the loaders had set it on the wrong step. Fixed, verified clean at the next sending window, and guarded in the loader scripts. Roughly 50 leads had already been double-sent.
  • 19 Jun 2026
    Piece 2B auto-pauses at 5.2 percent bounce
    The organiser and EA segment is the bounce-prone one. Response was a standing no-bulk-load rule: verify through NeverBounce, drip in batches of 50 or fewer, check the bounce rate between batches. Enforced in the loader, not left to memory.
  • 26 Jun 2026
    Piece 3 launches on its own domain
    Christmas cold, restricted to the three venue cities. It goes on to finish its list with a 0.4 percent bounce rate, the cleanest in the account.
  • 1 Jul 2026
    Corporate audience retargeted, Piece 3 ramped
    The June-generation corporate list (880 remaining leads on the 11 to 50 staff CEO/MD targeting) was the wrong ICP and was retired. New targeting is 201 to 2000 staff functional bookers, split by seniority rather than company size. Piece 3 daily limit raised 50 to 90.
  • 17 to 20 Jul 2026
    Piece 3 completes, Piece 1 bounce spike handled
    36 twice-unverifiable leads removed from Piece 1 on owner approval.
  • Aug 2026
    Corporate lists run dry, Make capacity doubled
    Piece 2A completes. Make operations tier moved 20k to 40k on 28 Aug, which also re-anchored the billing cycle to the 28th.
  • 3 to 8 Sep 2026
    September relaunch prepared, held on client answers
    Campaign status report and launch line-up sent to Gurmej. Plan is to upgrade A and B in place rather than retire them, plus a new Version C for named accounts. Nothing sends until two answers arrive.

Spare capacity for a Christmas push, without touching corporate

The Christmas side and the corporate side sit on different domains, so they do not compete. Available right now for Christmas: 180 emails a day from gurmej@mejimedia.com (90) and the two mejixmas boxes (45 each). A 2,400 person list at three touches needs about 150 a day across seven weeks, so mailboxes are not the constraint on the Christmas side. Corporate has its own separate 90 a day. bookings@christmasofficeparty.co.uk is deliberately excluded from that 180: it was created as the second sender for the inbound enquiry pipeline, and putting cold outbound on the enquirer-facing brand domain is the one thing that build exists to prevent.

What we changed

Infrastructure

  • Found the dead domain. mejimedia.co has no DNS at all; its three mailboxes were pool-banned by Instantly and cannot be recovered. Piece 1 was rerouted to a single working sender.
  • Separated the audiences by domain. One domain per piece, with a hard no-cross-wire rule and a validator hook that fails client drafts which mix them up.
  • Added a second Christmas domain (mejixmas.com) and warmed it before use.

Sending mechanics

  • Fixed the double-send. Instantly's step delay is the gap before the next email, not after the current one. The loaders now guard it.
  • Stopped bulk-loading the bounce-prone segment. Organisers go through NeverBounce, in batches of 50 or fewer, with a bounce check between batches.
  • Ramped what was working. Piece 3 went from 50 to 90 a day once its bounce rate proved clean.

Targeting

  • Retired our own June corporate list. 880 remaining leads on the original 11 to 50 staff CEO/MD targeting, replaced with 201 to 2000 staff functional bookers.
  • Split by seniority, not company size. Deciders and event owners in A, organisers in B, because the two segments bounce and reply very differently.
  • Kept Christmas cold to the three venue cities rather than going UK-wide.

Measurement

  • Verified what the API actually means. Status 1 includes people never emailed, which had been double-counting fresh leads in our own reporting.
  • Bounced leads confirmed as the negative lead status, matching exactly on 8 of 9 campaigns.
  • Weekly reporting and a reply-SLA monitor built and run unattended.

What needs work

1. There is effectively no opt-out record

The account-wide block list holds one address. The campaigns carry no unsubscribe link by deliberate choice, so Instantly has recorded zero unsubscribes across all 20,132 emails ever sent. The only opt-out signal that exists is 52 people manually tagged "not interested" plus whatever sits in the reply threads. Re-mailing the Christmas lists without rebuilding a stop list first would re-contact people who already asked to stop.

2. Corporate has produced no opportunities in three attempts

3,906 emails, 1,903 people, 9 replies, 0 tagged interested, across the inherited campaign and both of ours. Gurmej is giving it until the end of September. Worth knowing that more volume would test reach, not whether the message lands.

3. The September relaunch is blocked on two client answers

Nothing can send until Gurmej gives the word on what to do with Big Companies in UK, and supplies three to five inbox addresses for placement testing. The wait comes out of the month he is judging.

4. Smaller open items

mejievent.com DMARC is still on monitor-only, which is weaker than it should be for the domain carrying the corporate volume. The placement seed file is empty. bookings@christmasofficeparty.co.uk has been warm and idle since 16 Jul, waiting on the inbound multi-inbox build (A1/A3 send router, A2 dual-inbox reply detection) that has not shipped; it is reserved for that, not spare outbound capacity. Christmas Bookers needs its senders swapped before it could ever run again.
