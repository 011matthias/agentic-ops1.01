### Phased commercial structure
- **Choice:** Lead with hourly for the build, defer the retainer offer and its number until the system is proven, treat Make→n8n migration as a separate later fixed-price pitch.

### Recommended retainer model
- **Choice:** Base + performance share, seasonally weighted (Aug-Dec peak multiplier); scope tiers as the expansion ladder; hours-bank overage as margin protection.
- **Rationale:** A flat fee under-prices the seasonal peak and the upside, the two places most money is left on the table; tying premium to his results is the only way an anti-pushy client pays more without churning.

### Migration pitch deferred, not dropped
- **Choice:** Removed from the intro message (user did this), recorded as a milestone-triggered future action with a required cost-benefit analysis.
- **Rationale:** Pitching infra migration before the motion is proven reads as upsell; same trust logic as the retainer.
