## Key Decisions Made

### Track placement: startups (not solutions)
- **Choice:** Logged in `ideas/startups/`, not `ideas/solutions/`
- **Rationale:** Creative IP bet aimed at direct audience monetization, not a productized service for clients. Hit-driven economics, IP-as-asset, compounding-defensibility shape (if a character breaks out).

### Brand isolation from operator brand
- **Choice:** Path 2 over Path 1 — separate identity, handles, payment rails, device fingerprint
- **Rationale:** User chose pure-bet evaluation. Outcome doesn't compound with or compromise UnpauseAI / Matthias operator brand. Trade-off accepted: gives up IP-compounding-with-personal-brand upside.

### Kill metric: hit signal, not revenue
- **Choice:** 500K organic views on one video OR 20K subscribers by day 90
- **Rationale:** In hit-driven categories the kill metric must be a hit signal. Operators who scale on tepid signal die slowly. Conservative thresholds (Italian brainrot peaks hit them in 2-3 weeks).

### Hold pending concept lock
- **Choice:** Brief is committed but bet is parked; not launching yet
- **Rationale:** The concept IS the bet in this category. Italian brainrot worked because someone invented Bombardiro; Bigfoot vlogs worked because someone committed to the bit. Without a concrete character/world/format angle, this is intent not bet.

### Working Notes

**The reframe that mattered.** First-half analysis treated the user's "automated AI brainrot videos" as the standard slop-farm format. Inhibiting-factor list was comprehensive against that frame (platform demotion, EU AI Act, CPM compression, etc.). User pushed back with Bombardiro Crocodilo / Bigfoot vlog examples, which surfaced a completely different format: long-form, declared-AI, character-IP-driven, audience-in-on-the-bit. That format **escapes** most of the filters: original IP passes unoriginal-content demotion, visible effort passes mass-produced policy, audience-knows-it's-AI removes the deception penalty, long-form CPMs are 5-10x higher.

**Strategic conclusion.** The category that works in 2026 is post-irony AI character content, not faceless AI slop. Production is hours per video (creative direction, voice acting, continuity QA), not push-button automation. The "automatic" framing was the wrong mental model.

**Brand-separation tradeoff.** User chose Path 2 (isolated identity) over Path 1 (integrated with operator brand). Path 1 would have compounded the IP upside with the personal-brand authority play, but couples the outcomes. Path 2 isolates the downside, gives up the compounding upside.

**Hit-driven economics.** Power-law distribution. Most attempts produce nothing. One hit (a Spotify-charting character, a viral mockumentary) carries everything. 90-day window is chosen as a kill-fast horizon, conservative against Italian brainrot's 2-3 week peak times.
