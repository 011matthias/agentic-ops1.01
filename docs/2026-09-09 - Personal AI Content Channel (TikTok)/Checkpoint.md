## Key Decisions Made

### This is not an acquisition channel
- **Choice:** No CTA, no bio funnel, no LinkedIn-repurposing rationale, and it
  does not draw on the 5-7 h/wk uwi acquisition budget. Metric is watch time,
  saves, and whether the owner is still posting in six weeks.
- **Rationale:** Owner ruling, 2026-09-09: "not to gain clients rather just to
  get out there and inspire or something." The first answer had been built
  entirely as a GTM channel and defended against the committed strategy.

### The income-claim hook is rejected
- **Choice:** Recommended against "3,0 Abi und jetzt verdiene ich online Geld"
  as the channel's centre. Keep the orientation arc, drop the money.
- **Rationale:** The hook makes an income promise that invites "wie viel?", and
  the real 2026 figures (~EUR8.5k gross for the year, EUR0/mo recurring, one
  client halted, one capped) do not support it. That leaves dodging or
  inflating. It is also the most saturated and least trusted format in the
  German-language space. Not yet owner-ruled.

### Language fork surfaced, not settled
- **Choice:** Flagged that an Abi-centred arc makes the channel German by
  construction (smaller reach, far less competition in the agent topic).
- **Rationale:** "3,0" carries no meaning outside DE. Owner has not ruled.
