**Type:** client-dev
**Focus:** Closed Gap 1 of the Zoho month-end injection with a per-org category-to-account map, then posted and readback-verified the 13 USD July purchases in the TEST-BTS sandbox under owner greenlight.
**Projects:** brisken
**Built:** zoho/category_accounts.py (per-org category to GL code, fallback-only, production orgs unmapped) + leaf-account assertion; 15 tests in test_zoho_category_accounts.py; runbook sections for the map and the rehearsal. PRs #1193 (f4f31d32) and #1194 (93ca41c0).
**Did NOT work:** First category-to-account table mapped three categories to PARENT accounts (Professional Fees E600060, Travel Expense E100010, Marketing & Selling Expenses E600010) (why not: Zoho posts only to leaf accounts; parents are roll-ups. ChartOfAccounts.leaf_accounts already encoded the rule and caught all three before anything posted. Now asserted by test_every_shipped_mapping_targets_a_leaf_account, with a companion test proving that assertion can fail.); Mapping the 'Travel & Transport' category to any single leaf account (why not: Its two July rows are gasoline (E100010-01 Automobile Expense) and a vehicle rental (E100010-36 Rental Cars), and Travel Expense has no generic leaf to hold both. Left deliberately unmapped: a default wrong half the time is worse than a refusal that names the row.); Regenerating the payloads for readback verification via plan_expense_post (why not: It refused all 13 with already_in_ledger because the ledger had just recorded them. Correct behaviour, wrong tool: the readback uses build_expense_payload, which does not consult the ledger, and the refusal became a separate duplicate-guard assertion.); Sending vendor attribution as the text field vendor_name (why not: Zoho accepts it, returns 201 and stores nothing: 0 of 13 posted records carry vendor_name or vendor_id, and the audit note does not carry the vendor either. The payload builder's comment claimed it 'keeps the name visible'; measured false on 2026-09-23.); A name-only comparison of July account references against the charts (earlier in the session) (why not: resolve_ref also matches the 'CODE - name' label form by splitting the leading token as a code, so a name-only diff reported a confident miss for references that do resolve. Re-run through the real resolver with a known-present control.)
