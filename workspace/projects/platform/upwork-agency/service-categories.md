# Upwork Agency Service Categories

Upwork's category tree determines which searches surface your agency. You pick ONE primary specialty per member profile, but the agency itself can be tagged across multiple service lines via the agency description and "Specialties" field.

Category matching is the single biggest free distribution lever on Upwork. Wrong choice = invisible.

---

## Recommended Primary Specialty (Agency level)

**AI Services > AI Apps & Integrations**

Reasoning:
- This is where workflow automation buyers search in 2026 (post Make.com / n8n / Zapier convergence into "AI workflow" framing)
- UnpauseAI's positioning is heavily AI-adjacent (Claude API, AI-personalized follow-up, AI reply scoring)
- Higher willingness-to-pay than legacy "Automation" buyers
- Lower competition than "Web Development" generalist categories

---

## Secondary Specialties (member profiles can mirror)

In ranked order of importance for matching the existing service pillars:

1. **Sales & Marketing > Marketing Automation**
   - Covers: Lead and inquiry automation, sales campaign operations
   - Search keywords buyers use: "Make.com expert", "n8n developer", "Smartlead automation"

2. **IT & Networking > Database Administration & Engineering**
   - Covers: CRM-ERP sync, database polling, enrichment pipelines
   - Buyer type: more technical, longer-cycle engagements

3. **Web, Mobile & Software Dev > Scripts & Utilities**
   - Covers: Custom workflow automation, webhook processing, API integration work
   - Wide net for any "I need a script that..." inbound

---

## Member-level Specialty Recommendations

**Nicolas (you) — primary profile:**
- AI Apps & Integrations
- Sub-skills: Workflow Automation, AI Integration, Make.com, n8n, API Integration

**Matthias — primary profile:**
- Choose based on his strongest existing demonstrable work
- If he has more dev/code background: Web, Mobile & Software Dev > Scripts & Utilities
- If he's more delivery/PM: Sales & Marketing > Marketing Automation
- Default suggestion: mirror yours (AI Apps & Integrations) for unified positioning, then differentiate via sub-skills

---

## Categories to AVOID

- **Customer Service** (wrong buyer demographic, low margins)
- **Admin Support > Virtual Assistance** (race-to-bottom pricing)
- **Web Development > Full Stack Development** (oversaturated, agency reads as generalist)

---

## Action

In Upwork agency settings:
1. Set agency specialty to **AI Apps & Integrations**
2. List secondary specialties in the description (you can name them, Upwork doesn't enforce hierarchy in the bio)
3. Configure each member's individual profile specialty per the recommendations above
