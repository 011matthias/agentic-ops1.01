## Voice checklist used for all descriptions

- Zero em-dashes (universal, per rule_deliverables.md)
- No corporate-thesaurus words (robust, leverage, comprehensive, streamline, etc.)
- No "not just X but Y" constructions
- No sentence-opening adverbs
- Verified tools only (matches platform site capability list)
- Keywords aligned with each category's likely buyer searches
- Each description embeds at least one positioning anchor from the overview
