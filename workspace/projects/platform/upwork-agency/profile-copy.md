## Overview (Upwork "Overview" field)

This is the main description block under the profile. Keep it skim-friendly. Buyers decide in the first 10 seconds whether to keep reading.

---

**Production-grade workflow automation for businesses where reliability is non-negotiable.** We're a Karlsruhe-based consultancy serving regulated industries and scaling operations across Europe. EU residency, CET timezone, GDPR by design.

**Where we excel:**

- **Regulated industries.** GDPR-compliant patient journey workflows, consent tracking, multi-step routing, full audit trails for environments that get audited.
- **CRM and ERP integration.** Bidirectional sync, automated invoicing, deal-stage workflows across HubSpot, Fortnox, Upsales, TeamLeader.
- **Lead automation.** Instant form responses, AI-personalized follow-up sequences, multi-factor lead scoring, reply detection that actually stops the sequence.
- **AI-integrated workflows.** Claude API, confidence-threshold routing, sentiment scoring on inbound replies, lead classification, document extraction.
- **Custom automation and migration.** Database pipelines, scheduled reports, webhook routing, Make.com to n8n migrations with zero-downtime cutover.

**What's different about working with us:**

- **Platform-agnostic.** Make.com for visual workflows, n8n for self-hosted data pipelines, Trigger.dev for code-first AI tasks. We pick the right tool for the job, not the one we know best.
- **You own it.** Every system is handed off with documentation, monitoring, and credentials. No retainer trap, no ongoing dependency. If you want to take it in-house or hand it to another team, you can.
- **Production discipline.** We build and test with real data, not synthetic happy paths. Edge cases get found before your customers do.
- **GDPR-aware by design.** Real EU residency, real data-handling discipline. Not compliance theater bolted on at the end.

**How we engage:** detailed scoped proposal up front, fixed-fee delivery. EUR 2,500 to 5,500 typical project scope, 3 to 7 weeks delivery. English and German fluent. CET timezone overlaps with the full EU working day.

If you have a workflow someone on your team is doing manually today that should be running on autopilot instead, that's what we build.
