# Upwork Profile Intro Video Script — Matthias N.

Format: talk-to-camera, ~75 seconds. Positioning: reliable AI automation.
Multilingual edge featured early. One concrete proof example (user-supplied).

Delivery: warm, direct, unhurried. Look at the lens, not the screen. No
"hi my name is" wind-up, the first line is the hook.

---

## SCRIPT

SAY: Most automation breaks the moment real data hits it. I build the kind that doesn't.

SAY: I'm Matthias. I design AI automation for businesses, the systems that handle customer support, outreach, and the repetitive work your team shouldn't be doing by hand. The way I build is the difference: the AI handles the judgment, your real data handles the facts. So the system stays accurate instead of confidently guessing.

SAY: One thing that sets me apart up front. I work natively in English, German, and Portuguese, and conversationally in Spanish. If your customers or your team aren't English-only, that's not a problem to engineer around later, it's just how I already work.

SAY: Two quick examples from current work. For one client I built the outbound side of their sales motion, a cold-outreach email system that targets the right audience and runs the campaigns instead of someone doing it by hand. For another I'm building the other end, leads coming in from several different sources, all flowing into one automated follow-up pipeline so nothing slips through. Different problems, same principle: the system does the repetitive work reliably, the people make the judgment calls.

SAY: Every build I ship comes with documentation and a clean handoff, so you're never dependent on me to keep it running. That's the whole point. You own a system that works, not a black box.

SAY: If you've got a process that's slow, manual, or fragile, send it over. I'll tell you straight whether automation is the right call, and exactly how I'd approach it.

## NOTES

- ~185 words with both client examples, lands around 80 to 95 seconds at a
  relaxed pace. If you want it back near 75, keep only the first example
  (cold-outreach) and drop the second, or cut the support/outreach list to
  just "the repetitive work your team shouldn't be doing by hand."
- Pronounceability: deliberately no tool or platform names spoken (no n8n,
  Make, etc.). They read fine on the profile, they stumble on camera and add
  nothing for a non-technical hirer. Keep them out of the spoken take.
- Anti-AI-slop: no "leverage / robust / streamline / comprehensive", no
  triple-lists, contractions throughout, zero em dashes. Keep it that way if
  you adjust lines.
- The languages line is positioned right after the hook on purpose (you chose
  to lead with it). Do not bury it lower.
