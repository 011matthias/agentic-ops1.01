# Automation Services — Idea Board

Business ideas for automation services: things with real demand and thin
supply, or where automation makes us structurally superior to the existing
suppliers. One file per idea. To add an idea: copy `_template.md`, rename it
`idea-{slug}.md`, fill what you know, leave the rest as open questions.

## The selection rule (why these ideas and not others)

Don't invent demand. Pick pools where:

1. **A legal deadline or a labor shortage manufactures urgency** — nobody
   needs convincing they have a problem; the law or the market does it.
2. **Buyers cluster behind referral hubs** (Steuerberater, web agencies) —
   distribution gets borrowed instead of built over 12-18 months.
3. **Automation changes the cost structure**, not just the pitch — the
   incumbent supply works at day rates; a pipeline works at marginal cost.

An idea that scores on none of these is a commodity service and belongs in
the bin, not on this board.
