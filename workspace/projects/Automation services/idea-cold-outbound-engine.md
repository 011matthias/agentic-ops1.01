# Cold outbound engine (built and run for clients)

Status: idea
Added: 2026-07-28
Demand verified: no

## One-liner

Build and operate the outbound machine for B2B companies whose offer already
converts: list building, enrichment, personalized copy, sending infrastructure,
deliverability, reply handling into their CRM. Sold as setup + monthly operation.

## Demand (why they must buy)

Pipeline hunger is permanent, but nothing manufactures urgency here; against
the board's selection rule this scores weak on driver 1, possible on driver 2
(agencies as resell hub), strong on driver 3: an SDR costs 4-6k EUR/mo
(ASSUMPTION), an operated engine undercuts that at marginal cost.

## Supply / competition (honest)

The most crowded lane in the AI-services space; thousands of interchangeable
"AI outbound" shops. Results depend on the client's offer, which we do not
control, so attribution disputes are structural. The only durable
differentiators are niche focus and deliverability craft, not the concept.

## Automation edge

Reuses sending/deliverability, enrichment, and dedup patterns already built
once. Near-zero R&D; the work is per-client configuration and copy.

## Offer shape

Front door: fixed-price outbound/deliverability audit. Then setup 2-5k EUR
(ASSUMPTION) + 1-2k EUR/mo operation (ASSUMPTION). Take it as project revenue
plus retainer, not as the agency's identity.

## Channel (UWG-clean)

The offer itself is fenced inside DE: cold email to DE prospects violates
UWG §7 for us AND for the engine we would run. So: first clients are
international (marketplaces, e.g. Upwork, where the buyer targets US/UK
prospects), or DE clients selling abroad, or DE-targeting engines built on
LinkedIn/postal instead of email.

## First euro

One marketplace client for a paid outbound audit, converted into an engine
setup. Realistic: 2-4 weeks.

## Risks / open questions

- Saturation; price pressure from commodity competitors.
- Client-offer quality decides results; scope attribution explicitly.
- UWG §7 for any DE-targeted email; spam/reputation risk on infrastructure.
- Churn when campaigns underperform for reasons outside the engine.
