# Speed-to-lead / missed-call recovery

Status: idea
Added: 2026-07-28
Demand verified: no

## One-liner

Every inbound lead (form, phone, WhatsApp) gets an instant reply,
qualification, and a booked slot; missed calls trigger an automatic
text-back. Sold to local service businesses that visibly leak inquiries.

## Demand (why they must buy)

The leak is provable from the buyer's own call log and form timestamps; no
demand has to be invented, only shown. Understaffed front desks (labor
shortage) make it worse. No legal deadline: urgency comes entirely from
holding up their own missed-call count.

## Supply / competition (honest)

In English-speaking markets this is a saturated agency staple. DE supply is
thinner (ASSUMPTION, verify), and the channel differs: SMS culture is weak
here, WhatsApp Business is the reply channel. Honest score against the
board's selection rule: the most commodity-shaped idea on the board. Viable
as a productized volume install or as the down-sell from
`idea-ai-voice-receptionist.md` (same buyers, same stack), not as a moat.

## Automation edge

Templatized install: intake, AI qualifier, calendar booking, CRM log, owner
daily digest. After the first build each install is days, not weeks, which
is what makes the price point work.

## Offer shape

Setup 1.5-3k EUR + 200-500 EUR/mo (ASSUMPTION). Front door: free
"mystery call + response-time audit" of their own line, presented as one
page with their number on it.

## Channel (UWG-clean)

Local network and referral (Handwerk, physio, beauty niches), web agencies
as resell hubs, phone with presumed-consent care under §7(2), postal, walking
in. No cold email.

## First euro

Audit one local business's line this week, present their own missed-call
number, close a fixed install. Realistic: 1-3 weeks; fastest first euro on
this board.

## Risks / open questions

- Small budgets and churn; retention depends on the monthly digest proving
  recovered leads.
- WhatsApp Business API constraints (template approval, session windows).
- DIY substitution (Calendly + Zapier); the counter is the phone half, which
  DIY does not cover.
