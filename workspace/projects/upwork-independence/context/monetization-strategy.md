## (e) Decoupling mechanism and the math

Defense-phase decoupling is a coefficient swap, not scale: move hours from
EUR14/op-hour (Brisken observed; ~EUR8 net of the ~39% social load, below the
EUR13.90 minimum wage) toward capped renegotiated warm at EUR25+/op-hour
(EUR1,000-1,375/mo at 40-55 h/mo, planning-conservative at the EUR25 accept
boundary) and care annuities at EUR100-200/op-hour marginal (EUR200/mo at 1-2
h/client/mo, ASSUMED load, observed attach 0-for-1).

Build-phase decoupling runs through one coefficient: delivered hours per
client per month. Observed un-kitted (Meji, the only measured retainer):
26-35 h/client/mo, which caps the ~78 h/mo delivery budget at 2-3 retainers =
EUR3.7-5.6k/mo. That IS the stall band: the stall case is the no-kit outcome,
not bad luck. The u5 kit targets 6-10 h/client/mo (ASSUMED; a 4-6x
compression never demonstrated anywhere; instrumented from client one via the
value report). At the kit target the same 78 h/mo supports ~8 clients solo
(arithmetically up to 13 at the optimistic 6h end; client counts above 8-10
are u7-gated), at EUR185-308 per operator hour.

Ceilings, stated honestly: the solo-kitted ceiling is ~EUR11-12k/mo as an
EQUILIBRIUM, not a nameplate. Nameplate 8 x 1850 = EUR14.8k gross; the
effective ceiling sits lower because at ~8 clients churn-replacement
acquisition shrinks to 2-4 h/wk and observed admin overhead (~17% of a
measured week) bites; a mixed book (2 x 650 + 6 x 1850 = EUR12.4k) is the
realistic shape. EUR20k/mo is not solo-reachable on any honest reading of the
coefficient; every figure above ~EUR11-12k/mo is strictly gated on the u7
milestone. Operator hours stay flat (~23-28 h/wk total) while income scales
by adding kitted clients at an instrumented, falling coefficient. Growth
bands (new-client income only): base case = stall band EUR3.7-5.6k, which
still covers the EUR2,000 floor - that is the point of floor-first; month-6
upside EUR4-5k; month-18 EUR11-12k solo, above only with u7. Modeled
30-month kEUR appears nowhere as a plan number.

**Floor construction (EUR2,000 gross/mo by Nov 30, owner ruling 1):**
measured on total gross including warm. If both warm streams survive:
Meji EUR1,100 (observed 8h/wk utilization) to 1,375 (full cap, never yet
observed) + Brisken renegotiated 1,000-1,375 = EUR2,100-2,750, clears on warm
alone. Binding case (Meji dies at the gate): Brisken 1,000-1,375 leaves a gap
of EUR625-1,000 = roughly 3-5 care attaches at EUR200/mo; menu-independent
paths only (the 650 tier needs the u5 sign-off and a B2B channel, neither
guaranteed inside the defense window). Double-death case (both streams zero):
the floor is not reachable by Nov 30 from a zero-history channel and the
week-8/week-12 tripwires exist precisely to surface that early rather than
discover it in December. Meji figures are gross of the ~12% Upwork fee (net
~EUR1,211 at full cap).

**What breaks first, in order:** (1) send cadence, already broken at client
zero (30/33 draft-death, one ledger row ever); the machine-audited send
window is the first structural build for exactly this reason. (2) Delivery
hours at ~5 concurrent local clients plus a capped Brisken, around months
6-8 (two builds/mo alone consume half the delivery budget); the exit trigger
into kitted-retainer build mode exists to swap the coefficient before that
wall. (3) The kit-compression checkpoint: above 15 h/client/mo at client 3,
new starts go to a waitlist and the kit gets fixed before further closes.
(4) u7 oversight cost at the solo ceiling (~months 12-18), which is where the
milestone sits.
