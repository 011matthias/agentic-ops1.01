## The pool-15 data point (state it plainly)

The leadgen-portfolio model assumes a referral pool of 15 with ~4 warm
intros/month at conv 0.12. The enumerable reality on 2026-07-22:

- **7 named first-degree people, collapsing to 5 independent sources**
  (Brisken, Jochen, Meji, Wimmer, Coolify; rows 4 and 6 are same-account).
- Of those 5, one (Coolify) is a cold-ish past thread, and one (Jochen)
  is really a partnership/subcontract track, not a referral source.
- 5 second-degree names exist only through one hub (Dirk).

So the honest first-degree referral supply is **3-4 warm sources**, not
15. The model's ~4 intros/month would require every source to produce an
intro monthly, which no relationship of this kind sustains. This confirms
the gtm-v2-confirm flag: the referral channel's modeled supply is the
weakest assumption in the portfolio. Feed-back path per u4 status: once
asks begin and real intro-rate data exists, falsified pool numbers go to
a SCORER_LOCK_ALLOW re-pin PR + portfolio re-run. Until then, referral
stays the 0.154-effort channel, not more.

## Offer definition (recommendation for owner call)

Three candidate shapes were on the table: commission, reciprocity, none.

**Recommendation: no upfront commission for the current network; a
defined thank-you delivered after a conversion; partnership economics
reserved for Jochen and future u7 subcontractors.**

Reasoning, briefly:

- Rows 1-6 are paying clients or delivered engagements. A commission
  offer to a client contact reads transactional in the German Mittelstand
  register and converts a trust ask into a sales program; at 3-4 real
  sources the admin cost of a commission scheme also exceeds its yield.
- A **defined thank-you** (one care-month or an invoice credit on their
  own engagement, stated only AFTER a referral converts) keeps the
  gesture concrete without pricing the relationship upfront.
- **Jochen is a different shape**: his assessment clients are direct
  delivery pipeline. That is subcontract / revenue-share territory and
  should be negotiated as a partnership when u7 opens, not folded into a
  referral thank-you.
- Commission stays in reserve for arm's-length future partners (agencies,
  subcontractors) where a transactional frame is the honest one.

One-page ask asset: decision deferred; it only matters once the drafts
gate opens, and its shape follows the offer decision above.

## Revised recommendation after owner challenge (2026-07-22)

The owner's response to the offer question was to challenge the premise:
"you want to ask current clients for referrals?" That question lands
before the offer question, which this ledger had skipped.

Read the table again with it in mind: four of the five independent sources
are people who currently pay us or recently did. So "run the referral
channel" concretely means going to Dirk mid-delivery, and to Gurmej on a
live retainer, and asking them to spend their own credibility introducing
us to their network. That is a real withdrawal from the working
relationship, priced at zero in the model because the model counts only
our hours.

Revised recommendation, superseding the offer definition above:

- **Do not run referral as a channel now.** No asks, with or without an
  offer attached. The 0.154 modeled effort is better spent on u2 (content)
  and u3 (LinkedIn), both of which reach DACH legally and compound without
  spending relationship capital.
- **Keep it opportunistic.** If a client volunteers an intro, take it and
  thank them concretely. That needs no program and no commission table.
- **Jochen is a different conversation.** His assessments generate delivery
  work that has to go somewhere; that is a partnership or subcontract
  discussion with its own economics, and it belongs with u7.

With the channel not running, the offer question has no live answer: there
is no ask to attach terms to. Revisit if the owner opens the channel, or if
the Jochen partnership becomes concrete.
