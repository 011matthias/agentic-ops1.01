# Contact Enrichment & Verification

Automatically finds and verifies email addresses for your contacts using multiple data sources.

## What This Does

When you add contacts to Airtable without email addresses, this automation finds their professional email by:

1. Checking if the contact already has an email (if yes, just verify it)
2. Trying to find their email using their name and company domain
3. If that doesn't work, trying alternative sources
4. Verifying the email will actually deliver (not a dead address)
5. Updating Airtable with the results

**Runs:** Every 6 hours (4 times daily at 00:24, 06:24, 12:24, 18:24 Stockholm time)

## How It Works

### 1. Find Contacts That Need Emails

The automation looks for contacts in Airtable where "Enrichment Status" is set to "Pending".

### 2. Smart Email Search (3-Step Waterfall)

For each contact, it tries to find their email using the best available method:

**First Try - Domain-Based Search**
- Uses the contact's name and company website
- Example: "John Doe" at "acme.com" → finds john.doe@acme.com
- This works best and finds ~70% of emails

**Second Try - Alternative Search (if needed)**
- Uses the contact's LinkedIn profile and other data
- Helpful when the domain-based search doesn't work
- Finds another ~15% of emails

**Third Try - LinkedIn Enrichment (if needed)**
- Scrapes the contact's LinkedIn profile for email
- Last resort option when other methods fail
- Finds another ~5% of emails

**The automation stops searching as soon as an email is found** - no wasted API credits.

### 3. Verify Email Quality

Once an email is found, it checks:
- Will this email actually deliver?
- Is it a catch-all address (accepts everything)?
- Is it a valid, working inbox?

### 4. Update Airtable

The contact record is updated with:
- **Email** - The found email address
- **Enrichment Vendor** - Which service found it (Leadmagic, Trykitt, etc.)
- **Email Verification Status** - Valid, Catch-All, Invalid, or Unknown
- **Verification Vendor** - Which service verified it (Leadmagic)
- **Enrichment Status** - "Enriched + Verified" or "No Email Found"

## What You'll See

### When Emails Are Found Successfully

In Airtable, the contact record will show:
- **Email field populated** with the found email address
- **Enrichment Status:** "Enriched + Verified"
- **Email Verification Status:** "Valid", "Catch-All", or occasionally "Unknown"
- **Enrichment Vendor:** Shows which service found the email (helpful for cost tracking)

### When No Email Can Be Found

- **Enrichment Status:** "No Email Found"
- Email field remains empty
- The automation tried all available methods but couldn't locate an email

### In the Dashboard

Check `/logs` to see:
- How many contacts were processed in the last run
- How many emails were found vs not found
- Any errors or issues that occurred

## Example

### Before (Manual Process)

1. You add 50 contacts from a LinkedIn list to Airtable
2. Manually visit each person's LinkedIn profile
3. Try to guess their email format
4. Test emails one by one
5. Update Airtable manually
6. **Time:** 10 minutes per contact = 500 minutes (8+ hours!)

### After (Automated)

1. You add 50 contacts to Airtable with "Enrichment Status" = "Pending"
2. Wait for the next automation run (max 6 hours)
3. **Time:** ~2 minutes for the automation to process all 50 contacts
4. Check results in Airtable - typically 30-40 emails found

**You save 8 hours of manual work.**

## Understanding Verification Statuses

When you check a contact's "Email Verification Status" field:

| Status | What It Means | Should You Use It? |
|--------|---------------|--------------------|
| **Valid** | Email is active and will deliver | ✅ Yes - safe to contact |
| **Catch-All** | Domain accepts all emails, uncertain if this specific one is real | ⚠️ Risky - may work, may bounce |
| **Invalid** | Email will bounce or is disposable | ❌ No - don't contact |
| **Unknown** | Unable to verify (rare) | ⚠️ Risky - test carefully |

### Recommended Strategy

- **Valid emails:** Add to email campaigns immediately
- **Catch-All emails:** Add to a separate test campaign or verify manually
- **Invalid/Unknown emails:** Skip or try to find alternative contacts

## Processing Speed

The automation processes up to **20 contacts per run** with concurrent processing:

- **Sequential (old way):** 20 contacts × 5 seconds = 100 seconds
- **Concurrent (current):** 20 contacts ÷ 10 workers = ~20 seconds

If you have more than 20 pending contacts:
- They'll be processed in the next run (6 hours later)
- With 4 runs per day, you can enrich up to 80 contacts daily
- Need faster? The limit can be increased

## Troubleshooting

### "Enrichment Status still shows Pending after 24 hours"

1. **Check if contacts have required data:**
   - Do they have a Company Website or LinkedIn URL?
   - Without either, enrichment is impossible

2. **Check the automation logs:**
   - Go to dashboard → Logs
   - Filter by "a6_3_contact_enrichment"
   - Look for errors or "No Email Found" results

3. **Verify API keys are configured:**
   - Contact your technical team
   - Ensure LEADMAGIC_API_KEY and TRYKITT_API_KEY are set

### "Many contacts showing 'No Email Found'"

This is normal - not every contact has a public email. Typical success rates:

- **With company domain:** 70-80% success
- **With LinkedIn URL only:** 50-60% success
- **With neither:** <10% success

**Improve success rate by:**
- Adding company websites to contacts
- Ensuring LinkedIn URLs are correct and public
- Providing accurate first/last names

### "Email Verification Status shows 'Catch-All' for many emails"

Catch-All addresses are common for:
- Small companies (all emails accepted at domain)
- Some European domains
- Company-wide email policies

**Options:**
1. Send test emails to validate
2. Use these in lower-priority campaigns
3. Try to find alternative contacts at the company

### "Automation running slow"

Expected timing:
- **20 contacts:** ~20-30 seconds
- **Includes:** API calls, verification, Airtable updates

If it's taking longer:
- Check Railway logs for rate limiting warnings
- May need to adjust concurrent processing limit
