DNS housekeeping for September email deliverability
Meji Media, prepared 2026-07-29 by Matthias

One small edit now, one that follows in August. Both live in Porkbun (where
mejievent.com and mejixmas.com DNS is managed), and each is an edit to an
existing record, a few minutes at most.

------------------------------------------------------------------
NOW: mejixmas.com
   Edit the existing TXT record on host:  _dmarc
   Set its value to exactly:

   v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=25; rua=mailto:gurmej@mejixmas.com; adkim=r; aspf=r

   Only the report address changes. It currently points at a mejimedia.com
   address, and mail providers refuse to deliver reports across domains
   without an extra authorisation record, so today those reports simply
   never arrive. Pointing it at gurmej@mejixmas.com makes them arrive.
