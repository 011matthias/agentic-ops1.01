### The fresh list

The target audience is the July brief, unchanged: UK-wide, 50 to 2,000 staff,
17 event-buying sectors; decision-maker titles (HR Director, Head of People,
Head of Events and similar) go to Version A, organiser titles (Office Manager,
EA, PA to the Board, HR Manager and similar) to Version B. Roughly 41,000
people match; about 17,000 survive the filters. The list is pulled at launch so
the data is freshest, which is also when your Apollo credits get spent. New
contacts only: every batch passes five checks before loading (off-target names
out, your past customers out, anyone ever emailed in any campaign out, risky
mail gateways out, then address verification).

### What September can carry

The push sends from three mejievent mailboxes at 30 emails a day each, 90 in
total. At three emails per person that supports about 30 new people a sending
day: roughly 650 across a full month of 22 sending days, and nearer 450 to 500
with a launch in the second week of September, against the 1,500 to 2,000 a
month the plan was built around. The bottleneck is the mailboxes, not the list.

A second sending domain with three more mailboxes doubles that. New domains
need three to four weeks of warm-up, so one started this week comes into
service late September to the first week of October, depending on how the
warm-up goes; its mailboxes fold into A and B once warm.

### Order of work at the go

Authentication first step (mejievent.com is still on monitor-only; it steps to
full strength over the month as the reports stay clean), update the two
campaigns and build the third with stop-on-reply and bounce protection on,
