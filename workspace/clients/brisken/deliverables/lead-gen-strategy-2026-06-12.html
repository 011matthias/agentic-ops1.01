How the campaign works

Volume in, precision out. Many SAP companies narrow to a few hot ones, who get surrounded with trust and one personal message, then a demo, then your close.

Why precision, not volume

  • The high-volume route was already tried here: ~150 mailboxes, roughly 2M cold emails, zero qualified leads.
  • A system that touches the SAP money core is too high-risk to earn a meeting from a stranger in an inbox.
  • These buyers move on trust, timing, and seeing peers already use it.
  • So the metric is warm, well-timed meetings, not emails sent. The outreach this ends in is personal, not a blast.

Why now

  • SAP ends ECC mainstream maintenance in 2027 (2030 extended); 59% of SAP shops are already on S/4HANA, up 13 points in a year (Precisely, 2025).
  • Migration is when the market-data plumbing into SAP gets rebuilt or replaced. That is the buying window.
  • OnePilot is the buy-instead-of-rebuild answer: governed, provider-agnostic, no custom code.
  • Brisken's SAP co-innovation partnership makes it the credible one.

Three products lead the motion

Lead with the three whose buyer and pain are sharpest; carry the rest as proof of range.

Market Data Hub (flagship)

  • Who: SAP treasury teams, multi-currency, hedge accounting.
  • Signal: pipe Bloomberg / Refinitiv / 360T rates into TRM by hand or custom code.
  • Pain: bad or late data is costly; the migration forces a rebuild-or-buy now.

Trade Automation

  • Who: in-house FX / derivatives / commodity desk on SAP.
  • Signal: 360T, FXall or Bloomberg FX GO; active hedging; front-office hiring.
  • Pain: trades re-keyed from the platform into SAP TRM by hand.

OnePilot platform (the range)

  • The no-code orchestration layer on SAP BTP; the other apps (Cash Flow & Exposure, Bank Fee, Credit, Remittance, ESG) ride as proof one layer covers the whole estate, not eight campaigns.
  • Best forwardable: the Calvin AI co-worker clip (reads an email funding request, creates the SAP bank transfer end to end). Brisken already runs Remittance live on ChatGPT.
Beyond the suite: OnePilot for FSIBrisken's 2026 banking play targets banks, a different buyer. Its own track and research, not part of this plan.
