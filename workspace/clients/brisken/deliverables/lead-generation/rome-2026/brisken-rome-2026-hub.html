What we showed you

Four applications on your SAP data, one AI layer across them.

Brisken automates cash, trading, market data, and reconciliation directly on SAP, SAP and non-SAP both ways, no code. Less manual handling, more straight-through processing.

TreasuryCentral

The AI treasury cockpit

Your treasury operations on SAP data in one place: see the position, act on it, with every move governed and logged.

Into SAP ECC / S/4HANA
Market Data Hub

One point of control for market data

Ingest rates, curves, and prices from Bloomberg, Refinitiv, 360T, OANDA, and central banks; govern, transform, and distribute into SAP and non-SAP. No more hand-keyed uploads.

Single source of truth, no code
Smart Trading

The trade lifecycle, end to end

From decision and approval through execution to deal creation in SAP. Trading-venue and TMS agnostic: FXall, Bloomberg FX GO, 360T, BidFX into SAP TRM and FAM.

Front office to SAP, automatically
Remittance Advice Gate

Remittances read by AI, straight into SAP

An LLM reads unstructured remittance emails and attachments, structures them, and posts to SAP. Nobody retypes anything.

Into SAP S/4HANA
OnePilot

The AI layer, already in production.

OnePilot is the autonomous layer across all four applications: it asks, automates, and acts on your treasury operations, inside your controls, with a full audit trail and four-eye approval. It is running with customers today.

  • Bi-directional connectivity, SAP and non-SAP
  • Validation, anomaly detection, audit trail built in
  • Segregation of duties and four-eye approval
  • Manage-by-exception, the team stays in command
