## The problem we remove

Most SAP finance teams run several brittle point-to-point scripts to move market data, trades, bank files and remittances in and out of SAP. They break when a provider changes a field or the person who built them leaves: no audit trail, no owner, no monitoring.

## TreasuryCentral: the universal interface

TreasuryCentral is the product your team works in: cash, investments, debt, FX, market news and governance on one screen, on your SAP data. One cockpit over everything below it. It does not replace your stack; it sits on top of it and runs it, governed end to end.

**The stack, top to bottom:**

## The applications, in one line each

- **Market Data Hub:** market data into SAP, governed, from Bloomberg, Refinitiv, CME Group, 360T, Deutsche Börse and OANDA.
- **Brisken Smart Trading (BST):** trade automation on your SAP data.
- **Remittance Advice Gate:** AI reads the messy remittance emails and posts them into SAP S/4HANA, so the team stops retyping.
