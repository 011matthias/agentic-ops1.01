--- ABOUT / OVERVIEW ---  (Edit page > Description, 2,000 char max)

Brisken builds AI-powered treasury applications that run on your SAP data, live with customers today, not on a roadmap.

Most SAP finance teams move market data, trades, bank files and remittances in and out of SAP with brittle point-to-point scripts. They break when a provider changes a field or the person who built them leaves, with no audit trail and no owner. Brisken removes that.

TreasuryCentral is the single screen treasury works in: cash, investments, debt, FX, market data and governance on one cockpit, on your SAP data. Underneath it, OnePilot is the AI layer that operates the applications and keeps every step governed:

- Market Data Hub: market data into SAP from Bloomberg, Refinitiv, CME Group, 360T, Deutsche Boerse and OANDA.
- Brisken Smart Trading (BST): trade automation on your SAP data.
- Remittance Advice Gate: AI reads remittance emails and posts them into SAP S/4HANA, so the team stops retyping.

It does not replace your stack; it sits on top of it and runs it. You set the rules and approve the exceptions; OnePilot does the work humans shouldn't have to.
