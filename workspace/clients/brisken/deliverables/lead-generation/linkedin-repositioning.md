## 3. What we kept out, and why

- **Verve / the Universal-UI vision, by name.** It is the wow-reveal you close
  conversations with in the room, private beta. Naming it on a public profile
  spends the reveal early and pulls the headline off the treasury outcome. The
  post-event outreach leads with the treasurer's result; the vision is the
  enabler underneath, not the hook. Kept off both surfaces by design.
- **"Universal UI / whole working day / any role" as a headline.** Same reason
  the website keeps it off the homepage hero: it reads as a different company to
  a treasury buyer who came for "Bloomberg into SAP," and it dilutes the proof.
- **Anti-SAP framing** ("one vendor's frozen best practice"). The whole motion
  leans on SAP goodwill, the Store listing, the Co-Innovation trust mark. Keep
  that line for the room with a non-SAP-aligned buyer, never in public copy.

Optional, your call: a single forward-looking line at the end of your About
("Where this goes: one governed AI layer across every screen a finance team
works in") gestures at the trajectory without naming Verve. Default is to leave
it off and keep the profile on the outcome; say the word and I add it.
