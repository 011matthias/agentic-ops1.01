# Market Data Hub demo: screen-recording shot list

**For:** Dirk
**From:** UnpauseAI
**Date:** 2026-07-09
**Why this exists:** a 58-second Market Data Hub film already exists and is ready to
publish. It is built from the still screens in your own walkthrough deck
(`BRISKEN MDH WALKTHROUGH DEMO SLIDES 250710.pptx`). Stills carry the product
honestly, but they do not move. Twenty minutes of your screen turns the same film
into a live one, and nothing else in it has to change.

You do not need to narrate, script, or edit. Record the six moments below, in this
order, and send the raw file. The titles, captions, pacing, and brand treatment are
already built and will be re-applied to your footage.

## Before you start

- Use the demo tenant the walkthrough deck was captured from. Do not use a tenant
  with a real customer's data in it.
- Browser at **1920x1080**, zoom **100%**, full screen, no bookmarks bar, no
  notifications, no other tabs visible.
- Close anything that shows a personal name or address. The current film had to drop
  four screens because they exposed a customer name (BASF), a colleague's email
  address, and a named user in a log column. Anything on screen ends up on an SAP page.
- Record with whatever you have. Windows **Win+Alt+R**, Loom, or Teams all work.
  Screen only, no webcam, no microphone.

## The six shots

Move slowly. Land on a screen, let it sit still for three seconds, then do the one
action. A shot that feels too slow while you record reads as calm on playback.

| # | Screen | The one action | Hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The provider's own price page (ICE Sugar No. 11 Futures, as in the deck) | Scroll the contract table down one screen, slowly | 8s |
| 2 | Routine schedule detail | Open the recurrence dropdown, pick an hour, close it | 8s |
| 3 | Market data, Agri Prices list | Sort by date, then let the rows settle | 8s |
| 4 | Anomaly detection method | Scroll the rule form so the description and the function are both seen | 8s |
| 5 | A rate, change request modal | Type one sentence into the reason box, hover Save. **Do not submit** | 8s |
| 6 | Target audit log | Scroll two rows, then stop | 8s |

Roughly a minute of usable footage. Record each shot two or three times back to back if
a click goes wrong; we cut the bad takes. Keep it all in one file, do not trim.

## What we do with it

Replace the six stills with your six clips, keep the same captions and the same order,
re-render, and hand back the same three deliverables: a 16:9 master for the SAP
PartnerFinder media slot and the Discovery Center mission, and a square cut for
LinkedIn.

## Where it goes

Both SAP surfaces take a hosted video URL rather than a file, so the last step is a
YouTube or Vimeo upload on a Brisken account. Unlisted is enough; unlisted videos play
fine when embedded and do not surface in search. Once the URL exists:

- **PartnerFinder** (editor `0001663611`): At-a-Glance media slot, in place of the
  image. One slot, so it is either the hero image or the video, not both.
- **Discovery Center** mission `3904`: the demo field, which is empty today and is one
  of the gaps the 2026-06-17 audit flagged.
