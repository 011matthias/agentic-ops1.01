# OnePilot positioning: one clear story, told in the right order

**To:** Dirk Neumann, Brisken
**From:** UnpauseAI
**Date:** 2026-06-20

You said it best: "we keep changing what OnePilot is." Here is why that happens, and how we fix it.

OnePilot is two true things at once. It is a broad platform (one place to run your whole work day, across many systems), and it is a sharp, specific tool (it gets SAP financial data in order). Both are real. The problem starts when we try to say both at the same time, in the same place, to the same person. Someone who first hears "we get Bloomberg data into your SAP," then reads "OnePilot runs your entire working day," thinks they are looking at two different companies.

The fix is simple: tell one part of the story at a time, matched to how well the person already knows us. It is the same OnePilot throughout. We just lead with the part that fits the moment.

## What OnePilot is (this part never changes)

- OnePilot is the platform: one place where you build the workspace your job needs, and AI does the busywork across everything in it.
- It is **not an SAP-only product.** It connects to whatever you work in: SAP and other systems, banks and trading platforms, market data, email, Teams, Office.
- The named apps (Market Data Hub, Trade, Bank Fee, Remittance) are products that run on OnePilot. TreasuryCentral is simply OnePilot set up for a treasurer.
- We start with SAP treasury because that is where Brisken is strongest and easiest to sell. It is our first market, not the edge of what OnePilot can do.

## Tell the story in three steps

Match the message to how well the buyer already knows you:

| Who you are talking to | What you lead with | Where you say it |
|---|---|---|
| **A stranger** (first contact, does not know us yet) | The specific, believable hook: you run SAP, you use a data vendor like Bloomberg, so someone is moving that data by hand. We fix exactly that. | Website home page, product pages, the first LinkedIn message |
| **A customer** (already bought one thing) | The wider offer: TreasuryCentral for the whole treasury day, and OnePilot as one controlled layer over the tools they already run. | Follow-up meetings, account conversations, the TreasuryCentral page |
| **A big-picture buyer** (weighing where this goes) | The full vision: OnePilot as the one work surface, for any role, across any system, well beyond SAP. | A dedicated "vision" page, one click from the home page |

The one rule that holds it together: the broad "whole working day, any role" language stays on the vision page and in meetings. It does not go on the home page or in the first message. The next section is why that matters more than it sounds.

## The one change we would make to the website

The temptation is to put the big platform vision front and center on the home page. We would do the opposite, for one practical reason.

- The home page leads, top to bottom, with the SAP story: the specific problem, who it is for, and why now (the SAP system upgrade deadline).
- The full OnePilot vision gets its own page, clearly linked, one click away.
- **Why it matters:** the home page is also the page that AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI answers) read when someone asks them something like "how do I get Bloomberg data into SAP." If that page is full of broad "does everything" language, those tools cannot tell what we are for, and they stop recommending us for the SAP questions we can actually win. Leading with SAP here is about getting found, not about limiting OnePilot to SAP.

## Where the big vision wins you the deal

The vision is not just "nice to show later." It wins the second meeting.

- A serious SAP buyer will ask: "why you, and not a small tool, a consultancy, or SAP's own option?"
- The answer is the platform: buy one controlled layer now, at the SAP upgrade moment, instead of stitching together eight separate projects later. The part nobody can easily copy is how deeply Brisken connects into SAP.
- Two of your newer lines fit perfectly here (in meetings, never in cold outreach):
  - **"Orchestrate, don't replace":** SAP stays the system of record; OnePilot sits on top and runs the work across it.
  - **"Your best practice, not the vendor's":** processes you set up in plain language to fit your company, instead of a fixed template.
- Your strongest point of all: companies are buying AI tools and then not using them. Industry figures from your vision: Gartner expects $2.5 trillion of AI spending in 2026, yet 30%+ of AI projects are dropped after the trial stage and 40%+ of "agent" projects are cancelled by 2027. People adopt what makes their day easier. That argument belongs in the room and in the pricing, not in a first cold message where it is too broad to mean anything. (We will confirm each figure's exact source before it goes into any public material.)

## Three decisions we need from you

The structure is settled: OnePilot is the platform, TreasuryCentral is its treasury setup, the apps run on it. These three are yours:

1. **What do we call the treasury page?** "TreasuryCentral," or "OnePilot for Treasury"? Just the name.
2. **How prominent should the vision page be right now,** while the first product is still proving itself with new buyers? It gets built either way; this is about how forward we push it.
3. **Should banking (the financial-services track) be the lead example of "where this goes"?** Our suggestion: yes. It is the biggest, clearest expansion and the best proof OnePilot works beyond SAP finance. We would keep it out of the cold outreach either way.

## One careful note, about your own line

- "Your best practice, not the vendor's" can read as a jab at SAP (their "best practice" being one vendor's fixed template).
- Our whole plan leans on SAP goodwill: the SAP Store listing, the Co-Innovation Partner badge, the co-selling.
- Suggestion: keep the idea (run it the way that is best for you), but drop the anti-SAP edge anywhere public, or anywhere an SAP person might read it. Save it for a private conversation with a buyer who is not tied to SAP.
- It is your line and your SAP relationship, so we wanted to flag it directly.
