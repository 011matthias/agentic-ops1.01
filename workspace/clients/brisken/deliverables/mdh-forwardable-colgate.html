The step that is probably manual

You run SAP treasury and Bloomberg, with a multi-currency book across many entities. Getting Bloomberg rates and curves into SAP Cash Management and TRM is usually one of two things: someone keys them in by hand, or a custom script does it and breaks when Bloomberg changes a field. Either way it is a control risk, and your live S/4HANA TRM build is exactly when that interface gets specified or re-plumbed.

What Market Data Hub does

Market Data Hub governs that feed end to end, no code: ingest from Bloomberg, validate and normalize, distribute into SAP TRM and Cash Management, with a full audit trail and manage-by-exception alerts. One governed point of control instead of a per-security interface or a script to babysit. It is listed on the SAP Store and built by an SAP Co-Innovation Partner.
