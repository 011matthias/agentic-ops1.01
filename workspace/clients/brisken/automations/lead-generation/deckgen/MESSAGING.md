# TreasuryCentral deck messaging map

Source: Dirk's 8 comments on `Brisken - TreasuryCentral - Sanofi 2026.pptx`
slide 2 (2026-07-19), plus the OnePilot→TreasuryCentral rename in his own
`TreasuryCentral - Solutions Overview 2026.pptx` (2026-07-17). Applies to
every product and prospect deck, both pipelines.

## Naming (locked 2026-07-21)

Product = **TreasuryCentral**. Platform underneath = **OnePilot**. Lockup:
"TreasuryCentral, powered by OnePilot." OnePilot is the enabler layer, never
the lead.

## Terminology swaps

| Don't say | Say instead | Why (Dirk) |
|---|---|---|
| cockpit, dashboard, command center | workspace / spaces | cockpit instills old tech; workspaces are where agents meet their human colleagues |
| your live SAP data, live SAP data | across your SAP systems and data; in your SAP context; grounded in your SAP | "SAP data is always live" — the phrase is illogical |
| trading (the capability) | autonomous trading | "just trading is too little": trade automation, platform integration, governance, STP |
| the manual middle (alone) | legacy interfaces + brittle integrations + the manual middle | buyers with an interface otherwise think it doesn't apply to them |
| one clean, governed set of data (reads as a central repository) | systems connected, data flowing where it's needed; your book of records stays where it is | we don't hold the data; we connect the dots and make systems understand each other |

Keep and lead with: governed, governance, audit, compliance.

## The spine (one message)

We run on top of your SAP systems and data. Whatever you need to do
elsewhere, we connect, integrate and automate it. The result is an autonomous
treasury across any platform and app, grounded in your SAP context: the
process runs, effort drops, quality and speed improve, and your SAP record is
updated.

## The positioning edge (the buyer's own words)

A solution that works for more than just SAP, and actually works: live
customers, real features, not just slides. We give the treasurer the tool to
work the way they want, on the platform they choose, and make all their
systems talk to each other, building the autonomous treasury one use case at a
time. Not a German software house setting global standards for how a treasurer
must operate.
