# Video Walkthrough Guide; Insurance SaaS Startup (p027)

Speak from these points in your own words. Screen + camera bubble. Target ~2 minutes. Open on the work, not on an intro. Close on the question, never "hire me".

## 0:00 Open: two asks, one job

- Name it back: you want insurance agents found at volume, and cold email that reaches the inbox instead of spam.
- Reframe: those feel like two problems but they are one motion, finding reachable agents and sending in a way the inbox trusts. Both are solved, neither is the part you should be doing yourself.
- Reassure: this is a known, repeatable build. I run it for a live client right now.

## 0:20 Where insurance agents actually live as data

- The good news first: insurance agents are one of the easiest B2B audiences to source, because licensed agents are public record.
- Point at the sources on screen: state licensing registries, Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, agency directories.
- Land the edge: most people bidding on this will scrape one list and blast it. The list quality is what decides whether you ever reach an inbox, so every email gets verified before it is ever used.

## 0:50 Why cold email lands in spam, and how we stop it

- This is the part the job post is really worried about. Be concrete.
- Walk the cause: cold email from your real domain, on brand-new mailboxes, with no authentication set up, is the fastest way into the spam folder.
- Name the fixes one at a time and gloss each abbreviation as you say it: separate sending domains so your main domain is never at risk; SPF, DKIM and DMARC, the three DNS records that prove a domain is allowed to send mail; two to three weeks of warm-up before any cold email goes out; low volume per inbox, spread across many inboxes.
- Tie it back: do this and you are not gambling on spam, you are sending the way the inbox already trusts.

## 1:25 What running it for you looks like

- Make "handle it for me" literal: you do not touch a tool.
- The monthly loop on screen: we source and verify, we send and watch replies, interested agents get booked straight onto your calendar, and every week I report what is working and tune it.
- The only thing on your side is showing up to the demo and signing the client.

## 1:50 Close: the one number that scopes it

- Compress the offer to one line: lean setup, then a monthly fee to run the whole motion, well under the cost of an SDR.
- End on an easy question, not a pitch: roughly how many demos a month would make this worth it for you? That tells me how aggressive to make the sourcing and the send volume.

## Terms to gloss if you say them on camera

- SPF, DKIM, DMARC: the three DNS records that prove a domain is allowed to send mail
- DNS: the address book of the internet, where a domain's settings live
- DOI: state Department of Insurance, the public registry of licensed agents
- ESP: email sending platform, the tool that actually sends the campaigns
- ICP: ideal customer profile, the exact kind of agent worth targeting
- SDR: a sales development rep, the full-time hire this replaces
- warm-up: slowly ramping a new inbox so providers learn to trust it
- bounce rate: the share of emails that fail to deliver, kept under 2 percent
