BEAT 2 -- WALKTHROUGH [0:25-2:40]

== OVERVIEW [0:25-0:55] ==

>> Scroll to "How It Works" section

SAY: This is the overview. Four core components, two phases, two to three weeks.

SAY: The three zones show the pipeline: Research on the left -- Claude scrapes and analyzes the prospect's website. Personalize in the middle -- Claude drafts a message from the research, not a template. Deliver on the right -- email, LinkedIn, and tracking.

>> Scroll to "System Components" section

SAY: The key design decision is the approval gate. No message goes out without your review. The system drafts and queues. You decide what ships.


== SOLUTION [0:55-1:30] ==

>> Click "Solution" in top nav

SAY: The solution page breaks down each component.

>> Scroll to "Research Engine"

SAY: This is the core. Claude does not just pull keywords from a website. It reads the actual page content and reasons about the business. A web dev shop advertising WordPress sites gets a different message than one doing headless CMS architecture.

>> Scroll to "Message Generator"

SAY: The message generator takes the research output and drafts an email that references specific details from their site. Tone adjusts based on context -- enterprise versus startup, technical versus non-technical.

>> Scroll to "Delivery + Dashboard"

SAY: Then delivery. Email via Instantly or your SMTP. LinkedIn queued as tasks. Every touchpoint logged in a tracking sheet.


== WORKFLOW + LIVE DEMO [1:30-2:10] ==

>> Click "Workflow" in top nav

SAY: This is the workflow page. The architecture diagram shows the full data flow.

>> Scroll to "Live Demo" section

SAY: I am going to paste in a URL and let Claude research the prospect in real time.

>> Click URL input, type a sample URL
>> Click "Research + Draft" button, wait for result

SAY: And there it is -- Claude analyzed the website, identified the industry and services, scored the relevance, and drafted a personalized message. This is the actual flow that runs for every lead in your pipeline.

>> Scroll to "Technical Stack" section

SAY: The system runs on n8n, which is open source and self-hosted. No per-execution costs. At your outreach volume, that saves fifty to a hundred dollars a month compared to Zapier or Make.
