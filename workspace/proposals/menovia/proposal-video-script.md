## [0:20-0:50] The Challenge

**Screen:** Scroll down to the patient journey section on the overview page

> From your brief, the core challenge is clear: every new patient means manual data entry, manual emails, manual reminders, and manual consent tracking. That is 10 minutes of admin per patient, spread across multiple tools, with no audit trail for GDPR. When you scale to dozens of patients a week, that breaks.

> Our proposal solves this by connecting all seven tools into one automated flow.

## [0:50-1:30] The Solution

**Screen:** Navigate to the Solution page

> Here is the full solution. We have broken it into six areas.

> **[Scroll through each section]** Intake automation handles patient forms and validation. Booking connects to Cal.com for self-service scheduling. Communication covers confirmation emails, reminders, and follow-ups, all in Dutch. CRM integration keeps Zoho updated automatically. Intercom handles live chat. And the GDPR layer logs consent before anything else happens.

> Every one of these runs automatically after the initial setup.

## [1:30-2:00] GDPR Compliance

**Screen:** Navigate to the GDPR page

> This is what sets this proposal apart. We know GDPR is not optional for a Dutch healthcare provider.

> **[Scroll to the compliance matrix]** Every data touchpoint is mapped: what data flows where, how consent is logged, which servers are EU-hosted, and how long data is retained. Consent is always logged before any data is stored or sent. That is not a checkbox; it is baked into the workflow architecture.
