# Video Script -- MailerLite Zoom Signup Landing Page

## Target Duration: 2-3 minutes

---

### BEAT 1 -- Reframe (~20 seconds)

>> Their job posting

SAY: Hi there, Nico here. I saw your posting for help with MailerLite landing pages -- specifically domain authentication, a reusable template for your Zoom sessions, Stripe payments, GDPR consent, and an automated email that sends the Zoom link after signup.

>> Highlight "landing page" and "Zoom"

SAY: Good news -- everything you need here is already built into MailerLite. No third-party connectors, no custom code. I put together a proposal site to walk you through how each piece works.

---

### (continued -- Authority)

>> My Upwork profile

SAY: Quick background -- I am Nico, based in Germany. I build email automation workflows daily, and I have done GDPR compliance setups, Stripe payment integrations, and domain authentication configurations specifically in MailerLite. I am also in the CET timezone, so there is solid overlap with US business hours.

---

### BEAT 2 -- Walkthrough (~90 seconds)

>> Nav: Solution

SAY: Let me start with the solution page. There are five components here, one for each part of your brief.

>> Scroll to Domain Authentication

SAY: First is domain authentication. That is three DNS records -- DKIM, SPF, and DMARC. MailerLite generates the values, I add them to your domain registrar, and then we verify. The whole process takes under 30 minutes.

>> Scroll to Landing Page Template

SAY: Next is the landing page template. This uses MailerLite's drag-and-drop builder. Hero section, session details, signup form with the Stripe payment block -- all on one page. And it gets saved as a reusable template, so for your next Zoom session you just duplicate it and swap the details. Five minutes.

>> Scroll to Stripe Payment Integration

SAY: Stripe connects to MailerLite natively through a one-click OAuth setup. No Zapier, no middleware. You create a product in Stripe, drop the payment block onto the page, and it is done.

>> Scroll to GDPR Consent Setup

SAY: For GDPR, the signup form gets a required checkbox that cannot be pre-checked. The terms are displayed in a popup so the user stays on the page. And MailerLite automatically logs the IP address, timestamp, and consent text for compliance.

>> Scroll to Email Automation

SAY: After someone signs up and pays, a MailerLite automation fires immediately. It sends a branded email with your Zoom link, the session details, and a calendar add link.

---

SAY: Day four is the email automation, end-to-end testing, and a Loom walkthrough showing you how to duplicate the template yourself.

SAY: If this looks like a good fit, the onboarding page collects everything I need to get started -- your domain registrar, MailerLite plan, Stripe account, and session details. Five minutes to fill out, and I will start day one within 24 hours. Looking forward to hearing from you.
