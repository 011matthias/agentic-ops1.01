# Video Script: Shakil - Automation & Workflow Setup

---

### Pre-Roll 1: Open on Job Posting

>> Screen: Upwork job posting "Automation & Workflow Setup Specialist (n8n / Make.com / ClickUp)"

SAY: "Hi there, Nico here. I saw your posting for an automation specialist to help with n8n, Make.com, and ClickUp workflows."

SAY: "But I also noticed something interesting. You have 4 other open jobs right now, including 3 separate SEO specialists across different sites."

---

### Pre-Roll 2: Value Hook

>> Switch to proposal site, scroll briefly to show the SEO monitoring section on the overview page

SAY: "So I built you a free n8n workflow that pulls ranking data from all 3 of your sites into one dashboard. You can download it from the proposal page. Let me walk you through what I put together."

---

### Beat 1: Reframe

>> Sidebar: The Real Problem

SAY: "Shakil, looking at your posting, you mention wanting to reduce manual work and make day-to-day operations smoother. But I think the bigger picture is this: you're running multiple businesses from a small team, each with its own tools and its own manual processes."

SAY: "The automation challenge isn't just connecting ClickUp to n8n. It's building a system that scales as you add more people, more tools, and more businesses without everything falling apart."

---

### Authority

>> Briefly show Upwork profile

SAY: "Quick background on me. I build automation systems with n8n and Make.com full-time. I manage over 50 Make.com scenarios across client accounts and build production n8n workflows with proper error handling and monitoring."

---

### Beat 2: Structure

>> Sidebar: ClickUp Automation

SAY: "Here's how I'd structure this. Phase 1 is your quick wins: I audit what you currently have running, fix the reliability issues, and set up ClickUp integrations so tasks flow automatically instead of being created by hand."

>> Sidebar: API Integrations

SAY: "The second piece is connecting your tools properly. Webhooks with validation, retry logic when APIs fail, and data checks before anything gets written. No more silent failures where data just disappears."

>> Sidebar: AI Actions

SAY: "Then for the AI side, I'd connect OpenAI to your n8n workflows for things like automatically classifying incoming ClickUp tasks by priority, or summarizing long threads into a quick brief."

---

### Beat 3: SEO Monitoring (Cross-sell)

>> Sidebar: How It Works (workflow page)

SAY: "Now here's the bonus. You're paying 3 separate SEO freelancers to work across sipraw.com, trendRox.com, and your UK real estate site. They're probably sending you reports in 3 different formats."

>> Scroll through the architecture diagram

SAY: "This n8n workflow I built runs daily. It pulls ranking data from Google Search Console for all 3 sites, processes it, updates a Google Sheets dashboard, and creates a ClickUp task if any keyword drops more than 5 positions."

>> Sidebar: Download

SAY: "Your SEO specialists still do the actual work. But the monitoring and reporting runs automatically. One dashboard, all sites. And you can download the workflow right here."

---

### Beat 4: Investment

>> Sidebar: Overview (investment page)

SAY: "On pricing, I've broken this into 3 independent phases. Phase 1 is $120, covers the audit and ClickUp automation. Phase 2 adds the SEO monitoring dashboard. Phase 3 adds the AI layer."

SAY: "Each phase delivers working automations on its own. You can stop after any phase if the value isn't there. No lock-in."

---

### Beat 5: Close

>> Sidebar: Get Started (onboarding page)

SAY: "Shakil, if any of this looks useful, there's an onboarding form here where you can share some details about your current setup. That way I can scope Phase 1 accurately."

SAY: "Or just reply on Upwork and we can set up a quick 15-minute call. Either way, looking forward to it."
