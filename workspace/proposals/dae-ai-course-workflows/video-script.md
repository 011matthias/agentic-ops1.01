>> Sidebar: Content System

SAY: Take the content creation module. Input is a Google Sheet with topics and audience info. Process is ChatGPT drafting with a structured prompt that matches the brand voice. Output is a formatted draft saved to Google Drive. The whole thing runs in Make.com without touching code.

SAY: Same pattern for client communication. Inquiry comes in, AI drafts a response based on the message type, it goes through an approval step, then sends. Three stages, one system.

SAY: And for business automation: lead comes in from a form, gets scored and routed to the CRM, triggers a welcome sequence. Again, input, process, output.
