# Video Script: n8n Lead Generation & CRM System for Fitness Coach

## Target Duration: 4-5 minutes

---

### PRE-ROLL 1: Open on Source (~10s)

>> Screen: Upwork job posting page
SAY: Hi there, Nico here. I saw your posting about building an n8n automation system for lead generation and CRM for your fitness coaching business.

---

### PRE-ROLL 2: Value Hook (~15s)

SAY: What caught my attention is that you want a supervisor layer where nothing goes out without your approval. That's the right instinct, and it's also the part most people skip when building these systems.
SAY: I put together a full proposal page for this, and I'll also build your lead capture pipeline for free so you can see it working before committing to anything. Let me walk you through it.

>> Nav: Overview

---

### BEAT 1: Reframe (~30s)

>> Scroll to The Real Challenge
SAY: So your core problem isn't just capturing leads from Instagram and Meta ads. It's making sure every lead gets a quality first touch, gets followed up consistently, and moves through a clear process toward booking a call.
SAY: The way I'd approach this is as a phased system. Four phases, each one independently useful. And Phase 0 is free.

>> Scroll to Your System at a Glance
SAY: Here's the overview. Phase 0 handles lead capture and your CRM pipeline. Phase 1 adds the outreach drafting with your supervisor approval. Phase 2 is follow-up tracking and daily reporting. Phase 3 connects your Meta Ads data.

---

### (continued: Authority ~15s)

SAY: Quick context on why I'm a fit for this specifically.
SAY: I'm based in Germany, so there's a 4-hour overlap with Dubai. n8n is my primary platform, and I've built similar lead gen pipelines with CRM tracking and dedup logic before.

---

### BEAT 2: Solution Walkthrough (~90s)

>> Nav: Solution
>> Scroll to Phase 0
SAY: Let me show you how each phase works. Phase 0 is the free one. When someone interacts with your Instagram or fills out a Meta ad form, a webhook fires to n8n. It captures the lead, checks for duplicates, and logs it into a Google Sheets CRM with five pipeline stages.

>> Scroll to Phase 1
SAY: Phase 1 is the supervisor layer you described. When a new lead arrives, n8n drafts a personalized first message. You get a WhatsApp notification with the draft, and you can approve it, edit it, or reject it. Nothing goes out without your say.
SAY: Over time, once you trust the quality, we can reduce approvals to exceptions only.

>> Scroll to Phase 2
SAY: Phase 2 adds follow-up tracking. The system flags leads that go quiet, sends you reminders, and delivers a daily summary via WhatsApp with your pipeline numbers.

>> Scroll to Phase 3
SAY: And Phase 3 pulls your Meta Ads data. Spend, cost per lead, and performance by ad set, all logged into a dashboard tab in your Google Sheet.

---

### (Workflow page ~30s)

>> Nav: Workflow
>> Scroll to The Pipeline Flow
SAY: Here's the actual pipeline flow for the lead capture. Six steps from the initial trigger all the way through to the outreach queue. Each step is handled by a separate node in n8n, so if something fails, you know exactly where.

>> Scroll to Download the Template
SAY: There's a downloadable n8n workflow template here. You can import it into your n8n instance to see the structure before we even start.

---

### BEAT 3: Investment (~40s)

>> Nav: Investment
>> Scroll to Phase Pricing
SAY: On pricing. Phase 0 is free. That's your lead capture and CRM pipeline delivered at no cost, worth about $260 of build time. From there, the three paid phases total $900, and there's a $150 monthly retainer for maintenance, iterations, and adding your second business pipeline when you're ready.

>> Scroll to How This Compares
SAY: I compared this to the main alternatives. GoHighLevel starts at $97 a month with no custom automation. HubSpot needs their Professional plan at $890 a month for any workflow automation. A Zapier setup would need a freelancer build plus ongoing task costs. With n8n, you own everything and the automation is fully custom.

>> Scroll to Budget Transparency
SAY: Your posting listed $100 as the budget. I'm being upfront about this: the full scope doesn't fit in that number. But Phase 0 is free, so you'll get more than $100 of value before deciding whether to continue.

---

### BEAT 4: Close (~20s)

>> Nav: Onboarding
SAY: If this looks like a good fit, the onboarding page has everything I'd need to get Phase 0 started. Just your Instagram Business account, Meta Ads access, and a Google account for the CRM sheet.
SAY: This is designed as an ongoing partnership. Your system grows with your business, and when you're ready to bring in the second business, the same infrastructure handles it.
SAY: Looking forward to hearing from you.

---
