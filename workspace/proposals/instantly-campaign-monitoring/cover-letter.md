```text
Hi, Matthias here. In the Instantly campaigns I ran this year, the issues worth fixing were ones the dashboard didn't show: one security gateway causing most bounces, auto-replies counted as replies, a throttled send rate.

I've attached two files: a one-page checklist of what I'd check in your account in the first week, and a two-page case study with the numbers behind it. The parts closest to your post:

Spotting issues. A July batch where every address was NeverBounce "valid" still bounced at 8.6%, and 10 of the 11 bounces sat behind Mimecast, a security gateway a verifier can't see. Holding those domains out before load took the next two waves to 1.5% and then 0%. On a warm re-engagement campaign, sends came in at 45 a day against an expected 75: the campaign had inherited an 11-minute gap between emails from an older one. Cutting it to 5 brought it to about 72.

Tracking performance. Across two cold waves, out-of-office and leave notices outnumbered human replies 27 to 10, so the raw reply rate mostly measured holiday season. I report real replies and booked meetings per campaign, with automatic replies, bounces and unsubscribes on their own lines. I also check the replies Instantly flags as interested against who actually answered them; in one audit seven had no confirmed reply, one of them asking to reserve 10 seats.

How I'd start: with read access to your workspace, I'd run that checklist over every live campaign in the first week, read-only, and send you a short report ranked by impact with the first two or three changes. After that, a weekly read of the numbers and one change at a time, so each result can be traced to the change that caused it.

I work through UnpauseAI and do the work myself. I'm in Karlsruhe, Germany; my afternoons overlap Eastern-time mornings, and most of this work runs fine async.

How many campaigns are live right now, and which number matters most to you: replies, booked meetings, or something further down the funnel?

Matthias
```
