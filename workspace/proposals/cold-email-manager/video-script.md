# Video Content Guide: Cold Email Manager (p029)

Variant B (execution role, ongoing). Target 1:45 to 2:15. Speak from the
bullets in your own words; the prospect is anonymous, so address them as
"you" throughout. Screen plus camera bubble, start on the work.

## 0:00 Open: you have the strategy, you need clean execution

- One line of who you are, then straight to their post.
- The reframe to land: most applicants will try to prove value by adding
  opinions on targeting and copy. Their post says the opposite, the
  strategy is set. What they are buying is execution without drag: lists
  built clean, saved properly, campaigns launched accurately. Use those
  words; they are the client's own.
- Credibility in passing: this is what you do inside a live cold-email
  operation today, so everything in the video comes from running work.

## 0:20 Walk one task cycle on screen

- Have a simple left-to-right flow visible: instruction, saved list,
  sample review, verification, sync, mapping check, launch, completion
  note.
- The cycle, step by step:
  - Their instruction becomes a written checklist before any tool opens.
  - The list is built to their filters (industry, title, company size,
    geography, keywords) and saved under THEIR naming convention, so it
    is reproducible and findable.
  - A sample gets reviewed against the ICP before the full export; bad
    fits come out and the removal logic is noted.
  - Every email is verified (deliverable, catch-all, invalid) before
    leads sync into the campaign tool.
  - Campaign mapping is checked field by field before go-live.
  - They get a short completion note: what was built, where it lives,
    what was flagged, time spent.
- Their phrase to echo here: "check that campaigns are mapped correctly
  before going live".

## 1:00 The differentiator: a double-check with a track record

- This is the beat that wins the job. Everyone claims attention to
  detail; almost nobody can name a catch.
- The story, kept tight: in a live operation with 3 active Instantly
  campaigns, you caught a sequence-timing bug by reading the Instantly
  API spec instead of trusting what the UI showed. The kind of error
  that ships silently when nobody re-checks, and the reason "double-check
  before marking complete" is a habit, not a line in a proposal.
- Tools honesty, one line: Apollo, Instantly, and Sales Navigator are
  daily drivers. Clay and Smartlead follow the same patterns; their post
  says learning new lead databases quickly is what matters, and that is
  the honest framing.

## 1:40 Close: price in one line, then the easy question

- Price, stated plainly: $36 an hour, and a typical list-to-launch cycle
  runs 2 to 4 hours, so they see cost per task, not an open meter.
- End on the one-line question, not a pitch: which tool is the campaign
  side running on today, Instantly or Smartlead? The answer scopes
  onboarding to a day.
- One take, one ending.

## Terms to gloss if you say them on camera

- ICP: ideal customer profile, the exact people you target
- Apollo: a contact database for finding verified work emails
- Instantly: a cold email sending platform
- Smartlead: another cold email sending platform
- Sales Navigator: LinkedIn's paid prospect search tool
- Clay: a spreadsheet tool that enriches lead data
- Catch-all: a domain that accepts all mail, so the address is unverifiable
- Enrichment: adding emails and company data to a contact record
- Mapping: matching list columns to campaign fields before launch
- Sequence: the timed series of emails a campaign sends
