THE PLATFORM · GOVERNANCE
Governed, in plain terms.
OnePilot governs every process its Digital Co-Worker runs. What a safe, compliant AI-orchestrated process observes:
Bounded autonomy
Acts only within the permissions and limits you set — nothing beyond.
Human authority
A person owns the moves that matter, and can review, override or stop the agent.
Four-eyes / segregation of duties
Whatever initiates a step never approves it. Key moves need a second person.
Robust & fail-safe
Tested before it runs, behaves predictably, and safe-stops or rolls back on error.
Traceable & explainable
Every action and data change is logged — who, what, when and why — and can be explained.
Monitored & managed by exception
Outputs are watched in real time; anomalies go to a person, the rest just runs.
Governed data quality — completeness · consistency · timeliness · accuracy · anomaly checks · lineage
reconciled to your system of record — SAP, or wherever your truth lives
