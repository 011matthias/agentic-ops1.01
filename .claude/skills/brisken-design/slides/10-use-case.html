Intercompany Funding Request
Intercompany funding: when one company in a group lends cash to another, run through the group’s own in-house bank.
BEFORE · TODAY, BY HAND
At a chemicals group, subsidiaries email the in-house bank (the group’s own internal bank) to ask for funding. A treasury analyst reads each email and keys it into SAP by hand, across email, SAP in-house cash and the live cash position. Volume spikes at month-end.
AFTER · THE DIGITAL CO-WORKER, IN ORDER
1
Reads the funding email.
2
Pulls out amount, currency, entity and value date.
3
Checks the group cash position and the intercompany limit.
4
Drafts the funding memo in SAP.
5
Routes it for approval.
6
On sign-off, posts and confirms back to the requester.
A person approves the moves that matter.
