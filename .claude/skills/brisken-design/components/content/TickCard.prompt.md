The card Brisken uses everywhere: flat `#F4F6F7`, 24-28px radius, a 49×7 teal tick above the title, **no border and no shadow**.

```jsx
<TickCard title="Systems connected">Your ERP, treasury and finance systems — linked to the banks, market data and authorities around them.</TickCard>
<TickCard title="You stay in control" accent="bright">People own the moves that matter.</TickCard>
```

In a row of three or four, give exactly one card `accent="bright"` — that is how the deck marks the point it wants read.
