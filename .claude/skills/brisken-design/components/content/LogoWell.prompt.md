Holds a customer logo on the "Treasury teams already run on TreasuryCentral" wall, or a partner mark in a footer row.

```jsx
<LogoWell src="assets/customer-logos/ford.png" alt="Ford" />
```

Logos keep their own colours — never greyscale or tint them. With no `src`, the well shows `alt` as a monospace placeholder label, which is the correct way to leave a slot empty.
