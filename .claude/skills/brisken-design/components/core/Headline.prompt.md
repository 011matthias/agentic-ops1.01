Century Gothic display heading — the only typeface Brisken sets headlines in.

```jsx
<Headline size="l">One source for every rate you rely on.</Headline>
<Headline size={92} tone="onDark" as="h1">About</Headline>
```

Never bold it, never italicise it, never letterspace it. Headlines are sentences and take a full stop. Longer headlines step *down* a size rather than wrapping to three lines.
