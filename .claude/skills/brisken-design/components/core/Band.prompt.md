The strip that closes a content slide: what a thing connects to, or the governance guarantee.

```jsx
<Band tone="ink">CONNECTS TO   Bloomberg · Refinitiv · 360T  →  SAP ECC &amp; S/4HANA</Band>
<Band tone="teal" align="center" sub="reconciled to your system of record — SAP, or wherever your truth lives">
  Governed data quality — completeness · consistency · timeliness · accuracy
</Band>
```

Use middle dots to list and `→` to show direction. One band per slide, at the bottom.
