Tracked all-caps teal label that opens every slide and page section — use it instead of a small heading.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>THE PLATFORM · GOVERNANCE</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="muted">SOURCES</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="bright" size={25}>CONTACT</Eyebrow>
```

Separate a section from its topic with a middle dot, never a colon. On `#0F1417` use `tone="bright"`.
