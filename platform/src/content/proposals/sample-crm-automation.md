## What We Understood

You need an automated workflow that syncs new leads from your web forms into your CRM, enriches them with publicly available data, and notifies your sales team in real time. Currently, this process involves manual data entry that takes 2-3 hours per day and is prone to errors.

## Our Proposed Solution

We will build a fully automated pipeline using Make.com that:

- **Captures leads** from your existing web forms via webhook
- **Enriches contact data** using public business databases
- **Creates CRM records** with all relevant fields pre-populated
- **Notifies your sales team** via Slack with a formatted lead summary
- **Logs all activity** to a Google Sheet for reporting and audit

The system will process leads within seconds of form submission, eliminating manual entry entirely.

## Timeline & Milestones

- **Week 1:** Integration setup, webhook configuration, CRM field mapping
- **Week 2:** Data enrichment pipeline, Slack notifications, error handling
- **Week 3:** Testing with live data, edge case handling, documentation
