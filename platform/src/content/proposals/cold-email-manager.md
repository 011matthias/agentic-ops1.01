## What We Understood

You have the targeting, the messaging, and the campaign direction. What you need is the execution layer: saved lead lists built inside the prospecting tools to your ICP filters, reviewed so bad-fit prospects come out, synced into the campaign tool, and campaigns launched exactly as instructed, with the mapping checked before anything goes live.

That is an operator role, not a strategy role, and the plan below treats it that way.

## Our Proposed Solution

Each assignment runs as the same repeatable cycle:

1. Your instruction becomes a written task checklist before any tool gets opened
2. The saved list is built to your filters (industry, title, company size, geography, keywords) and saved under your naming convention, so it is reproducible
3. A sample gets reviewed against the ICP before the full export; bad fits are removed and the removal logic is noted
4. Emails are verified (deliverable / catch-all / invalid) before leads sync to the campaign tool
5. Campaign mapping is checked field by field against your instructions before launch
6. You get a short completion note per task: what was built, where it is saved, what was flagged

Anything off, a filter pulling the wrong titles, list quality below normal, a mapping mismatch, gets flagged to you before launch rather than discovered after.
