## What We Understood

You're running a comprehensive technology audit across your entire stack. We counted 12 open specialist positions covering everything from automation workflows to AI architecture, chatbot logic, full-stack development, CRM integration, and data visualization.

The pattern is clear: you're preparing for a significant scaling push and want an honest assessment of what works, what's fragile, and what needs fixing before you build on top of it.

Here's what most auditors will miss: your automation layer (n8n, Make.com, chatbots, AI pipelines) is the connective tissue between all your other systems. An n8n audit that doesn't understand your AI pipeline, or a chatbot review that doesn't trace data flow back to your automation triggers, gives you a partial picture at best.

## Our Proposed Solution

A tiered audit that starts with your automation workflows and extends into your AI and integration layers, depending on how deep you want to go.

### Tier 1: Automation Audit ($175)
- Full review of n8n workflows and Make.com scenarios
- Bottleneck identification and error pattern analysis
- Optimization report with prioritized recommendations
- Architecture diagram of current automation state

### Tier 2: Automation + AI Audit ($300)
- Everything in Tier 1
- Chatbot flow logic and conversation structure review
- LLM/RAG pipeline assessment (prompt design, retrieval quality, response accuracy)
- Integration map showing how AI components connect to automation triggers

### Tier 3: Full Stack Audit ($450)
- Everything in Tier 2
- Python/Django AI integration debugging
- Cross-system dependency map (automation, AI, chatbot, API, CRM)
- Implementation roadmap with priority fixes and estimated effort

### Deliverable: Structured Audit Report

Each tier delivers a clear, actionable report covering:
1. Current state assessment (what exists, what's connected, what's not)
2. Issue inventory (categorized by severity and effort to fix)
3. Optimization opportunities (quick wins vs. strategic improvements)
4. Recommended next steps (prioritized implementation roadmap)
