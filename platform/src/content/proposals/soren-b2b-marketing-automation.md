## What We Understood

Soren's team has the marketing tools: Apollo, HeyReach, LinkedIn, ad platforms, SEO tools. The gap is the automation layer connecting them. Leads come in from different channels, get scored inconsistently, and follow-ups depend on someone remembering to do them.

The goal is a reliable pipeline: lead captured, enriched, scored, routed to the right sequence, and followed up automatically. Not just traffic. Qualified pipeline.

## Our Proposed Solution

Make.com handles the API integrations (Apollo enrichment, CRM sync, webhook listeners for inbound leads). n8n handles the workflow logic that needs flexibility (scoring algorithms, conditional branching, custom code nodes for complex routing).

Phase 1 builds the lead capture layer: webhook endpoints for each source, data normalization across channels, deduplication, and CRM sync.

Phase 2 builds outbound automation: cold email sequences with reply detection, LinkedIn outreach via HeyReach integration, follow-up cadence management.

Phase 3 adds scoring and reporting: engagement-based and firmographic scoring, hot lead alerts, weekly performance summaries.

## Investment

Retainer model: $1,500-2,000/month covers ongoing pipeline work, new integrations, maintenance, and iteration as the marketing strategy evolves. This positions the engagement as infrastructure ownership rather than a one-time build.
