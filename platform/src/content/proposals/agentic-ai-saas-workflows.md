## What We Understood

You're helping a SaaS company move from AI experimentation to production-grade agentic workflows. The priorities are clear: AI support agents that auto-resolve tickets, escalation pipelines that route to engineering with structured context, and n8n orchestration tying it all together. The team has executive buy-in and an AI champion driving adoption. What they need now is someone who can build the infrastructure and make it reliable.

The job isn't just "connect some APIs." It's designing decision-making agents that classify, route, and act on real customer data, with confidence thresholds, fallback handling, and monitoring built in from day one.

## Our Proposed Solution

A 3-tier AI support system built on n8n:

**Tier 0 (Auto-Resolve):** Webhook receives ticket, Claude classifies intent and priority. Known issues with high confidence (>0.85) get auto-resolved with a generated response. The ticket is tagged and logged.

**Tier 1 (Agent-Assisted):** Medium-confidence tickets get enriched with context from the knowledge base, CRM history, and monitoring data. A structured summary is pushed to the support team via Slack with a suggested response they can approve or edit.

**Tier 2 (Engineering Escalation):** Tickets flagged as bugs or infrastructure issues trigger a structured bug report (repro steps, affected systems, severity) and route directly to the engineering team's project management tool.

Each tier connects to existing tools via REST APIs and webhooks. MCP integrations for real-time tool access where supported.
