# n8n Automation Operator, Documentation & Multi-Client Ops

Ongoing operator role for a small automation consulting agency. The owner
handles strategy and client relationships across clients in ecommerce,
Amazon marketing, print trade, event management, and promotional products,
and wants a reliable operator who executes on his behalf with genuine
documentation discipline and multi-client organisation.

## What We Understood

He is managing a lot of moving parts across multiple clients and needs one
dedicated operator, not another agency, who is strong in n8n, obsessive
about documentation, honest about what he knows, and responsive during EST
business hours. Reliability and documentation discipline matter to him more
than any specific tool or CV.

## Our Proposed Solution

Apply as an individual operator. Lead with the multi-client documentation
system already in use (per-client isolation, per-workflow specs, JSON in
git, dated change notes, handoff-readiness as the bar), because that system
IS the discipline he is hiring for. Answer all six screening questions in
full, ship a working n8n client-onboarding workflow built for his stated
Amazon-agency onboarding case as proof of real n8n depth, and commit
explicitly to an EST overlap window.

## Investment

Operator rate $40-50/hr depending on the week's mix. Suggest starting with
one small real task so he can judge the work and the operating rhythm
before any ongoing commitment.

## About UnpauseAI

Matthias runs production automation across multiple clients. UnpauseAI is
the consultancy and the multi-client system behind that work, used here as
the portfolio proof of the documentation and isolation discipline the role
is built around.
