## What We Understood

A U.S. business services company wants one operator for two jobs over a four-week pilot: running Instantly deliverability across Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace mailboxes, and preparing four ICP campaigns a week through AI research, scoring, enrichment and list cleaning rather than database exports. They supply strategy, ICP briefs, copy and tools.

## Our Proposed Solution

A week-one audit of every sending domain and mailbox with a findings list, then one n8n workflow for the weekly lists: model research with a source URL per claim and a review tab for anything without a source, provider-aware address checks with an MX hold for Mimecast and no-MX domains, dedupe against every live campaign, a 20-row hand check, a pre-launch check on every campaign, and a weekly report computed from the campaign records.

## Timeline & Milestones

| 1 | Audit and findings list | Research workflow built on the first ICP briefs |
| 2 | Fixes applied, seed tests repeated | Four ICP lists |
| 3 | Pre-launch checks, volume only where placement holds | Four lists, scoring adjusted from replies |
| 4 | Monitoring and runbook | Four lists, workflow documented |
