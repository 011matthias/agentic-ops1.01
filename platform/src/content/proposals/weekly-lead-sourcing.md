## What We Understood

You need leads sourced from three designated sources every week, accurate contact information researched for each, everything organized into your provided template, and the completed list delivered once per week. Accuracy, consistency, and clean data entry are the bar, over 1 to 3 months of recurring work.

## Our Proposed Solution

Each weekly delivery runs the same cycle: source from your three sources, research and verify contact information, enter into your template exactly as provided, sample-check the batch, deliver on the deadline with a short note covering lead count per source and anything flagged (duplicates, dead contacts, a source running dry).

## Timeline & Milestones

| Phase | When | What happens |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Trial | Week 1 | First full list at the posted $20; you check accuracy and data quality against your template |
| Steady state | Weekly | One delivery per week on your set day, completion note included |

## Investment

First weekly list at the posted $20 so the data quality can be checked against the template before any ongoing commitment. The recurring weekly rate gets agreed after that trial, based on actual list size and research depth per cycle.
