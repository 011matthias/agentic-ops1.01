## What We Understood

You own an early-stage startup that helps insurance agents analyze policies and sell more insurance. To sign your first clients, you need to get in front of insurance agents at volume and book demos. You said it plainly: you do not know how to find these agents in volume or run mass email that stays out of spam, and you want someone to handle that for you.

That is two problems that are really one motion: a sourcing problem (where do thousands of real, reachable insurance agents come from) and a deliverability problem (how do cold emails reach the inbox instead of the spam folder). We run that motion end to end for another client today.

## Our Proposed Solution

A done-for-you cold outbound operation. You do not learn a tool or run anything. You show up to the demos we book.

**Sourcing.** Insurance agents are unusually findable. Licensed agents are public record through state Department of Insurance registries, and they cluster in known directories and on LinkedIn. We build a verified list from Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and public license data, then verify every email so the bounce rate stays under 2 percent.

**Deliverability infrastructure.** Cold email lands in spam when it is sent from your real domain on cold mailboxes with no authentication. We avoid that: separate sending domains (not your primary), full SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication, two to three weeks of mailbox warm-up before a single cold email goes out, low daily volume per mailbox, and inbox rotation to scale safely.

**Campaigns.** We write the sequence for insurance agents, launch it, watch replies, and book interested agents straight onto your calendar. We report what is working and tune subject lines, targeting, and copy every week.

## How It Works

1. Source and verify a list of insurance agents that match your ideal customer
2. Stand up sending domains and mailboxes, authenticate them, warm them up
3. Write and launch the cold sequence, ramping volume as the inboxes mature
4. Catch replies, book demos to your calendar, report and optimize weekly

## Timeline & Milestones

| Phase | When | What happens |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Setup | Weeks 1-2 | Lead list built and verified, sending domains and mailboxes live and authenticated, warm-up started, first sequence written |
| First send | End of Week 2 | First campaign goes out as warm-up completes |
| Managed | Ongoing monthly | Run campaigns, book meetings, replenish lists, weekly optimization and reporting |

Warm-up is a hard physical constraint: mailboxes need two to three weeks before they can send cold volume without hurting deliverability. We start it on Day 1 so the first real send lands the moment they are ready.
