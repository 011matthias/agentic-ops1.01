# UnpauseAI pricing

Machine-readable twin of https://unpauseai.com/pricing. Every project is
scoped and priced before work begins; fixed price, no hourly billing.

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Self-Service | $99 - $349 | Make.com or n8n blueprint file plus step-by-step setup documentation; you set it up in your own accounts |
| Premium | $800 - $2,500 | We implement the automation in your accounts, configure it with your data, test every path, and provide 30 days of post-launch support |
| Custom | From EUR 2,500 | Scoped proposal for custom automation projects: systems assessed, workflow complexity and testing level defined, fixed price agreed before any work begins |

## How pricing works

- Custom projects start with a scoped proposal and a fixed price before any
  work begins. If the scope changes, we re-scope and agree on the new price
  first.
- Self-service means the blueprint file and documentation; premium means we
  implement, configure, test, and support it for 30 days after launch.
- A self-service blueprint price is credited toward a premium implementation
  if you upgrade later.
- Post-launch support covers bug fixes, configuration adjustments, and
  edge-case guidance for 30 days. Ongoing changes after that are scoped as
  maintenance agreements or follow-up projects.
