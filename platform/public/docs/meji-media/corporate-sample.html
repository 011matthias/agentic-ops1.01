Why the 100 / 40 / 30 / 30 split (and not raw-proportional)

A strict universe-proportional split would over-weight Managing Director and under-represent the highest-conversion cut. The 200-company sample is instead weighted to where the buyer actually sits for a corporate Christmas party at each company size:

  • PA / EA / Office Manager (100 in sample, biggest slot). At mid-market companies, the PA tier typically researches venues, opens the booking inquiry, and decides the shortlist. The CEO might sign off, but the PA pulled the trigger. This is the buyer.
  • HR Manager / Head of HR (40). Same company-size band, secondary slot. Owns staff events and year-end celebrations, but venue selection is often co-owned with the PA tier rather than HR-led.
  • CEO and Managing Director (30 each). At small companies (11-50 employees) there is no dedicated organiser, so the CEO or MD IS the buyer by default. Smaller slot allocation reflects lower revenue per booking at this company size, not weaker conversion.

What this means for pilot runway

Total addressable UK universe across the four cuts is roughly 91,000 people before de-overlap and the Moonlight & Mistletoe past-customer exclusion. Your Apollo Basic plan supports about 2,500 verified contacts per month at current credit pricing, which works out to roughly 3% of the addressable universe per month. There is multiple months of ICP-fit volume to scale into before we would need to refine the cuts further.

What we explicitly do not see at this stage

The Apollo people-search free path returns company name, role title, and "has-this-field-on-record" flags for email, industry, and employee count. It does NOT return person names, email addresses, phone numbers, exact employee counts, company websites, or industry classifications. Those all sit behind the per-credit enrichment endpoints, which is why this sample is "names only, no credit spend": you get the company-shape signal you need to approve the direction, without burning the budget that pays for actually reaching them.

How We Got This Data

Pulled directly from Apollo's mixed_people/api_search endpoint, the free read path that returns company names and titles without spending verification credits.

  1. Four separate searches, one per ICP segment, each filtered on UK location plus the size band plus the title list.
  2. Results deduplicated by company name within each segment, then across segments.
  3. Universe counts are the full total_entries returned by Apollo for each segment (the addressable population), the sample is the first N unique companies returned.
  4. No personal data, no email addresses, no phone numbers have been pulled or revealed at this stage. That happens at the enrich step after your go.
  5. The Moonlight & Mistletoe past-customer exclusion runs at enrich time against the booking archive (full_data_parties.leader_email and delegates.email) by domain match.
