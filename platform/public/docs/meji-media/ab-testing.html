A/B Testing Battle Plan

Split-test your email templates to find what gets more replies. This guide covers everything: how it works, how to run a test, and a longer-term testing strategy.

How A/B Testing Works

Track Results

Key point: Once assigned, a lead stays in their group for the entire follow-up sequence (initial email, follow-up #2, follow-up #3). The assignment is permanent.

Quick-Start Battle Plan

Enable A/B testing

Go to eu2.make.com → Data Stores → Pipeline Config → set ab_testing_enabled to true

Edit your Version B templates

Data Stores → Email Templates. Edit the _b variant of the email you want to test. Change the subject line, body text, or both.

Let it run

Wait for at least 30-50 enquiries per group before drawing conclusions. With fewer leads, random chance can skew results.

Check your results

Open your Google Sheet → AB_Analytics tab. Compare reply rates between Group A and Group B. The Lift column shows the difference.

Pick the winner

Copy the winning version into both A and B templates (or disable A/B testing and keep the winner as Version A).

Example Test Ideas
Version A (Control)
Subject: "Thanks for your enquiry"
Professional, straightforward acknowledgement. Includes AI-generated personalised opener.
Version B (Challenger)
Subject: "Your Christmas party is going to be amazing"
Warmer, more enthusiastic tone. Leads with excitement about their event before the details.

What to test (in order of impact)

  1. Subject lines -- highest impact, easiest to test. Change one thing at a time.
  2. Opening line tone -- formal vs friendly, question vs statement.
  3. Call-to-action -- "Reply to this email" vs "Call us on..." vs "Book a viewing".
  4. Email length -- short and punchy vs detailed and informative.
Reading Your Results

Open your Google Sheet → AB_Analytics tab:

MetricWhat It Means
Total LeadsHow many enquiries in each group
RepliedHow many people replied to your emails
Reply RatePercentage who replied -- this is the key metric
ColdWent through all follow-ups without replying
Still ActiveStill in the follow-up sequence
Lift (B vs A)Difference in reply rates -- positive means B is winning

Statistical significance: With 30+ leads per group, a 5%+ lift is meaningful. With fewer leads, differences could be random noise. When in doubt, keep running.

Longer-Term Testing Roadmap
Phase 1

Subject line testing

Start here. Test 2-3 different subject line approaches over 2-4 weeks. Find your best-performing hook.

Phase 2

Tone and style

With the winning subject line locked in, test different email body styles: formal vs casual, short vs detailed.

Phase 3

Follow-up timing

Adjust cadence settings (not A/B -- change timing in Pipeline Config). Try faster follow-ups for warm leads or longer gaps for standard.

Phase 4

Seasonal refresh

Re-test before peak seasons (September-November for Christmas parties). What worked in spring may not work in autumn.
