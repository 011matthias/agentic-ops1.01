Lead Scoring & Priority System

Every enquiry gets an automatic score that determines priority, email timing, and whether your team gets an immediate notification. Here's how it works and how to tune it.

How Scoring Works
Scoring Pipeline
Enquiry Data
Name, email, phone, topic, organisation, message
→
Scoring Engine
Weighted factors are summed to produce a numeric score
→
Priority Assignment
Hot (50+), Warm (25-49), Standard (<25)
Priority Tiers
50+

Hot Lead

Your team gets an instant email notification with full details. The enquirer receives a personalised acknowledgement. The lead also enters the follow-up sequence with the fastest timing (6h, 30h). If the person replies, A2 stops the sequence automatically.

25-49

Warm Lead

Standard acknowledgement email sent immediately. Follow-ups sent on a faster schedule (12h, then 48h). Higher chance of conversion.

< 25

Standard Lead

Standard acknowledgement email. Follow-ups on the regular schedule (24h, then 72h). Still fully automated with AI-personalised emails.

Scoring Weights

How it works: Scoring is rule-based (keyword and presence checks), not AI. Each factor checks a simple condition: is a field filled in? Does the topic contain a keyword? The AI module is used only for personalising email opening lines -- it does not affect the score.

Each factor adds points to the lead score. All weights are configurable in Pipeline Config.

How scores add up: A corporate enquiry from a business email, with phone, detailed message, submitted at 2pm: organisation (20) + business email (15) + corporate topic (20) + phone (10) + long message (10) + business hours (5) + form completeness (10) = 90 points → Hot lead. Only one topic weight applies per enquiry (highest match wins). Maximum possible score: ~105.

Example Scenarios
Hot · 90

"We're XYZ Corp and need to book a corporate Christmas party for 200 staff at the Birmingham venue. Can you send a quote for VIP and Super VIP packages? Contact me at 07700 900000."

Organisation (20) + business email (15) + corporate topic (20) + phone (10) + long message (10) + budget keyword "quote" (15) + form completeness (10) = 100. Submitted at 2pm adds business hours (5) = 105.

Warm · 35

"Hi, I work for Acme Ltd and we're interested in booking a corporate event for about 30 people sometime in December. Could you let me know what's available?"

Organisation (20) + corporate topic (20) = 40. But gmail address (0) and no phone (0). If submitted outside business hours: 40. With business email instead: 55 = hot. The email domain is the swing factor here.

Standard · 10

"How much does it cost?"

Default topic (10). Short message (0), no org (0), no phone (0), gmail (0), no budget keywords (0). Score: 10.

Warm · 30

"Hi, I'm looking at the Wolverhampton venue for a wedding reception next spring. We'd have around 150 guests and I'd love to know about pricing and what packages are available for the evening."

Wedding topic (25) + long message (10) = 35. No org, no phone, gmail address. One factor away from warm/hot boundary.

Tuning Guide

Getting too many "hot" notifications?

Raise handoff_threshold from 50 to 60 or 70. Only the highest-quality leads will trigger team notifications.

Missing valuable leads?

Lower handoff_threshold from 50 to 40 or 35. More leads will trigger notifications, but there will be more noise.

Phone numbers should matter more?

Increase weight_has_phone. Leads with phone numbers will score higher.

Corporate emails should matter more?

Increase weight_business_email. Enquiries from company domains (not gmail/yahoo) will score higher.

Want corporate events to score highest?

Increase topic_weight_corporate and lower topic_weight_default. Corporate enquiries will stand out more.

Want to disable team notifications entirely?

Set handoff_enabled to false. All leads will go through the automated follow-up sequence regardless of score.

Want to change who gets notified?

Edit handoff_email -- comma-separated list of email addresses. Currently: gurmej@mejimedia.com, enquire@christmasofficeparty.co.uk.
