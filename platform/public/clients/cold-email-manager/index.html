1. The price

$33.85 an hour. A typical cycle, from your ICP filters to a launched campaign, runs 2 to 4 hours: saved list built, bad fits removed, emails verified, leads synced, mapping checked, campaign live.

Every completion note includes the time spent, so you see cost per task, not an open meter. An experienced operator spends fewer hours per cycle than a generalist, which keeps the per-task total in a normal range while the launch gets double-checked.

2. The process, per task

Your instructions run through the same cycle every time:

  1. Your instruction becomes a written checklist before any tool gets opened
  2. The saved list is built to your filters (industry, title, company size, geography, keywords) and saved under your naming convention, so it's reproducible
  3. A sample is reviewed against the ICP before the full export; bad-fit prospects come out and the removal logic is noted
  4. Every email is verified (deliverable / catch-all / invalid) before leads sync to the campaign tool
  5. Campaign mapping is checked field by field against your instructions before go-live
  6. You get a short completion note: what was built, where it's saved, what was flagged, time spent
Flags come before launch, not after
A filter pulling the wrong titles, list quality below normal, a mapping mismatch: these get raised to you before the campaign goes live. Catching them after costs sender reputation and burned leads.

Apollo, Instantly, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator are daily drivers. Clay and Smartlead follow the same patterns; new lead databases are quick to pick up.
