Common Questions

Filip, here are the questions that come up most often on compliance automation projects. Straight answers, no hedging.

About the Tool

1 Is this a replacement for a lawyer?

No. The compliance scanner does the analytical work -- flagging potential issues, referencing specific articles, and suggesting rewording. Think of it as a pre-review that makes a lawyer's job faster and cheaper. You'll still want qualified legal counsel to sign off on the final version of your privacy policy and terms of service.

2 How is this different from using ChatGPT to review my documents?

Three differences. First, the scanner uses structured regulatory checklists per jurisdiction, not general-purpose prompts. It knows the specific articles, enforcement precedents, and meditation-app edge cases. Second, it gives you risk ratings and traceable references -- every flag links to a specific article in a specific regulation. Third, it's configured for your exact markets and data flows, not a generic review.

3 Can I run the scanner myself after the project?

Yes. The scanner is yours to keep and run whenever your policies change. The $300 covers the build and configuration. Future scans cost nothing additional.

4 What if my app changes and I need to re-scan?

That's the whole point. Unlike a one-time legal review, the scanner runs on demand. New feature? New data collection? New market? Run the scan again. The regulatory checklists update as regulations change.

Legal & Compliance

5 Does this constitute legal advice?

No. The scanner output is an analytical tool, not legal advice. We explicitly recommend using the compliance report as a briefing document for qualified legal counsel. The tool flags potential issues -- a lawyer confirms and finalizes.

6 How accurate are the regulatory checklists?

The checklists are built from the official regulation text, enforcement guidance, and published supervisory authority interpretations. For GDPR specifically, we reference EDPB guidelines and ICO guidance. However, regulatory interpretation can vary by case, which is why legal sign-off remains important.

7 What about GDPR Article 9 and health data?

This is one of the meditation-app-specific flags. If your app collects data about meditation sessions, mood tracking, sleep patterns, or breathing exercises, this data may be classified as "health data" under Article 9. The scanner specifically checks for this and flags any clauses in your privacy policy that need to address special category data processing.

8 Do I need a Data Protection Officer (DPO)?

Under EU GDPR, you need a DPO if you process special categories of data (like health data) on a large scale. The scanner will flag whether your data processing activities likely trigger this requirement based on the information in your privacy policy.

Technical

9 What format should I share my documents in?

Google Docs, PDF, Word, or plain text all work. The scanner parses the document structure regardless of format.

10 How long does a scan take?

The initial scan takes a few minutes per document. The full report (with all five jurisdictions, risk ratings, and suggested fixes) is generated automatically.

11 What does the output look like?

An HTML report with each flagged clause highlighted, a risk rating (high/medium/low), the specific regulatory article it relates to, and suggested alternative language. You can also export as PDF. There's a sample report on the solution page.
