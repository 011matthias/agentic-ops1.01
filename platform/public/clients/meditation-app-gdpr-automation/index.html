The Challenge

Filip is expanding to UK, EU, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. Each market has its own privacy framework. A one-time legal review gives a snapshot -- but every app update, new feature, or regulation change makes it outdated. For a solo founder managing five jurisdictions, this becomes a recurring cost.

The meditation app adds specific complexity: if it tracks sessions, mood, or sleep, that data may qualify as health-related under GDPR Article 9. Push notification consent differs between UK and Canadian law. Cross-border data transfers need documentation most apps miss.

Why a one-time review is not enough

Privacy policies need to stay current. New SDK integrations, feature additions, and regulatory updates can all create compliance gaps. The scanner makes that re-review fast enough to actually happen.
