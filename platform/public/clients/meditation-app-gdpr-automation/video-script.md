## BEAT 2 -- Structure

>> Nav: Overview page / The Challenge heading

SAY: So here's the core problem with a one-time legal review. It gives you a snapshot. The moment you add a feature, change how you collect data, or a regulation updates, that review is outdated. Across five jurisdictions, that gets expensive fast. What I've built is a compliance scanner -- a tool you can run on demand, every time something changes.

>> Nav: Solution page / Scanner Architecture heading

SAY: You paste in your terms of service or privacy policy, and the scanner runs it through structured regulatory checklists for each of your five target markets. UK GDPR, EU GDPR, Canadian PIPEDA, Australian Privacy Act, NZ Privacy Act. Each one has different requirements, and the scanner flags exactly which clauses need attention, with specific article references.

>> Nav: Solution page / Meditation-Specific Flags heading

SAY: Here's where it gets specific to your app. If your meditation app tracks sessions, mood, or sleep data, that may qualify as health-related data under GDPR Article 9 -- that's a special category with stricter requirements. Push notification consent rules differ between UK and Canadian law. And cross-border data transfers need specific documentation that most apps miss completely.

## BEAT 3 -- Edge Cases

>> Nav: Solution page / Jurisdiction Framework heading

SAY: The five jurisdictions overlap in some areas but diverge in others. The scanner handles this by running separate checklists per framework and cross-referencing the results. So you get one unified report, but each flag traces back to the specific regulation and article that applies.

>> Nav: Solution page / How It Works heading

SAY: The output gives each flagged clause a risk rating, the regulatory reference, and suggested alternative language. Again, this isn't legal advice -- it's a structured briefing document. Your lawyer reviews the flagged items directly instead of reading every line from scratch. It's a tool that makes the whole process faster and cheaper for you.
