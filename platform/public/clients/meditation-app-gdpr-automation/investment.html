Why Option 2 exists

GDPR enforcement guidance changes. The EDPB publishes new opinions. The ICO updates its position on health data. Canada amends PIPEDA. With Option 1, your checklists are frozen at the time of build. With Option 2, they stay current -- so the scanner catches issues that didn't exist when it was first built. You still run it yourself; we just keep the regulatory engine up to date.

The scanner reduces legal cost, not legal need

We're not saying skip the lawyer. We're saying the scanner does the heavy lifting so the lawyer spends 2-3 hours instead of 20. Your total cost (scanner + focused legal review) will likely be lower than a full review from scratch. The difference between the two scanner options: with Build + Maintain, the checklists stay current as regulations evolve. With Build + Hand Off, you get a powerful tool that works perfectly today but won't automatically reflect future regulatory changes.
