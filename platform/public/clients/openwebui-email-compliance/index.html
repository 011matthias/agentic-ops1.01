The Real Challenge

You've done this before. The Azure/OpenAI email monitoring project was a first pass. This time you need it production-grade: event-driven, self-healing, GDPR-compliant, and hands-off after deployment.

The part most proposals will miss: a 100+ person UK company routing customer emails through AI has real regulatory exposure. International data transfers to Anthropic's US servers. Automated decision-making that may trigger Article 22 rights. Retention of personal data without proper lifecycle management. That compliance layer is what separates a script from a system.

Why this matters

Most proposals will focus on connecting APIs. We're focused on building this so your compliance team signs off on it -- because at your scale, the GDPR exposure is the real risk, not the technical integration.

GDPR First

Compliance isn't bolted on at the end. It's baked into every layer of the architecture.

Data Minimization
Reduce exposure at the source
  • Strip PII before storage
  • Process only required fields
  • Anonymize where possible
  • Minimal data footprint
Controlled Retention
Automated lifecycle management
  • Auto-archive after X days
  • Auto-purge after Y days
  • Full audit trail
  • No manual cleanup needed
Transfer Compliance
Cross-border data protection
  • Anthropic API zero-retention mode
  • UK-to-US transfer documented
  • Staff access controls
  • Role-based permissions
