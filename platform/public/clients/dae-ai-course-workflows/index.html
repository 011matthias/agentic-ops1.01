The Opportunity

Dae is building a premium digital course focused on using AI for content creation, automation, and business workflows. This isn't a generic AI course. The goal is practical systems that non-technical users can actually apply.

The gap in most AI courses: they teach individual tools (how to prompt ChatGPT, how to set up a Zap) but never connect them into reusable systems. Students leave knowing buttons to click, but not how to think about their workflow end to end.

What makes this project different: every module in this course will center on a complete system, not isolated tutorials. A content creation module won't just show ChatGPT prompts. It'll show how content flows from idea, through AI drafting, to scheduling, to publishing, with automation handling the repeatable parts. Make.com and Zapier handle the connective tissue so students aren't stuck doing manual work between tools.
