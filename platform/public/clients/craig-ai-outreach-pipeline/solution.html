Solution Design

System Architecture

Four components working together. Each one handles a specific stage of the outreach pipeline.

Overview

The system runs on n8n, an open-source automation platform. Each component is a separate workflow that can be tested, modified, and exported independently. Claude API handles the intelligence layer — research analysis and message generation.

CRM Lead Pull

1
CRM Lead Pull
Webhook trigger from your CRM
1
New lead enters CRM (HubSpot, GoHighLevel, or any CRM with webhooks)
2
Webhook fires to n8n with lead data: name, company, website URL, email
3
Deduplication check — skip if this lead was already processed
4
Lead queued for research pipeline
CRM flexibility

We configure the webhook for whatever CRM you use. If your CRM does not support webhooks natively, we poll it on a schedule instead.

Research Engine

2
Research Engine
Claude API analyzes each prospect
1
Fetch the prospect's website HTML (homepage + about page)
2
Send website content to Claude API with a structured research prompt
3
Claude returns: industry, services offered, team size estimate, recent activity, likely pain points, and a relevance score
4
Research summary stored alongside the lead record
5
If relevance score is below threshold, lead is flagged for manual review instead of auto-outreach
Research depth

Claude does not just extract keywords. It reads the actual page content and reasons about the business. A web dev agency with "WordPress" on their site gets a different message than one advertising "headless CMS architecture".

Message Generator

3
Message Generator
Personalized drafts from research, not templates
1
Research summary feeds into a message generation prompt
2
Claude drafts an email referencing specific details from the research (not generic placeholders)
3
Tone and angle adjusted based on prospect context — enterprise vs startup, technical vs non-technical
4
Draft lands in approval queue with the research summary side by side
5
You review, edit if needed, and approve with one click

Delivery + Dashboard

4
Delivery + Dashboard
Send, track, and follow up
1
Approved messages sent via your email tool (Instantly, Smartlead, or SMTP)
2
LinkedIn messages queued as tasks — manual send or Dripify automation
3
Every touchpoint logged: lead, channel, message, timestamp, status
4
Dashboard view shows pipeline: researched → drafted → approved → sent → replied
5
Follow-up queue surfaces leads that need a second touch after N days
You keep control

No message goes out without your approval. The system drafts and queues. You decide what ships.
