Data Flow

Step by step, here is what happens from the moment a lead enters the system to the moment a message is sent and tracked.

  1. Lead enters CRM — You add a prospect to your CRM pipeline stage (e.g., "Ready for Outreach"). The webhook fires immediately.
  2. Deduplication — n8n checks if this lead was already processed. If yes, skip. Prevents duplicate messages.
  3. Website scrape — n8n fetches the prospect's homepage and about page. Extracts text content, strips HTML.
  4. Claude research — The scraped content goes to Claude API with a structured prompt. Claude returns: industry classification, services offered, team size estimate, recent activity, pain points, and a relevance score (0–100).
  5. Relevance gate — If the score is below 60, the lead goes to manual review. Above 60, it proceeds to message drafting.
  6. Message draft — Claude generates a personalized email using the research. References specific details from their website. No generic placeholders.
  7. Approval queue — The draft appears in your dashboard alongside the research summary. You review, edit, or approve.
  8. Send — Approved messages go out via your email tool. LinkedIn messages are queued separately.
  9. Track — Every action is logged: research completed, draft generated, approved, sent, reply received.

Technical Stack

Every component chosen for a specific reason. No bloat, no unnecessary tools.

Component Technology Why
Orchestrator n8n (self-hosted) Open source, no per-execution costs, full control
AI Claude API (Sonnet) Best reasoning for research tasks, cost-effective
Hosting Railway or VPS Your infrastructure, not ours
Email Instantly / Smartlead / SMTP Whichever you already use
LinkedIn Manual queue or Dripify API limitations make direct automation unreliable
Tracking Google Sheets Simple, shareable, no extra tool needed
CRM Your existing CRM Webhook or polling integration
Why n8n, not Zapier or Make?

n8n is self-hosted, so there are no per-execution costs. At outreach volume (50–200 leads/week), hosted tools like Zapier would cost $50–150/month in execution fees alone. n8n hosting on Railway costs ~$5–10/month regardless of volume.
