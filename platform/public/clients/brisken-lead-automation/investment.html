Pricing Approach

Each phase is priced based on the actual scope of work involved - not on what the market charges for "automation projects" generically. The rate is fixed per phase, agreed before work starts, with no surprises.

Because each phase delivers a working system independently, you can approve one phase at a time. There is no lock-in to the full project. If your priorities shift after Phase 1, or if Phase 2 needs to be delayed, you retain full ownership of what was built and can pick up later.

No ongoing fees from us

Each phase is a fixed-price engagement. There are no retainer fees or per-workflow charges from UnpauseAI. Infrastructure costs (n8n hosting, OpenAI API) are third-party and billed to Brisken directly.

On third-party costs

The most significant ongoing cost is n8n hosting. n8n.cloud starts at around $20/month for small volumes. Self-hosted on a VPS is cheaper but requires infrastructure management. OpenAI API costs for response drafting are minimal at typical lead volumes - a few dollars per month. We will give concrete estimates during scoping based on your expected lead volume.

What Comes After the Support Period

The 2-week post-launch support period per phase covers bug fixes, threshold adjustments, and configuration changes. It does not cover new features or scope additions.

After the support period ends, ongoing changes can be handled in one of two ways: a separate change request (fixed scope, fixed price) or a light maintenance retainer if you prefer a standing arrangement. We will discuss the right option once the system is live and you have a sense of how much ongoing adjustment is needed.

The workflows and all documentation are yours. If you decide to manage them independently or bring in another developer, the handover documentation is written to support that.
