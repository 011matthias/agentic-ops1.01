The Challenge

Brisken receives leads from multiple channels: SAP Discovery Center, SAP Store, website forms, and LinkedIn. Each channel delivers leads differently - email notifications, form submissions, lead gen forms - and each has different signal quality and volume characteristics.

Manual processing means slow response times, inconsistent follow-up, and no lead prioritization. High-value leads from the SAP Store sit in the same inbox as general enquiries. There is no consistent first response, no deduplication, and no way to know which leads are worth prioritizing without reading each one individually.

The goal is to fix this without removing the human from the loop where it matters. Automated processing handles the repeatable work. Human judgment stays in place for high-value leads and all follow-up responses.

What We're Building

A unified lead automation pipeline that captures, normalizes, scores, and responds to leads across all channels - with configurable review gates to keep a human in the decision loop.

  • Multi-channel capture - leads from SAP Discovery Center, SAP Store, website, and LinkedIn all flow into a single pipeline
  • Normalization - every lead is converted to a standard schema regardless of source channel
  • Lead scoring - signals like company email, company name, message detail, and source channel determine a High / Medium / Low tier
  • AI response drafting - GPT-4o-mini drafts a personalized first response acknowledging the lead's specific interest
  • Human review gate - high-value leads are held for approval before sending; medium and low tiers send automatically with a notification
  • Reply monitoring - when a lead replies, the follow-up sequence stops and the reply is escalated to human review (Dirk's requirement)
