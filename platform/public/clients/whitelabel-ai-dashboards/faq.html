FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the most common questions about white-label partnerships and how we work.

White-Label

Do my clients ever see UnpauseAI?

No. Every system is delivered under your brand. Your domain, your logo, your colors. We do not appear in any client-facing materials. If you need us on a call, we join as part of your team.

Who owns the code and workflows?

You do. Every n8n workflow is exported as JSON and delivered to you. Every dashboard is hosted on your infrastructure or ours under your control. There is no proprietary lock-in. If the partnership ends, you keep everything.

Can I resell the systems at my own price?

Yes. The per-build price is your cost. You set whatever margin makes sense for your business. Most partners charge 2-3x the build cost to end clients.

Technical

What if a client needs tools you have not integrated before?

If it has a REST API, we can integrate it. Standard integrations (Google, Slack, HubSpot, etc.) are included. Exotic or custom APIs may add 1-2 days to the build timeline.

How do you handle AI model updates and prompt changes?

Claude API updates are backward compatible. When model improvements are released, we test existing prompts and update if needed. This is covered under the monthly retainer.

What happens if a system breaks at 2am?

N8n has built-in execution logging and we configure Slack alerts for failures. Most issues self-recover (API timeouts retry automatically). For critical failures, the retainer includes priority response within 4 hours during business hours.

Can you use tools other than n8n and Claude?

Yes. We also work with Make.com for simpler workflows and can integrate any AI model via API (OpenAI, Perplexity, etc.). We recommend n8n + Claude as the default because they offer the best combination of flexibility, cost, and quality for this type of work.

Process

How do we communicate during a build?

Slack or email, async by default. We share progress updates at each phase boundary. No unnecessary meetings. For complex builds, we may schedule a 15-minute sync at the spec stage.

What if the client's requirements change mid-build?

Minor adjustments (different dashboard panels, additional integrations) are absorbed. Major scope changes (entirely new agent types, different architecture) are handled as a change order with a revised timeline and price.

How many systems can you build per month?

After the pilot, typically 2-3 standard builds per month. Complex builds with custom agents may take more time. We will be transparent about capacity.

Pricing

Why fixed-price instead of hourly?

Our AI-augmented workflow means we build faster than hourly billing reflects. Fixed-price aligns incentives: we are rewarded for efficiency, and you get predictable costs. If a build takes us 40 hours instead of 80, you still pay the same price - and we deliver in half the time.

What if the pilot does not work out?

The pilot is specifically designed to test the partnership at a reduced rate ($2,000 vs. $2,500-3,500). If the output does not meet expectations, there is no obligation to continue. You keep the system we built.

Is the retainer required?

Not strictly, but recommended. Without it, support requests are billed separately and response times are not guaranteed. The retainer ensures you have priority access and ongoing maintenance included.
