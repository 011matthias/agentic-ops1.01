Why Fixed Price

About the Hourly Posting

This job is posted as hourly at 30+ hours per week. The actual deliverable is a defined demo with a clear scope that can be built in a week. Fixed-price protects you: $650 for the complete system, regardless of hours spent.

Here is why fixed-price makes more sense for a scoped deliverable like this.

1
You know the total cost upfront
$650 is the final number. No hourly tracking, no surprise invoices at the end of the week.
2
The deliverable is the measure, not the hours
You are paying for a working demo, not for my time. If I build it efficiently, that benefits you, not me.
3
Single milestone on Upwork
One milestone, one payment on delivery. Straightforward contract with no ongoing billing complexity.
4
If scope grows, we discuss terms
If the demo leads to contract-to-hire or a larger engagement, we switch to hourly terms at that point. No commitments baked into this milestone.
