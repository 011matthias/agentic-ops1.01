What I'm not asking for

  • Equity in your company.
  • Exclusivity. UnpauseAI runs other client work in parallel. The tenant pace stays at one every 2 to 3 weeks as agreed.
  • Decision rights on the product roadmap. That's your seat.

What I am asking for

  • A 30-day trial via tenant 1. If the onboarding-call quality and the 30-day bonus criteria aren't met, we walk away clean. No retainer trap.

  • Realistic bonus criteria. Numbers we can both look at and agree on, set in writing before the call, not negotiated after the 30 days are up.
No invoice surprises

Hours logged and reported weekly. Bonus invoices issued at 30 days per tenant, only if the criteria we agreed on are met. If they aren't, no bonus invoice, no awkward back-and-forth.
