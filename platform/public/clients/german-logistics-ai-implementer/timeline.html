Why start with one

It reduces risk for both sides. You've never seen me on a customer call yet. I've never seen the actual config edge cases of your AI-OS yet. One tenant gives both of us real signal before committing to a pipeline. The 30-day bonus structure you posted already implies this shape; the proposal here just makes the first-tenant trial explicit instead of implicit.

If the onboarding call quality is off, or the tuning round surfaces problems I can't close, we walk away clean after tenant 1. No retainer to unwind, no contractor handover.

Sustainable pace

One tenant every 2 to 3 weeks is my comfortable cadence at this kind of intensity. Two in parallel is doable. Three in parallel only if onboarding calls are spaced out by a week minimum: the calls are the bottleneck, not the config work.

Per-tenant hours stay in the 8 to 15 range. Early tenants pull toward the top of the range; by tenant 4 or 5 they drift toward the bottom because the dry-run set, the template library, and the onboarding-call structure have all hardened.

If volume spikes

A pipeline of 5 to 10 tenants in Q3 2026 fits the cadence above. If demand spikes beyond that, I'd want to talk about it before saying yes, not after. The seat is one person; saying yes to 12 tenants in the same window would mean compromising the onboarding-call quality, which is exactly the thing the 30-day bonus is set up to protect against.
