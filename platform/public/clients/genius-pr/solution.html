Workflow 1: AI Reply Classifier

The priority build. Handles every inbound reply from your email and LinkedIn outbound campaigns. Claude API classifies intent in real time and routes each reply to the appropriate action automatically.

1
AI Reply Classifier
Instantly • HeyReach • Claude API • HubSpot • Sheets • Notion • Slack
1
Webhook receive. Instantly fires a reply_received event. HeyReach fires its reply webhook. Both payloads are normalized to a standard schema: contact email, message body, campaign name, timestamp.
2
Claude API classification. The normalized message is sent to Claude with a structured prompt. Claude returns a JSON object with: classification (HOT/WARM/COLD/NOT INTERESTED), confidence (0.0–1.0), reasoning (one sentence), and suggested_response (for HOT leads only).
3
Confidence threshold gate. If confidence is below 80%, the lead bypasses auto-response and routes to manual review in Slack. This prevents embarrassing auto-replies on ambiguous messages. Threshold is configurable in n8n.
4
HOT lead auto-response. For HOT leads above the 80% threshold, the system sends a personalized reply (generated by Claude) with your Calendly link. Response is sent via the originating channel (Instantly API or HeyReach API).
5
Deduplication check. Before creating or updating any record, the workflow queries HubSpot by email to check if a contact exists. If found, it updates the existing contact. If not found, it creates a new contact. This prevents duplicate records across entry points.
6
Output layer. HubSpot contact updated with classification and confidence score. Row appended to Google Sheets log. Notion lead tracker entry created. For HOT leads: Slack alert with full lead summary, classification, and Calendly link.

Classification Categories

Claude is prompted to classify every reply into one of four categories, each with a distinct routing path:

CategoryCriteriaAuto-Action
HOTPositive interest, asks about pricing, mentions timeline, wants to book a callAuto-respond with Calendly link, Slack alert to closer
WARMMildly interested, asks a question, wants more informationLog to CRM, queue for manual follow-up
COLDNo clear interest, generic response, not engagedLog to CRM, no action
NOT INTERESTEDExplicit opt-out, unsubscribe request, firm declineLog and suppress from future sequences
Confidence threshold detail

When Claude returns a confidence score below 80%, the lead routes to a dedicated Slack channel for manual review instead of auto-responding. This is not a failure state. It is the safety valve that makes auto-response viable at scale.

Workflow 2: Booking Confirmation

Triggers the moment a lead books a call via Calendly. Updates your CRM deal stage, logs the booking across your stack, and briefs your closer before the call happens.

2
Booking Confirmation
Calendly • HubSpot • Google Sheets • Notion • Slack
1
Calendly webhook. Fires on invitee.created event. Payload includes invitee email, name, meeting time, event type, and any custom intake form answers.
2
HubSpot contact match. Look up the invitee by email. If a contact exists, retrieve their full record (company, lead source, classification from Workflow 1, previous notes). If no contact found, create one.
3
HubSpot deal stage update. Move the associated deal to "Meeting Booked" stage. Create a deal if one does not exist. Record the meeting date and Calendly event ID on the deal record.
4
Sheets + Notion logging. Append a row to the Booked Meetings sheet with invitee name, email, meeting time, and lead source. Create or update a Notion entry in the booked meetings database.
5
Slack closer alert. Post a structured message to the closer Slack channel with: lead name, company, meeting time, how they came in (Instantly / HeyReach), their original classification from Workflow 1 (if available), and any intake form answers. Your closer walks in briefed.
Why the closer alert matters

Without this, closers walk into calls cold. With it, they know who they are talking to, how the lead came in, what classification they got, and what they said in the intake form. That context changes the call.

Workflow 3: Post-Call Intelligence

Activates after every recorded sales call. Fathom delivers the transcript and summary. Claude classifies the call outcome and generates structured notes that land directly in HubSpot.

3
Post-Call Intelligence
Fathom • Claude API • HubSpot • Slack
1
Fathom webhook. Fires after the recording is processed. Payload includes the full transcript, Fathom's AI summary, action items list, meeting duration, and participant details.
2
Claude call outcome classification. The transcript and summary are sent to Claude. Claude returns: outcome (WON / FOLLOW UP / NOT QUALIFIED), confidence, key_points (list of 3–5 bullets), next_action (specific follow-up step), and deal_notes (formatted for HubSpot).
3
HubSpot note creation. A structured note is created on the contact and deal record. Fields include: call outcome, key discussion points, objections raised, next steps, and a link to the Fathom recording. The deal stage is updated based on outcome: WON moves to "Proposal Sent", NOT QUALIFIED moves to "Closed Lost".
4
Auto-create follow-up task. For FOLLOW UP outcomes, a HubSpot task is automatically created with the next_action from Claude, assigned to the closer, with a due date 48 hours out. Nothing falls through the cracks.
5
Slack outcome alert. Post call outcome summary to your team Slack channel: lead name, outcome classification, key points, and the follow-up task created (if any). Full Fathom recording link included.

Shared Infrastructure

Error Handling
Every failure path is explicit
  • Slack alert on workflow failure
  • Failure reason logged to Sheets
  • No silent failures
  • Retry on transient errors

Retry Logic
Handles API rate limits gracefully

  • Claude API timeout handling

Full audit trail across all workflows
  • Google Sheets master log
  • Workflow name + run ID
  • Input/output summary per run
  • Error rows flagged in red

Contact Deduplication

All three entry points (Instantly reply, Calendly booking, Fathom recording) can arrive for the same contact. The shared deduplication layer queries HubSpot by email before any write operation, ensuring each lead has exactly one contact record regardless of which workflow first encountered them.

Entry PointLookup KeyMatch ActionNo-Match Action
Instantly replyReply sender emailUpdate existing contactCreate new contact
HeyReach replyInvitee emailUpdate existing contactCreate new contact
Calendly bookingInvitee emailUpdate + create dealCreate contact + deal
Fathom recordingParticipant emailAdd note to contactCreate contact + note
Why deduplication is hard and matters

Without deduplication, one lead who books a call after replying to an email creates two HubSpot contacts, two Sheets rows, and a confused closer. The shared lookup layer prevents this across all three workflows using a consistent key: email address.
