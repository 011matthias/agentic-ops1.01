Section 3: Classification Rules

Claude classifies replies based on the message content and context. The default prompts work well, but your industry may have specific signals that indicate high intent. Share any special rules before Day 2.

What makes a reply HOT in your context?
Default: explicit interest, asks about pricing or availability, mentions timeline, asks to book a call. Any additional signals specific to PR clients? (e.g., "mentions a specific campaign launch date" = HOT)
What makes a reply WARM?
Default: asks a question, requests more information, mildly engaged but not ready to book. Any specific WARM signals for your market?
What are hard NOT INTERESTED signals?
Default: "not interested", "remove me", "unsubscribe", explicit decline. Are there any industry-specific phrases that should immediately trigger NOT INTERESTED and suppress future outreach?
How to share this

Reply on Upwork with a brief message: "HOT = X. WARM = Y. NOT INTERESTED if they say Z." We incorporate it into the Claude prompt on Day 2. You can also refine it after seeing the first round of live classifications during testing.
