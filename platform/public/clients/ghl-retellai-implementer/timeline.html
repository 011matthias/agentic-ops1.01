From First Setup to Ongoing Operations

How we'd go from introduction to live client coverage for VoiceAI Agency, with a clear evaluation gate before any ongoing commitment.

Phase 1: Test Setup

Week 1

The first week is about getting me up to speed on your stack and proving I can execute a full client setup without hand-holding. No ongoing commitment until you're satisfied with the output.

1
Receive access and SOPs

You share Loom walkthroughs, written SOPs, and access to GoHighLevel, n8n, and Retell AI. I work through these before touching anything live.

2
Study the master snapshot structure

I review the master GHL snapshot and your existing n8n workflows to understand the patterns before I start cloning anything. This is where questions surface early rather than mid-setup.

3
Set up one client environment end-to-end

Snapshot clone, post-snapshot reconfiguration, Retell AI agent configuration, n8n workflow instances. The full sequence on one real or sandbox client.

4
Test the full call flow

Create a test lead in GoHighLevel, move it through the relevant pipeline stage, and verify: webhook fires, Retell call runs, GHL contact record updates correctly. Pass or document exactly where it broke.

5
Document blockers and process notes

Anything that took longer than expected, any step in the SOPs that was ambiguous, any patterns I'd want to template. This becomes useful input whether or not we continue.

Evaluation phase

This is where you see how I work, how I communicate, and whether the output meets your bar. No ongoing commitment until you're satisfied with what comes out of Week 1.

Phase 2: First Live Clients

Weeks 2-3

After the test setup clears your bar, we move to live client environments. This phase is about establishing the working rhythm before volume picks up.

Transitioning from test to live. The first live setups may surface differences between the sandbox and real client accounts. GoHighLevel sub-account configs vary. I document these as they appear so the process gets tighter with each one.

Establishing the change request workflow. We agree on how requests come in (async message, shared doc, ticketing tool, whatever works for your team), how I confirm scope, and how I close and communicate completion. Getting this right early prevents ambiguity at higher volume.

Handling 2-3 change requests per active client per week. Retell prompt refinements, n8n workflow updates, GHL pipeline adjustments. I work through these in the background and report back with what changed and why.

Refining n8n workflow templates. After two or three setups, patterns emerge. Steps that always need adjustment, variables that differ per client, error-prone configuration points. I'll flag these and propose template improvements that reduce setup time going forward.

Phase 3: Ongoing Operations

Week 4 onward

Steady state. New client setups as your VoiceAI Agency adds clients, ongoing maintenance across all active accounts.

New client setups follow the refined process from Phase 2. Each one should take less time than the last as the templates and process notes accumulate.

Per-setup fee ($415) plus a monthly retainer per active client covers the ongoing change request volume. The retainer scales with the number of active clients you're running.

Proactive monitoring. I check n8n execution logs, watch for webhook failures, and review Retell API response patterns. Issues I catch before you notice them don't turn into urgent support requests.

Continuous improvement. When the same type of change request comes up repeatedly, that's a signal that something upstream could be templated or automated. I'll surface these observations and propose fixes rather than quietly doing the same manual task every week.
