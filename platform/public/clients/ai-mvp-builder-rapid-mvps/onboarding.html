Start the paid test

10 hours, $400 fixed. Real matching quality on real data within a few days of starting. This page lists what I would need from you, what you will get, and what happens after.

Quick intake

What you provide for the paid test

  • A slice of real data: 200 to 500 products, 2 to 3 supplier CSVs. As messy as it actually is. Please do not pre-clean it.
  • OpenAI API key for embeddings (under $1 at this volume), or I can run on a key I provide and reimburse out of the $400.
  • A few example queries your team would actually search for, so I can test on realistic patterns.
  • Optional but useful: 5 to 10 known-good matches and 5 to 10 known-bad ones if you have them. Gives me a benchmark to measure against.

What you get from the paid test

  • Working CLI: normalize, dedupe, embed, search on your data with confidence scores
  • Code repo: well-commented Python, runnable end-to-end
  • Matching quality report: one to two pages on what worked, what did not, what the failure modes were
  • Honest recommendation: custom build, SaaS, or "this data needs cleanup before any search tool will work." Whichever the actual numbers point to.
  • A 30-minute walkthrough call at the end if useful

Timeline of the paid test

  1. Day 1, share data and queries via secure transfer (your choice of method)
  2. Day 2 to 3, build and ship the normalize plus dedupe plus embed pipeline
  3. Day 4 to 5, search and confidence scoring plus written report
  4. Day 5 or 6, walkthrough call and decision on next phase

After the paid test

Three paths from here, all of them clean exits:

  • Proceed to Phase 1: apply the pipeline to the full 2,000 products. Hourly, scoped per the Investment page.
  • Pivot to SaaS: if the report shows a hosted product search would cover this fine, I will help with the setup and stop there.
  • Stop: you took a $400 risk and learned something concrete about what your data needs. No further commitment.
Why this shape works

Small paid tests reduce mutual risk. You see real matching quality on your data before committing to a multi-week build. I see whether the project is what was described before quoting a full MVP. The posting specifically called this out as the preferred shape; it is also the shape I default to for any messy-data MVP.
