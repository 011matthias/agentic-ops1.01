Ongoing Work

Once we've worked together, I know your stack, your patterns, and your client expectations. New projects ramp up faster because I'm not starting from scratch every time.

The ongoing model is simple: you send me a brief, I scope it, build it, test it, and hand it back. Async, efficient, no hand-holding needed. If the scope is clear, I can usually start the same day. If it needs more discussion, I'll send back questions before starting.

I don't need to be managed. You'll get a status update at the end of each work block, and I'll flag anything that needs your input before it becomes a blocker.
