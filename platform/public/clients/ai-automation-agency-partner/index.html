How I Work

Every project follows the same four-phase pattern, whether it's a simple webhook integration or a multi-scenario pipeline.

Understand First
Before picking any tool
  • I decompose the business problem into triggers, routing logic, and failure points
  • What data moves where, and what happens when something breaks?
  • Tool selection comes after the system design, not before
Build with Guardrails
Error handling from the start
  • Validation, logging, and error routes are part of the first build, not an afterthought
  • Structured error messages that tell you what failed and why
  • Retry logic where it matters, fail-fast where it doesn't
Ship and Verify
Tested with real data
  • I test with actual records, not mock data
  • Output verification field by field against expected results
  • Deploy with monitoring so issues surface immediately
Document and Hand Off
Your team can maintain it
  • Clean documentation covering every scenario and decision
  • Your team or your client's team can maintain it without calling me
  • No black boxes, no tribal knowledge
