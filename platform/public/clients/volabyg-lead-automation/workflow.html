Your Flow, Traced End to End

The same three handoffs you have today, drawn out so the failure points are visible, then the rebuilt version next to it. This is where the count mismatch hides.

The Current Flow

Four steps, and a lead has to survive all of them to ever get an email that lands.

1. Facebook Lead Ad a prospect submits the in-ad form
2. Transfer the lead is pushed to a Google Sheet
3. Sheet a row is added (your record of the lead)
4. Instantly the 3-step sequence fires: day 0, day 2, day 4-5

On paper this is fine. In practice, each arrow between those boxes can lose a lead quietly, and the final step sends through infrastructure that was built for cold prospecting. Two different failures, stacked.

Where Leads Drop Today

StageHow a lead goes missing
Lead Ad to SheetA sync that retries silently, times out, or skips a row when Facebook delivers leads in a burst. No error is surfaced, so no one knows.
Inside the SheetA shifted column or a manual edit breaks the mapping Instantly reads from. New rows still arrive, but the sequence reads the wrong field.
Sheet to InstantlyDedupe that matches too aggressively drops legitimate separate leads, or an import that only runs on a schedule misses leads between runs.
Instantly sendThe email is created and sent, but fails authentication and lands in spam. It counts as sent, never as delivered. This is the gap between your Facebook count and your reply count.
Why the counts disagree

Some leads never reach the sequence (a transfer drop). Others reach it but their mail never reaches the inbox (the spam problem). Both reduce replies without reducing the Facebook number. The audit measures each one separately, so you know how much is transfer and how much is deliverability.

The Rebuilt Flow

Same inputs, same three emails, but every handoff is verified and the send is authenticated.

1. Lead Ad webhook leads pushed in real time, no scheduled gaps
2. Validate + dedupe conservative match, bad emails flagged, every lead logged
3. Record of truth Sheet or CRM, one row per real lead, nothing silently dropped
4. Authenticated send the 3-step cadence from a domain that passes SPF, DKIM, DMARC
5. Reply detection a reply stops the sequence immediately
6. Reconciliation Facebook count vs rows vs contacts, reviewed so the numbers agree

What Breaks, and How We Handle It

Designing for the unhappy path is the difference between a flow that demos well and one that holds up at real volume.

●
A service is down

Leads queue and retry instead of vanishing. If something cannot be delivered, it is logged and surfaced, not lost.

●
Duplicate or bad leads

Match logic catches true duplicates without merging separate people. Invalid emails are flagged before they hurt your sending reputation.

●
A reply mid-sequence

The moment a lead replies, their remaining emails stop. No one gets a day-4 follow-up after they already booked a call.

●
The Sheet changes shape

The flow validates the structure it expects and warns when something moved, instead of silently reading the wrong column.
