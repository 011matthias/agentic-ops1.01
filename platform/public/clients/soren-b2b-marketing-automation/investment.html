Pricing Structure

Your posting lists $10-15/hr. My Upwork rate is $35.63/hr. That's a real gap, and I want to address it directly rather than ignore it.

The hourly rate reflects the specialization: I'm not doing general marketing tasks. I'm building automation infrastructure that runs independently after it's built. The value isn't in hours worked, it's in hours your team stops spending on manual pipeline management.

That said, the retainer model makes the economics work better for both sides.

The manual hours estimate assumes someone on your team spends 15-20 hours per week on lead processing, follow-up coordination, and pipeline management. At even $15/hr (well below what this work costs in practice), that's $900-1,200/month of labor that the automation handles instead.

Break-even: If the automation helps convert even one additional lead per month that wouldn't have been caught or followed up in time, the entire retainer is paid for. That's not a stretch when leads are being scored and routed in seconds instead of hours.

The difference

A junior hire manages the pipeline manually. An agency builds on their own tools. An in-house engineer writes custom code you have to maintain. This proposal builds on visual platforms (Make.com + n8n) that your team can see, understand, and modify. When the engagement ends, you keep everything and it keeps running.
