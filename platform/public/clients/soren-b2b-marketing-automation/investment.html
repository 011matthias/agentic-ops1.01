10-15 hours per week. Covers pipeline building, optimization, new integrations, and ongoing maintenance. Predictable cost, no hourly tracking.

The retainer makes more sense than hourly for this kind of work. Automation projects don't fit neatly into tracked hours. Some weeks are heavy (new integrations, debugging something unexpected). Some weeks are light (monitoring, small tweaks). The retainer smooths that out and means I'm not incentivized to bill more hours than the work actually takes.

Pipeline building
New automation scenarios as your marketing stack grows. Each new source or workflow follows the same architecture, so additions don't require rethinking what's already running.

Scoring model tuning based on conversion data, sequence A/B testing, and conversion rate improvements. The pipeline gets more accurate over time, not frozen at week-one assumptions.
New integrations
Connect new tools and channels as you add them to the stack. New sources slot into the existing capture-normalize-enrich-score-route pipeline without rebuilding from scratch.

Pipeline health checks, error resolution, and performance reporting. If an integration breaks or lead volume drops unexpectedly, it gets caught before it affects your pipeline for a week unnoticed.
