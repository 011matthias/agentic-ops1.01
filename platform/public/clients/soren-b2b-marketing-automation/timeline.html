Build Timeline

Three phases, each ending with something running in production. Explicit deliverables per phase so you know exactly what you're getting and when.

Phase 1: Foundation

Phase 1
Capture + Enrich Pipeline
Week 1-2 (10 working days)

The goal is a working pipeline for your highest-volume lead source. By the end of Phase 1, leads are being captured, enriched, deduplicated, and written to your CRM automatically.

  • Day 1-2 Stack audit. I connect to your Make.com account, review existing scenarios, map your current CRM fields, and document what's already automated vs. manual. Output: a current-state diagram and gap analysis document you can review.
  • Day 3-4 Capture pipeline for primary source. Make.com webhook endpoint for your highest-volume source (likely Apollo or HeyReach). Data normalization to the canonical schema. Deduplication check against existing CRM records. Output: leads flowing into a staging Google Sheet so you can verify the data before we connect it to the CRM write.
  • Day 5-6 Apollo enrichment integration. Leads that arrive without firmographic data get enriched via the Apollo API. Rate limit management configured for your plan tier. Partial enrichment handling (missing data flagged, not silently passed). Output: enriched records visible in the staging sheet with company size, industry, and seniority filled in.
  • Day 7-8 CRM write + second source. Connect the staging sheet to your actual CRM. Leads write directly. Then add a second capture source (e.g., web forms or HeyReach if Apollo was first). Output: two sources feeding into one normalized, enriched pipeline.
  • Day 9-10 Testing + error handling. Run test leads from each source. Verify deduplication, enrichment failures, and CRM write. Add error logging to Google Sheets and Slack notifications for failures. Output: test results document, error handling confirmed working, your team can test with real leads.
Phase 1 milestone

At the end of Week 2, you have a working capture pipeline. Leads from two sources are being automatically captured, normalized, enriched, deduplicated, and written to your CRM. Errors surface via Slack. You can see every lead in the Google Sheet log.

Phase 2: Intelligence

Phase 2
Scoring + Outbound + Reporting
Week 3-4 (10 working days)

Phase 1 captures and enriches leads. Phase 2 makes them actionable: scored, routed to the right sequence, and visible in a weekly report.

  • Day 11-13 Scoring model in n8n. Code node that calculates engagement + firmographic score (0-100). Weights stored in a Google Sheet you can edit. Initial thresholds: Hot 80+, Warm 50-79, Cool 20-49, Cold 0-19. Output: every new lead gets a score written to the CRM within seconds of capture.
  • Day 14-15 Routing and notifications. Hot leads trigger instant Slack alert with contact details and CRM link. Warm leads enter the nurture queue. Cool and Cold leads route to digest and archive respectively. Output: Slack notifications firing for test hot leads, CRM pipeline stages updating automatically.
  • Day 16-17 Outbound nurture sequences. 5-touch email sequence for warm leads built in n8n. Day offsets configurable per campaign. IMAP reply detection auto-stops the sequence. HeyReach LinkedIn outreach trigger for leads that meet the threshold. Output: test sequence running on a warm lead, reply detection verified.
  • Day 18-19 Reporting dashboard. Google Sheet with weekly summary: lead counts by source, score distribution, conversion by pipeline stage. Automated email digest every Monday with the previous week's numbers. Output: first weekly report generated from real pipeline data.
  • Day 20 Phase 2 review. Walk through the full pipeline with your team. Test edge cases (duplicate lead, partial enrichment, boundary scores). Adjust scoring weights based on initial feedback. Output: documented configuration, team walkthrough completed.
Phase 2 milestone

At the end of Week 4, leads are being captured, enriched, scored, and routed automatically. Hot leads surface in real time. Warm leads get nurtured without manual intervention. You have a weekly report showing pipeline health.

Not included in Phase 2
  • Scoring model recalibration based on conversion data (Phase 3, needs 4 weeks of data)
  • Additional source integrations beyond the initial 2-3 (Phase 3)
  • A/B testing infrastructure (Phase 3)
  • SEO tool and ad platform integrations (Phase 3)

Phase 3: Scale

Phase 3
Optimization + Additional Sources
Week 5-8 (flexible scope)

By Week 5, the core pipeline has been running for a month. You have real conversion data. Phase 3 uses that data to recalibrate scoring, adds remaining integrations, and builds the infrastructure for ongoing optimization.

Scoring recalibration

After 4 weeks of data, you'll know which score bands actually convert. If your Hot leads at 80+ are converting at 40% but Warm leads at 65+ are converting at 25%, you might move the Hot threshold down to 65. The data tells you where the natural breakpoints are. I'll analyze the conversion funnel and propose adjusted weights and thresholds.

Additional source integrations

Add remaining channels from the integration map. Priority based on volume and your team's needs:

  • Reddit keyword monitoring (RSS + Make.com)
  • Google Ads lead form sync
  • LinkedIn Ads lead gen forms
  • Google Analytics conversion data feeding into scoring
  • Ahrefs/SEMrush ranking change alerts
  • Comet Browser (integration path determined during onboarding)

A/B testing infrastructure

Split incoming warm leads into two nurture sequences with different messaging or timing. Track which variant converts better. Results visible in the reporting dashboard. This turns the pipeline from a fixed process into an evolving system.

Anomaly detection

Automated alerts when pipeline metrics deviate from baseline: lead volume drops more than 30% week-over-week, enrichment failure rate spikes, or average score shifts significantly. Catches issues before they affect pipeline health.

Phase 3 milestone

All sources connected. Scoring calibrated to real conversion data. A/B testing running on nurture sequences. Anomaly detection catching pipeline issues proactively. The system is now self-improving.
