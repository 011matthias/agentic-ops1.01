The Automation Gap

A LinkedIn prospect replies through HeyReach. Someone on the team notices, opens Apollo, looks up the company, checks if they match the ICP, adds them to the CRM, figures out which email sequence they should get, and kicks it off manually. If that person is in a meeting, the lead sits for hours. If it's Friday afternoon, it sits until Monday.

Meanwhile, an Apollo prospecting list export has 40 new contacts that need the same treatment. A web form submission came in overnight. A Reddit thread mentioned a problem your service solves. Each one needs attention, and each one competes for the same person's time.

The tools all work. The gap is between them. Data doesn't flow from one to the next without someone doing the work of moving it, deciding what to do with it, and remembering to follow up.

Four Outcomes

●
Every Lead Captured

LinkedIn, Apollo, web forms, Reddit, ad platforms. Each source has its own webhook. A failure on one channel doesn't affect the others. Nothing falls through because someone was busy.

●
Automatic Follow-up

Nurture sequences run on a schedule without manual triggers. Reply detection stops the sequence so nobody gets a follow-up after they've already responded. Timing and channel order are configurable per campaign.

●
Scored and Routed

Every lead gets a 0-100 score combining engagement signals and firmographic data. Hot leads surface to your team in real time. Cool leads get a lower-touch drip until they show interest. The scoring weights are yours to adjust.

●
Visible Pipeline

Weekly email digest with lead counts by source, score band, and pipeline stage. Error alerts when something breaks. A Google Sheet log you can filter, sort, and build analysis on top of.
