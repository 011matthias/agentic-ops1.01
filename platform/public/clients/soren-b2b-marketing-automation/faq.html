Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the automation approach, tool coverage, and how the engagement works.

Automation Approach

How is this different from hiring a marketing generalist?

A marketing generalist handles strategy, content creation, ad management, and campaign execution. I focus specifically on the automation infrastructure: the Make.com scenarios and n8n workflows that connect your tools and run your processes without manual intervention.

Think of it as hiring someone to build the engine, not someone to drive the car. Both are important, but they're different skills.

Can you handle the marketing strategy side too?

My strength is building the automation, not designing the marketing strategy. If you already have a strategy (which channels to use, what messaging works, how to position your product), I can build the infrastructure to execute it at scale.

If you need help with strategy, I'd recommend pairing this engagement with a marketing strategist who handles the "what to do" while I handle the "how to do it automatically."

Tool Coverage

What about Comet Browser? You mentioned it specifically.

I haven't used Comet Browser directly. If it has an API or webhook capabilities, I can integrate it into the same pipeline as your other tools. If it's purely a browser-based tool without API access, we can explore workarounds like browser automation (Playwright) or manual data export into Make.com.

I'd want to look at how you're using it before committing to a specific approach.

Can you manage LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads?

I can build the automation around your ad campaigns: pulling performance data into dashboards, triggering alerts when metrics hit thresholds, syncing lead data from ad forms into your CRM, and adjusting budgets via API.

The creative and strategic side of ad management (writing copy, choosing audiences, A/B testing creatives) is outside my core focus. I'd handle the infrastructure, and you or a specialist would handle the strategy.

What about SEO tools like Ahrefs and SEMrush?

I can integrate the data these tools produce into your automation pipeline. For example: pulling keyword ranking data into a Make.com scenario that alerts you when rankings drop, or importing backlink data into a lead scoring model.

I wouldn't be managing your SEO strategy, but I can make sure the data from your SEO tools feeds into your broader marketing automation.

Working Together

How do you handle the time zone difference?

I'm based in Germany (CET), which is 1 hour behind Tallinn (EET). That's essentially the same working day. I'm available for real-time communication during European business hours.

Is this suitable for long-term collaboration?

Yes. The retainer model is specifically designed for ongoing work. After the initial pipeline is built, there's always optimization, new integrations, and new automation to build as your marketing stack evolves. I've maintained client automation infrastructure for months at a time.

Pricing

Why is your rate higher than the posted range?

The $10-15/hr range works well for general marketing tasks that require manual execution. What I build is automation infrastructure that replaces those manual hours. A Make.com pipeline that captures and scores leads automatically saves 15-20 hours per week of manual work. The math works out in your favor within the first month.

The monthly retainer ($1,500-2,000/mo) is the better comparison point than the hourly rate.

Can I start with a smaller scope to test?

Absolutely. We can start with a single pipeline (e.g., Apollo lead capture into CRM) as a paid test project. If it works well, we expand from there. No commitment beyond the initial scope.
