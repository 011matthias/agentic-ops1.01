What We See

Your team is running a full technology audit: workflow automation (n8n, Make.com), AI chatbot logic, LLM/RAG architecture, full-stack development, CRM integration, data visualization, and more. Each audit makes sense on its own, but the automation layer is what connects them all.

That connection layer is where the real risk lives. A broken webhook between your n8n workflow and your CRM won't show up in a code review. A prompt that works in isolation but fails under load won't appear in a frontend audit. These are automation-layer problems, and they need an automation-layer review.

The Core Problem

An n8n audit that doesn't account for your AI pipeline misses dependencies. A chatbot review that doesn't trace data back to automation triggers gives you a partial picture. Your automation layer is the nervous system of your operation.
