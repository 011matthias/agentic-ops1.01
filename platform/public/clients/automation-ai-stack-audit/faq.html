Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the questions we expect. If something isn't covered here, reply on Upwork and we'll answer the same day.

Pricing

Why is this more than the posted budget? +

The posted budget ($90) covers a single-platform workflow review. Our Tier 1 ($175) covers your full automation layer, including how n8n and Make.com interact with each other and with your AI and chatbot systems. The extra cost buys cross-system visibility that a single-platform audit can't provide.

A reviewer who only looks at n8n won't see how a broken Make.com data mapping is causing downstream failures in your AI responses. That connection is usually where the real problems live.

Can I start with Tier 1 and upgrade later? +

Yes. Tier 1 gives you a complete automation audit with a full report. If the findings point to AI or integration issues worth investigating further, we can scope Tier 2 or 3 as a follow-up. You'll have the Tier 1 report to make that call with real data rather than guesswork.

Scope and Access

What access do you need? +

For Tier 1: viewer access to your n8n instance and Make.com organization. For Tier 2: add chatbot platform access and any AI integration documentation you have. For Tier 3: add read-only access to your Django codebase. A GitHub repo invite with read access is enough. We never need admin credentials or production database access.

How is the audit report structured? +

Four sections: (1) Current state assessment with architecture diagrams showing how your systems connect. (2) Issue inventory categorized by severity and effort to fix. (3) Optimization opportunities ranked by impact. (4) Recommended next steps with a prioritized implementation roadmap.

It's designed to be actionable whether you implement fixes yourself, hand it to your dev team, or bring us in for implementation.

Delivery

Do you also implement the fixes? +

The audit and implementation are separate engagements. The audit gives you the full picture first. If you want us to implement fixes afterward, we'll scope that based on the actual issues we find. Keeping them separate means the audit stays objective. We're not incentivized to surface problems that require our services to fix.

What if you find critical security issues? +

We flag them immediately, before the final report. Critical issues, such as exposed credentials, unvalidated webhooks, or missing authentication on public endpoints, get communicated within 24 hours of discovery with specific recommended actions. We don't sit on findings like that until the report is done.

Background

What's your experience with n8n and Make.com? +

We manage 50+ Make.com scenarios across client accounts and build n8n workflows daily. Our work includes multi-scenario architectures, AI classification systems with confidence-threshold routing, and integration patterns between automation platforms and external APIs. The stack audit came from repeatedly noticing the same failure patterns across different clients' systems.

Are you available in US business hours? +

We're based in Europe (CET), which gives us a 6-hour overlap with US East Coast hours. We're available for calls during your morning. Most async communication, including questions and progress updates, lands within the same business day regardless of time zone.
