What Happens Next

  1. 1
    Tier confirmation and access checklist
    We confirm your tier choice and send a short checklist of exactly what access we need. No guesswork on your end.
  2. 2
    30-minute kickoff call
    We align on your priorities and the specific pain points you want the audit to focus on. This shapes the analysis.
  3. 3
    Audit begins within 24 hours of system access
    Once we have viewer access to your tools, work starts the same day or next morning.
  4. 4
    Daily progress updates
    Short notes via your preferred channel (Upwork messages, email, Slack) so you know where we are each day.
  5. 5
    Final report delivered on day 5–7
    Structured PDF with architecture diagrams, prioritized issue inventory, and recommended next steps.
  6. 6
    Optional walkthrough call
    Walk through the findings together, ask questions, and agree on priorities. Tier 3 includes a 60-minute call. Tiers 1 and 2 can add one if useful.
Post-go-live support included

Questions about the report findings don't expire. If you're acting on recommendations weeks later and need clarification, reach out and we'll answer.
