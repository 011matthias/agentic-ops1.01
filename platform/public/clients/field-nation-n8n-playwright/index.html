The Manual Bottleneck

Field Nation work orders are first-come-first-served. You get an email notification, open it, read the details, check the pay rate, check the location, decide if it fits your schedule, then navigate to Field Nation and accept or request the job. By the time you've done all that, someone else may have already taken it.

The goal isn't just to save time on clicks. It's to reduce the gap between "email arrives" and "action taken" from minutes to seconds.

Why This Approach

Field Nation's REST API (developer.fieldnation.com) has 200+ endpoints, but those are oriented toward companies posting work orders. Provider-side actions like accepting and requesting jobs aren't exposed through the API. That's why Playwright is the correct tool here -- it interacts with the website the same way you would.

n8n handles the workflow logic, email triggers, and decision branching. It runs on your VPS alongside Playwright, so there are no external dependencies or monthly SaaS costs beyond the tools you already have.
