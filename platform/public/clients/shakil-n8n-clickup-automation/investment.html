Why Phase-Based Pricing

Most freelancers quote a single number for all the work upfront. That puts the risk on you. If the first workflow isn't what you expected, you're already paid in full and renegotiating from a weak position.

Phase-based pricing changes that. Each phase is a self-contained contract. You see what you're getting before you pay for the next part.

How it protects you

Phase 1 at $120 is intentionally sized so you can evaluate the work quality at low risk. If the ClickUp automation is solid and the reliability fixes are done well, continuing to Phase 2 is an easy call. If something isn't right, we fix it before moving on.

  • Each phase delivers working automations independently. You don't have to wait for Phase 3 to see value from Phase 1.
  • You can stop after any phase without owing anything more. No kill fee, no contract extension pressure.
  • No retainer required upfront. If you want ongoing support after the three phases, we can discuss that separately with full context on what was built.
  • Phase 1 at $120 is designed as a low-risk starting point. It's enough to show the quality of the work and enough to deliver something genuinely useful.
