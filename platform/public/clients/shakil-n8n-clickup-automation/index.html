The Real Problem

You're running multiple businesses (e-commerce, real estate, a SaaS startup) from a small team. Each business has its own tools, its own freelancers, and its own manual processes. The automation job you posted mentions n8n, Make.com, and ClickUp, but the underlying challenge is bigger than any single workflow.

When your team has to manually move data between tools, follow up on tasks in ClickUp by hand, or wait for a freelancer to send a report, that's time spent on coordination instead of growth. That's what I'd fix: build the automation layer that lets your tools talk to each other reliably, so your team focuses on decisions instead of data entry.

The Bonus: SEO Monitoring Across Your Sites

As part of this engagement, I've built a ready-to-use n8n workflow that consolidates SEO monitoring across all your properties into a single dashboard.
