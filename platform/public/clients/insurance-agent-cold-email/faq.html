Common questions

Will this hurt my main domain's reputation?

No. We never send cold email from your primary domain. Cold sending lives on separate, dedicated domains we stand up for it, so your main domain's reputation is never on the line. If anything ever goes sideways on a sending domain, your real one is untouched.

Isn't cold email spam, or illegal?

B2B cold email is legal in the US under the CAN-SPAM Act when it's done right: a real, working opt-out, a physical mailing address, and honest subject lines and headers. We follow all of it on every send. This is how we run it in practice, not legal advice; if you want a formal read, a lawyer is the right call.

How do you actually find insurance agents?

Licensed agents are public record, so we start from state Department of Insurance registries. We add Apollo and LinkedIn Sales Navigator for working contact data, plus agency directories to fill gaps. Whatever the source, every address is verified before it's used, which keeps the bounce rate under 2 percent.

How fast will I see meetings?

Honest answer: there's a 2 to 3 week warm-up before any cold email goes out, then volume ramps from there. Cold reply rates vary a lot by product, list, and copy, so we don't promise a number of meetings. What we do is optimize toward booked demos and report the real numbers to you every week, so you always see what's working and what's changing.

What do you need from me?

Not much. A clear picture of your product, who your ideal insurance-agent customer is, and a calendar link to book demos onto. That's most of it. We refine the targeting with you in the first week.

Do I own the lists and the domains?

Yes, both. The agent lists we build and the sending domains we set up belong to you. If you ever want to bring the whole thing in-house or move it elsewhere, you take them with you. No lock-in.

What if it isn't working?

You're month to month, so you're never stuck. While we run it, we tune the copy, the targeting, and the volume based on what the numbers show. If it isn't earning its place, you cancel anytime, no long contract holding you in.

Can we scale up later?

Yes. We scale by adding domains and mailboxes, never by cranking volume on a single inbox. That keeps deliverability healthy as the numbers grow, so more reach doesn't mean more spam folders.
