The two asks are one motion

You need two things, and they feel separate: a way to find insurance agents at scale, and a way to email them without landing in spam. They are really one motion. Finding reachable agents and sending in a way the inbox trusts are two halves of the same machine, and the quality of the first half decides whether the second half ever reaches anyone.

Most people bidding on this will hand you a tool and a login and wish you luck. You said you want someone to handle it for you. So this is a service, not software you have to learn: we source, we send, we book demos onto your calendar, and we tune it every week.

How the motion runs

Four stages, all run by us. Insurance agents are unusually findable because licensed agents are public record, so the sourcing is strong from day one.

Source
Find and verify agents
  • State license registries
  • Apollo and Sales Navigator
  • Agency directories
  • Every email verified
Warm
Build inbox trust
  • Separate sending domains
  • SPF, DKIM, DMARC set up
  • 2 to 3 weeks warm-up
  • Low volume per inbox
Send
Run the sequence
  • Copy written for agents
  • Volume ramps safely
  • Replies detected
  • Sequence stops on reply
Book
Demos on your calendar
  • Interested agents booked
  • Weekly reporting
  • Copy and targeting tuned
  • Lists replenished
The constraint worth knowing up front

Mailboxes need two to three weeks of warm-up before they can send cold volume without hurting deliverability. We start that on day one, so the first real campaign goes out the moment the inboxes are ready.

  • A weekly report on sends, replies, meetings booked, and what we are changing
