Where the insurance agents come from

Insurance agents are one of the easiest professions to find at scale, because licensed agents are public record. Every state runs a Department of Insurance that publishes its licensee registry, so the people you want to reach are documented by name, license type, and often location. That public layer is the backbone; we add commercial databases and directories on top to fill in working email addresses.

Whichever channel a contact comes from, the address gets verified before it's used. That keeps the bounce rate under 2 percent, which matters more than it sounds: a high bounce rate is one of the fastest ways to wreck a sending reputation, so a clean list is the first line of deliverability defense.

Why cold email lands in spam, and how we stop it

Cold email lands in spam for a predictable reason: a real domain starts sending from brand-new mailboxes, with no authentication and no sending history, to people who've never replied. Mailbox providers read that as a stranger shouting, and they filter it. The fix isn't a trick; it's doing the unglamorous setup that earns the inbox's trust, every step below.

  • Separate sending domains. We never send cold from your primary domain. Cold sending lives on dedicated lookalike domains, so your main reputation is never on the line.
  • SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication. The three records that prove the mail genuinely comes from you and isn't forged.
  • A 2 to 3 week warm-up. New mailboxes ramp gradually, building a normal-looking sending history before any cold email goes out.
  • Low volume per inbox. Roughly 30 to 40 emails per mailbox per day. We scale by adding inboxes, never by cranking volume on one.
  • A verified list. Every address checked first, bounce kept under 2 percent.
  • Plain-text style. No heavy HTML, no image-stuffed templates. Cold mail that reads like a person wrote it gets filtered less.
  • Opt-out and a physical address. A real unsubscribe path and a mailing address on every send, in line with US CAN-SPAM rules. This is how we run it, not legal advice.
  • Reply detection. The moment someone replies, the sequence stops for that person, so nobody gets a follow-up after they've already answered.

The campaign itself

With a clean list and warmed inboxes, the actual sending is the simplest part. We write a short sequence aimed at insurance agents, speaking to what they care about and what your product does for them. It launches at a low daily volume and ramps as the inboxes mature. Replies get detected and routed, interested agents get booked, and anyone who answers drops out of the follow-up automatically.

It's a conversation starter, not a blast

The sequence is short and built to earn a reply, not to hammer a list. A few well-aimed touches to the right agents beats thousands of emails to the wrong ones, and it's far safer for deliverability too.
