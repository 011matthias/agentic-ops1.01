Why Phased

The phased structure isn't a sales tactic. It's how the system actually works in practice.

Phase 0 alone gives you something you don't have today. Right now, leads arrive and you're manually tracking them, or they're falling through the gaps entirely. Phase 0 fixes that before you've spent a dollar. Every lead from Instagram and Meta shows up in a structured sheet with a source tag and a timestamp.

You don't need to commit to the full build to get started. See Phase 0 working. Watch leads land in real time. Then decide whether the outreach layer (Phase 1) is worth $350 to you. The decision is easier when you're looking at a live pipeline than when you're reading a proposal.

Skip phases that don't fit. If Meta Ads tracking (Phase 3) isn't a priority yet, skip it. Phases 0, 1, and 2 come to $650 total plus the retainer. That's the lead capture, outreach approval, follow-up, and daily reporting stack. Most coaches won't need Phase 3 until their ad spend is high enough to warrant weekly optimization reviews.

The retainer covers the second business. When you're ready to bring the second fitness coaching business into the pipeline, that gets added under the same retainer. No separate setup charge.
