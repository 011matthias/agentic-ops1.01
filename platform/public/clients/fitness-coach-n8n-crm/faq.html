How It Works

How does the supervisor layer actually work?

When a new lead comes in and a message is drafted, you get a WhatsApp notification with the draft. You reply with "approve", "reject", or send back an edited version. Nothing goes out until you say so.

Over time, once you trust the quality, we can switch to exception-only approval: the system only flags messages it's less confident about. The rest go out automatically. You stay in control of how much you hand off.

What happens if n8n goes down?

n8n Cloud has built-in uptime monitoring. If a workflow fails, it retries automatically. I also set up error notifications so both of us know immediately if something breaks.

The retainer covers fixing issues like these. You won't be left chasing a broken workflow on your own.

Reliability and Ownership

Do I own the data and the workflows?

Yes. Your Google Sheets CRM is in your Google account. Your n8n workflows run on your instance. If we part ways, everything stays with you.

There's no vendor lock-in. You don't need me to export your data or hand over credentials. It's your system from day one.

Cost and Tools

Why not just use GoHighLevel or HubSpot?

GoHighLevel starts at $97/month with rigid automation you can't fully customize. HubSpot needs their Professional plan at $890/month for any real workflow automation.

n8n gives you full custom automation at a fraction of the cost, and you own everything. The Investment page has a full comparison table so you can see the numbers side by side.

For a fitness coaching business at your stage, paying for enterprise CRM tooling before you've validated the lead pipeline doesn't make sense. Build the automation first, upgrade the tooling when volume demands it.

Can the system handle my second business too?

Yes. The n8n instance supports multiple pipelines. When you're ready to expand, we add a separate pipeline for the second business with its own tracking, stages, and reporting.

Same infrastructure, separate data. The retainer covers this. You won't need a second subscription or a second build fee.

Scope and Timeline

What do I need to provide to get started?

For Phase 0: access to your Instagram Business account and Meta Ads account (for the lead webhook), and a Google account for the CRM sheet. I'll create the sheet structure.

For Phase 1: WhatsApp Business API access. I'll walk you through each one. The Onboarding page has the full list.

How long before I see results?

Phase 0 takes 3 to 4 days. Once it's live, you'll see leads appearing in your CRM sheet automatically.

The daily reporting from Phase 2 gives you full visibility within 2 to 3 weeks of starting. By that point, you'll know exactly how many leads are coming in, what stage they're at, and what the conversion rate looks like across your fitness coaching pipeline.

What if I only want some phases?

Each phase works independently. If you only need lead capture and follow-up (Phases 0 to 2), skip Phase 3 entirely. You're not locked into the full build.

We can start with Phase 0, see how it runs, and decide on the next phase based on what's actually useful. The Investment page shows the per-phase pricing so you can plan accordingly.
