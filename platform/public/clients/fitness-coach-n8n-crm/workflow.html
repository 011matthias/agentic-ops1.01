Lead Capture Pipeline

How leads flow from Instagram and Meta into your CRM, get qualified, and move toward booking a call.

The Pipeline Flow

Every lead that comes in from Instagram, Meta ads, or your website follows the same path. n8n handles each step automatically. Here's what happens from the moment someone submits a form or sends a DM.

1
Lead Triggers
Instagram DM, Meta ad form submission, or website inquiry fires a webhook to n8n. The workflow starts the moment a lead takes action.
2
Data Capture
n8n extracts name, contact info, source, and ad set. It normalizes the format so everything lands in the same structure regardless of where the lead came from.
3
Deduplication Check
Checks against existing leads in Google Sheets. If the lead already exists, it updates the record instead of creating a duplicate. You won't see the same person twice.
4
CRM Logging
New lead written to Google Sheets with stage "New Lead", timestamp, and source tag. You'll see exactly where each lead came from and when they arrived.
5
Notification
You get a WhatsApp message with the new lead's name, source, and contact info. No dashboard to check. The update comes to you.
6
Outreach Queue
(Phase 1) Lead enters the outreach drafting queue for a personalized first message. You approve before anything gets sent.

CRM Pipeline Stages

Your Google Sheets CRM tracks every lead through five stages. Each stage tells you exactly where that person stands in your sales process. The fitness coaching business pipeline is simple by design so you can work it yourself.

New Lead
Stage 1
Just captured. Not yet contacted.
→
Contacted
Stage 2
First message sent. You approved it.
→
Replied
Stage 3
Lead responded. Conversation active.
→
Call Booked
Stage 4
Discovery call scheduled via Google Calendar.
→
Closed
Stage 5
Won or lost. Outcome logged.
You move leads manually

Stage changes are manual. You drag a lead from "Contacted" to "Replied" when they respond. The automation handles capture, dedup, logging, and notifications. You keep control of the actual sales decisions.
