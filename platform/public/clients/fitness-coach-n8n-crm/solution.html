Technical Solution

How Your System Works

Four phases, each one independently useful. Start free, expand when ready.

Phase 0: Lead Capture + CRM Pipeline (FREE)

Free delivery You'll have this running before you spend anything.

When someone interacts with your Instagram or fills out a Meta ad form, a webhook fires to n8n. That's the starting point for everything else.

n8n captures the lead data, checks for duplicates against existing entries, and logs it into a Google Sheets CRM. Each record gets a timestamp, the lead source (Instagram or Meta Ads), and the contact's info written in automatically. You don't need to touch it.

The CRM pipeline tracks every lead through five stages:

New Lead
Contacted
Replied
Call Booked
Closed

You'll have a single view of every person who's shown interest in your fitness coaching business and exactly where they stand in the process. No spreadsheet maintenance. No manual data entry. Leads just appear.

Delivered free

This phase ships at no cost. You'll see leads flowing in from Instagram and Meta Ads before spending anything on the rest of the system.

Phase 1: Outreach + Supervisor Layer

Paid phase Nothing goes out without your approval.

When a new lead arrives, n8n drafts a personalized first message. It uses the person's profile and how they found you to shape the tone and content. Someone who clicked a fat-loss ad gets a different opener than someone who found you through an organic Instagram post.

Before anything is sent, you get a WhatsApp notification with the drafted message. You can approve it, edit it, or reject it. That's the supervisor layer you described in your brief. It starts with full approval on every message.

Over time, once you trust the output quality, you can reduce the approval burden. We can configure n8n to only flag messages where the AI's confidence score is below a threshold, or only require approval for cold outreach while follow-ups go out automatically. You decide when you're ready for that. The system won't change behavior until you do.

The supervisor layer is the foundation

Every other automation in this system respects it. Nothing goes to a lead unless you've seen it first. That's not a temporary constraint we'll remove later; it's a design principle.

Phase 2: Follow-up + Daily Reporting

The system tracks every conversation and flags leads that need follow-up based on time gaps. If someone replied three days ago and hasn't heard back since, you get a WhatsApp reminder. Not a list to check, a specific notification with the person's name and what they said.

No leads fall through the cracks because n8n is watching the timeline, not you.

Daily summary

Every morning, you get a report delivered via WhatsApp (or email if you prefer). It covers:

  • New leads captured the previous day and which source they came from
  • Messages sent and whether they were approved, edited, or rejected
  • Replies received and what stage each conversation is at
  • Calls booked and synced to Google Calendar
  • Follow-ups flagged as overdue

It's a one-minute read. You start each day knowing exactly what's in motion in your fitness coaching business without opening a spreadsheet or digging through DMs.

Phase 3: Meta Ads Tracking

n8n pulls data from the Meta Ads API on a scheduled basis: spend, leads generated, cost per lead, and performance broken down by ad set. This gets written into a Google Sheets dashboard you can check any time.

Weekly, the system flags what's performing well and what isn't. You'll see simple observations: this ad set generated 12 leads at $4.20 each, that one generated 3 leads at $18.50 each. The suggestion will be direct: increase budget on the winner, pause or review the weak performer.

Over time, this data feeds back into the outreach quality. When n8n knows which ad sets tend to bring in leads who convert, it can factor that into how it drafts the initial message. The tracking and the outreach aren't separate pipelines; they inform each other.

This is where the system pays for itself

One paused underperforming ad set will likely cover the cost of the entire retainer. Most accounts have at least one ad set that's been running on autopilot without anyone checking its cost per lead. This phase makes that visible.

Second Business Expansion

Your system is built for your fitness coaching business first. That's where the CRM lives, that's where the outreach logic is tuned, and that's where the ad tracking is pointed.

When you're ready to expand into your second business, the same n8n instance handles both. You don't need a new setup. We'll add a separate pipeline in Google Sheets, separate notification routing in WhatsApp, and separate tracking logic for that business's lead sources. The infrastructure doesn't change; we're just adding lanes.

The retainer covers this. When you're ready, we add it.

One infrastructure, two businesses

This is one of the clearer advantages of building on n8n rather than point-to-point tools. Adding a second business isn't a new project; it's a configuration change on top of something that's already working.
