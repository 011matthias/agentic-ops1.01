The Real Challenge

You're running Meta ads and Instagram to attract fitness coaching clients. Leads come in, but without a structured system, some get contacted, some get forgotten, and there's no clear view of where anyone stands.

The real problem isn't just capturing leads. It's making sure every lead gets a quality first touch, gets followed up on time, and moves through a clear process toward booking a call. And you need to see what's happening without checking five different tools.

You also want control. No messages going out without your approval. A supervisor layer that keeps you in the loop while the system handles the repetitive work. That's exactly how I'd build this.
