Two Workflows, Five Pipelines

Workflow 1 (Web Scraping Dispatcher): Pipelines 1 + 2. Handles data ingestion: reading the company registry, dispatching scraping jobs, extracting and classifying documents, deduplicating, and storing results. Runs on a schedule.

Workflow 2 (Thesis + News + HITL): Pipelines 3 + 4 + 5. Handles intelligence processing: synthesizing thesis updates from new documents, filtering news by relevance, and routing changes through email approval before modifying core files.

Dispatcher Pattern

The dispatcher-worker pattern is how we handle 300+ companies without a single failure blocking everything.

How it works

  1. Schedule Trigger fires at configured interval (daily, weekly, or manual)
  2. Google Sheets node reads the company registry (ticker, URL, site type, last scraped)
  3. Filter selects companies due for scraping based on their frequency setting
  4. Split in Batches groups companies into batches of 10-20
  5. Execute Sub-Workflow dispatches each batch to the scraper workflow
  6. Each batch runs independently with its own error handling
  7. Failed companies are logged and retried next run

Why batching? Rate limiting, error isolation, and memory management. If company #47 has a broken IR page, companies 1-46 and 48-300 are unaffected.

HITL Approval Flow

The human-in-the-loop pattern ensures no AI-generated content modifies your core research files without explicit approval.

Step 1
AI generates a proposed update
Thesis synthesis or news filter produces a change to review
Step 2
HTML email sent with before/after preview
Shows exactly what will change, with source references
Step 3
n8n Wait node pauses execution
Pipeline holds state until you respond (7-day timeout, day-3 reminder)
Step 4
You click Approve or Reject in your email
Works on any device. Signed URLs prevent unauthorized approvals.
Step 5
Webhook resumes execution
Approved: markdown append runs. Rejected: change logged, no file modified.

The approval email includes: the proposed change (before/after), the source document that triggered it, and signed Approve/Reject URLs. Clicking either button hits an n8n webhook that resumes the paused execution. Timeout: 7 days with a reminder at day 3.
