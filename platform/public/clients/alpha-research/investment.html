The DIY Alternative

Both n8n workflow templates are downloadable for free on the Workflow page. If you want to build this yourself, here is what to expect:

  • Learning curve: 50–80 hours to learn n8n + AI integration patterns
  • Build time: 100+ hours for 5 pipelines from scratch
  • Typical timeline: 3–6 months (part-time, including debugging)
  • API costs: $100–$600/month (Claude API, news APIs, browser APIs)

The templates give you a working starting point. We handle the hard parts: tuning LLM prompts for your specific company types, configuring scraper modules for 300+ different website structures, and building the HITL approval flow.
