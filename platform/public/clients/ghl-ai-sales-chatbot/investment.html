Pricing Breakdown

Fixed price, milestone-based. Eight components across a 3–4 week build. Snapshot-ready for duplication across your client base.

$3,500
Fixed price — complete AI sales chatbot system
Payable on Upwork. 100% milestone-based: 50% to start, 50% on delivery.
What this price reflects

Eight major components: AI conversation engine, voice pipeline, multimedia messaging, behavior-based follow-ups, conversion tracking, GHL integration, snapshot system, and AI prompt engineering. This is a 3–4 week build with production-grade delivery, testing, and documentation.

What Is Included

Everything required to run the system in production and clone it to new clients.

  • ✓
    Custom AI conversation engine — Sales playbook, objection library, and conversation memory. The AI reads full conversation history before every response.
  • ✓
    ElevenLabs voice persona — Cloned from your recording or preset voice. Integrated with Twilio for voice note delivery via MMS.
  • ✓
    Context-triggered multimedia messaging — Testimonials, screenshots, and media sent automatically based on conversation context.
  • ✓
    3 behavior-based follow-up sequences — Ghost (no response), clicked-not-bought, and asked-not-converted. Each generates AI-personalized messages.
  • ✓
    GHL pipeline integration — Conversion detection, auto-tagging, onboarding handoff. Pipeline stages update automatically based on lead behavior.
  • ✓
    n8n orchestration layer — Connects all components. Webhook receivers, routing logic, follow-up triggers, and error handling.
  • ✓
    GHL snapshot — Captures all CRM configuration: pipelines, tags, custom fields, workflows, templates. One-click deployment to new sub-accounts.
  • ✓
    Provisioning script — One-command client cloning. Handles Twilio number, ElevenLabs persona, n8n webhooks, and AI config per client.
  • ✓
    AI prompt engineering — System prompt, sales playbook, objection library, brand voice tuning. All calibrated during the testing phase.
  • ✓
    End-to-end testing with real sales scenarios — Not sample payloads. Real conversations run through the full system and verified.
  • ✓
    Documentation + Loom walkthrough — System architecture doc, maintenance guide, and a recorded walkthrough covering the full system.
  • ✓
    Maintenance guide — Covers common issues, troubleshooting steps, and how to adjust AI behavior without code changes.

What Is Not Included

Clear boundaries so there are no surprises.

  • −
    Third-party subscription costs — ElevenLabs, Twilio, OpenAI/Anthropic, GHL, n8n hosting. These are billed directly to your accounts.
  • −
    Ongoing maintenance after delivery — The system is built to run independently. Post-delivery support is a separate engagement.
  • −
    Additional niche adaptations beyond the first credit repair setup — Each new niche requires playbook customization (separate engagement, estimated $500–800 per niche).
  • −
    GHL sub-account costs for additional clients — Billed through your GHL agency plan.

Cost per conversation

At typical volumes (500 conversations/month), the AI + voice cost is roughly $0.15–0.25 per conversation. That is the cost of having an AI sales rep that works 24/7, handles objections, and sends personalized voice notes.

Cloning Costs (Per New Client)

Each new client clone costs roughly $200–300 to provision.

Breakdown

  • GHL snapshot deployment: free (automated)
  • Twilio phone number: $1/month
  • ElevenLabs voice persona setup: $0 (clone from recording) or $0 (preset)
  • Provisioning script execution: automated, 5–10 minutes
  • Sales playbook customization: $150–250 (1–2 hours of prompt engineering for new offer/niche)
  • Testing and verification: included in playbook customization
The math

If you charge $1,500–3,000 per client for the chatbot system and each clone costs $200–300 to deploy, the margin is significant from client #2 onward. The initial $3,500 investment builds the template. Everything after that is cloning.
