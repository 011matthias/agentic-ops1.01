Why this matters

Most GHL chatbots use the built-in Conversations AI. That is an FAQ bot – it handles trained Q&A but freezes on real sales objections. An external AI brain follows your actual sales flow, handles pushback, and sounds like your brand owner.

Key Differentiator

What sets this apart

You are building this to scale across clients. That means the system needs to work reliably at scale, not just in a demo. The external AI architecture means you control the conversation logic independently of GHL. Update the sales playbook once, every client gets the improvement. That is not possible with GHL's built-in bot where each sub-account's training data is separate.
