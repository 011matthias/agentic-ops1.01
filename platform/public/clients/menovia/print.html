1. Executive Summary

Menovia is building a digital menopause clinic and needs automation infrastructure to support the full patient journey — from first contact to confirmed appointment and follow-up care.

This proposal covers 8–10 workflows connecting 7 integrations across intake, booking, payments, communications, and CRM. The build is scoped at 4–7 weeks and is designed to be GDPR-compliant, EU-hosted, and built on n8n.

  • 8–10 workflows covering the full patient journey

  • 4–7 weeks to production
  • GDPR-compliant by design — EU hosting, DPAs, consent tracking
  • n8n-based — open-core, no per-operation pricing

2. How It Works

The automation system is structured in three zones:

Data In Patient data enters through intake forms (Typeform), chat (Intercom), and manual entry. Webhooks and scheduled polls keep everything in sync in real time.
Automation n8n processes each event: validating data, routing decisions, triggering the right sequence — booking confirmation, payment link, Dutch SMS reminder, or CRM update.
Results Patients receive timely, personalised communications. The team sees a clean CRM with full patient history. No manual data entry, no missed follow-ups.

3. Solution Overview

1. Patient Intake Flow

Typeform intake form submits via webhook to n8n, which validates the data, checks for duplicates, creates or updates a contact in Zoho CRM, and sends a confirmation email via Brevo — all within seconds of submission.

2. Booking and Payment

Cal.com handles appointment scheduling with automated availability logic. Mollie generates payment links sent directly to the patient. Booking confirmation and payment status are written back to the CRM automatically.

3. Communication Sequences

Email and SMS sequences in Dutch are triggered by patient journey milestones: intake complete, appointment booked, payment received, appointment reminder (24h and 2h prior), and post-appointment follow-up. Brevo handles email; MessageBird handles SMS.

4. CRM as Central Hub

Zoho CRM is the single source of truth for every patient record. All workflow outcomes — intake status, appointment dates, payment state, communication history — are written to CRM fields so the Menovia team has a live dashboard without touching any underlying tools.

5. Intercom Integration

Intercom handles live chat and FAQ deflection. When a patient chats in, n8n checks whether they already exist in Zoho CRM and links the conversation. Support agents see full context without switching tabs.

6. GDPR by Design

Every tool in the stack hosts data in the EU. Consent is captured at intake and logged with a timestamp. Data retention policies are configured per tool. DPAs are in place or available for all providers. See the GDPR Compliance Matrix below.
