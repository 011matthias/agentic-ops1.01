Executive Summary

Menovia is a Dutch online menopause clinic launching a new digital patient journey. As patient volume grows, manual processes for intake, booking, communications, and follow-ups create bottlenecks that limit the clinic's ability to scale while maintaining quality of care.

We propose building a fully automated patient journey across 8 integrated tools: Typeform/Tally, n8n, Zoho CRM, Brevo, Cal.com, Mollie, Intercom, and MessageBird. The system will handle intake, booking, payment processing, multi-channel communications, and CRM management -- all with GDPR-compliant data handling built in from day one.

Scope: 6 integrated automation components across 8 tools
Timeline: 7 weeks across 2 phases
Investment: EUR 5,500 fixed price

The Challenge

Menovia currently handles patient intake, booking, reminders, and follow-ups manually. As the clinic scales, this creates several critical bottlenecks:

  • Missed follow-ups -- patients fall through the cracks when reminders depend on manual tracking
  • Inconsistent communication -- different staff members send different messages at different times
  • Manual data entry errors -- copying patient information between systems introduces mistakes
  • GDPR compliance risk -- sensitive medical-adjacent data handled across multiple tools without a unified consent and data governance framework

The patient journey involves 10+ touchpoints from initial inquiry to post-consultation follow-up. Each touchpoint currently requires manual intervention, creating a process that does not scale.

Our Solution

We propose 6 integrated components that together automate the full patient journey while keeping clinical data safely separated from administrative systems.

Patient Intake Flow

Typeform captures patient information, n8n validates and routes it, Zoho CRM creates the patient record, and Brevo sends the confirmation email. Fully automatic from submission to first contact.

Booking & Payment

Cal.com handles appointment scheduling with automatic Mollie payment integration. Payment status syncs back to CRM, and patients receive booking confirmations instantly.

Communication Sequences

Brevo manages email sequences (pre-appointment prep, post-consultation follow-up) while MessageBird handles SMS reminders. Timing and content are fully configurable.

CRM as Central Hub

Zoho CRM serves as the single source of truth with a custom pipeline reflecting the patient journey stages. All tools sync status updates back to CRM automatically.

Intercom Integration

Website chat conversations sync to CRM, ensuring patient inquiries are tracked and no conversation is lost between support and the clinical team.

GDPR by Design

Consent is logged at every collection point. All tools use EU-hosted infrastructure. A clear boundary separates administrative data from clinical records in the certified EHR.

GDPR Compliance

Medical-adjacent data requires careful handling. Our approach: clinical records stay in the NEN 7510-certified EHR. The automation layer handles only administrative data (contact details, appointment times, consent records, payment status).

Tool Data Category EU Hosting DPA Available Our Measures
Typeform / Tally Intake responses Yes Yes Consent checkbox, data minimization
n8n Processing orchestration Yes (self-hosted) N/A EU server, encrypted at rest
Zoho CRM Contact & pipeline Yes (EU DC) Yes Admin data only, no clinical records
Brevo Email communications Yes Yes Double opt-in, unsubscribe in every email
Cal.com Appointment scheduling Yes (EU) Yes Minimal data: name, email, time slot
Mollie Payment processing Yes (NL-based) Yes PCI DSS compliant, no card data stored
Intercom Chat conversations US (EU transfer) Yes SCCs in place, no clinical data in chat
MessageBird SMS notifications Yes (NL-based) Yes Appointment reminders only, no health data

Clinical records (diagnoses, treatment plans, prescriptions) are never stored in or transmitted through the automation layer. These remain exclusively in the NEN 7510-certified EHR system.
