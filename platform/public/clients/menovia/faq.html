Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the project, answered upfront.

1. Data & Security

All tools in the stack are configured with EU data hosting. Medical/clinical data never enters the CRM or automation layer — it stays exclusively in your NEN 7510-certified EHR system. The CRM handles only administrative data: contact details, appointment dates, package selection, and consent records. Every data flow is logged and auditable.
Consent is captured at every data collection point before any data is stored. Each consent record includes a timestamp (ISO 8601), the form version, and the exact consent text shown to the patient. Separate opt-in tracking for email and SMS. Opt-out flows suppress future sends immediately.
All tools are configured on EU servers. Zoho CRM (EU datacenter), Brevo (EU), n8n Cloud (EU region), Cal.com (EU available), Intercom (EU workspace). We verify hosting location for each tool during setup and document it in your compliance matrix.

2. Project & Timeline

The 7-week timeline is based on our experience with comparable projects. If scope changes arise during the project, we discuss them openly and adjust the timeline together. Minor delays (1-2 weeks) are absorbed at no extra cost. Significant scope additions are quoted separately before any work begins.

3. Tools & Technology

The automation logic is built in n8n, which is tool-agnostic. Swapping a CRM or email provider means updating the relevant n8n nodes — not rebuilding the entire system. We design specifically for this flexibility.
Tool costs are separate from our project fee. If a tool changes pricing, you can evaluate alternatives. Because we build on n8n (not proprietary integrations), switching to a different provider is straightforward.

4. Working Together

We agree on a preferred channel (email, Slack, or WhatsApp) during the kickoff call. We provide weekly progress updates and are available for questions throughout. A structured onboarding checklist ensures we have everything we need before we start building.
Access credentials for the tools listed in the onboarding checklist, your email/SMS content in Dutch, brand assets, and decisions on package pricing and CRM configuration. We provide a detailed checklist — see the Onboarding page.
We work with what you have. If you already use Zoho CRM or Brevo, we integrate with your existing setup rather than creating new accounts. During onboarding, we map your current tools to our implementation plan.

5. Cost & Value

Fixed-price removes uncertainty. You know the total cost upfront, and scope is clearly defined. We absorb efficiency gains and minor overruns. This aligns our incentives: we are motivated to build efficiently and correctly the first time.
All development, testing, deployment, documentation, a free n8n starter template, GDPR compliance matrix, and post-launch support. Tool subscription costs (approximately EUR 100-250/month) are separate and paid directly by Menovia.
Building in-house requires n8n expertise, GDPR knowledge, and integration experience across 8 tools. A typical Dutch automation agency quotes EUR 12,000-18,000 for comparable scope. Our fixed-price model and pre-built patterns (like the starter template) keep costs significantly lower.
