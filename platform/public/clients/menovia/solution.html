1. Patient Intake Flow

The first touchpoint a prospective patient has with Menovia sets the tone for everything that follows. This component ensures that every enquiry is captured cleanly, consented properly, and routed into the right workflow without any manual intervention.

📋
Screening Form
Typeform or Tally → n8n → Zoho CRM
1
Patient submits the screening form
The intake form (Typeform or Tally) collects the patient's details and health background. GDPR consent is captured on this same form before any data is stored anywhere.
2
n8n workflow triggers instantly
The form submission fires a webhook to n8n. The workflow validates the data, maps fields to CRM structure, and checks for duplicate records.
3
Contact created in Zoho CRM
A new contact is created (or an existing one updated) in Zoho CRM with the correct pipeline stage, package interest, and consent status recorded as custom fields.
4
Confirmation email sent via Brevo
An automated confirmation email in Dutch is dispatched immediately via Brevo, acknowledging the submission and inviting the patient to book their introductory call.
5
Booking prompt delivered
The email contains a direct link to Cal.com for the patient to schedule their introductory call. The booking link is pre-populated with their name and email where possible.
6
All steps log back to the CRM
Every action in this flow appends a timestamped activity note to the patient's CRM record: form received, email sent, booking link clicked. Nothing is invisible.
Typeform / Tally n8n Zoho CRM Brevo Cal.com
GDPR Note

Consent is recorded before any personal data is written to the CRM. The consent timestamp, form version, and consent text are stored as custom fields on the contact record. No data is processed without a valid consent entry.

2. Booking & Payment

Once a patient has expressed interest, frictionless scheduling and payment capture are critical to conversion. This component handles appointment booking, deposit collection, and the downstream notifications that kick off the clinical workflow.

📅
Scheduling & Deposit Collection
Cal.com → Mollie → Zoho CRM → Brevo / MessageBird
1
Patient books via Cal.com
Cal.com handles appointment scheduling with automatic Google Meet link generation. The patient selects a time slot that fits the physician's calendar, with no back-and-forth email.
2
n8n triggers a Mollie payment link
On booking confirmation, n8n immediately generates a Mollie payment link for the deposit amount corresponding to the patient's selected package (Basic, Plus, or Complete). The link is sent by email.
3
Payment confirmation triggers next steps
When Mollie confirms payment, n8n updates the CRM deal stage to "Deposit Paid", notifies the physician by email or SMS, and activates the pre-appointment communication sequence.
4
All three packages supported
Basic, Plus, and Complete packages are all handled with the correct deposit amounts. Subscription and recurring payment handling for ongoing packages is configured in Mollie and reflected in the CRM.
Cal.com Google Meet Mollie n8n Zoho CRM
Package Handling

Each package triggers a different payment amount and communication sequence. The CRM tracks which package the patient selected, and all downstream automations branch accordingly. Subscription renewals for Plus and Complete are handled automatically.

3. Communication Sequences

Consistent, timely communication is what turns a patient who books once into a patient who completes their full treatment journey. Every message below is automated and sent in Dutch, using Menovia's own drafts as the content foundation.

💌
Automated Email & SMS Flows
Brevo (email) + MessageBird (SMS) orchestrated by n8n

Appointment Reminders

  • Reminder sent 24 hours before each consultation with the Google Meet link
  • Reminder sent 6 hours before via SMS (MessageBird)
  • Final reminder sent 1 hour before via SMS

Blood Kit & Lab Workflow

  • Notification when blood kit is dispatched, including track-and-trace information
  • Confirmation email when the lab receives the sample
  • Results notification when the physician has reviewed findings

Treatment Journey

  • Treatment plan invitation email with next steps and portal access
  • Monitoring appointment scheduling prompt at the appropriate interval
  • Aftercare check-in at month 1, 3, 6, and 12 post-treatment
Brevo MessageBird n8n Zoho CRM
Content in Dutch

All email and SMS content is written in Dutch, using Menovia's own drafts as the starting point. Templates are stored in Brevo and can be edited by the Menovia team at any time without touching the automation logic.

5. Intercom Integration

Website visitors and existing patients often have questions before they are ready to commit. Intercom provides a live and automated chat layer that feeds directly back into the CRM, ensuring no conversation is ever lost.

💬
Chat & Support Routing
Intercom → Zoho CRM via n8n
1
Visitor starts a chat
When a website visitor begins a chat, Intercom checks whether their email matches an existing CRM contact. If it does, the conversation is linked to that patient record automatically.
2
FAQ and screening flows
Configured chat flows handle common pre-sales questions and can route visitors toward the intake form for more complex enquiries. Screening chat flows reduce time spent on unqualified leads.
3
Support routing by query type
Different query types (booking questions, medical questions, billing questions) are routed to the correct team member or queued appropriately, based on configured rules.
4
EU workspace for GDPR compliance
The Intercom workspace is configured on EU servers. Data residency is verified before any patient data flows through the integration.
Intercom n8n Zoho CRM
CRM Matching

Intercom conversations are matched to CRM records by email address. When a known patient starts a chat, the support agent immediately sees the patient's stage, package type, and recent activity without switching tools.

6. GDPR by Design

For a medical practice handling sensitive health data, compliance is not a checkbox exercise. Every component of this system is built with GDPR requirements as a first-class constraint, not an afterthought.

🔒
Compliance Architecture
Data governance built into every touchpoint

Consent Management

  • Consent captured at every data collection point before any data is stored
  • Consent timestamp, form version, and exact consent text stored as CRM fields
  • Separate opt-in fields for email communications and SMS communications
  • Opt-out flows for both channels that suppress future sends immediately

Data Hosting Verification

  • EU data hosting confirmed for every tool in the stack before integration
  • Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) documented for each vendor
  • Intercom workspace configured on EU servers specifically

Medical Data Boundary

  • Medical data never enters the CRM. Clinical records, diagnoses, and lab results remain exclusively in the NEN 7510-certified EHR system.
  • The CRM holds only administrative and communication data: contact details, pipeline stage, package type, appointment dates, and consent records.
  • n8n workflows are designed to pass identifiers only, never medical content, across system boundaries.

Data Retention

  • Data retention policy defined and documented per data category
  • Automated deletion or anonymisation triggers configured where technically feasible
  • Manual review process documented for categories requiring human judgment
