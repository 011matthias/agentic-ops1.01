Delivery Overview

You asked for "a working version within a few days, with room for improvements after testing." That's exactly how this is structured. Phase 1 gives you the working version. Phase 2 adds the AI and follow-up layer after you've confirmed the core pipeline works.

Why two phases instead of one

Shipping everything at once means if something's wrong with the sourcing logic, you don't find out until after the outreach emails have already gone out. By splitting into two phases, you can review the lead quality from Phase 1 before any messages get sent. It's a safety valve.

Phase 1: Core Pipeline ($500)

Days 1-4

Lead Sourcing + Storage + Basic Outreach

By the end of Phase 1, new leads from your target area are showing up in your Google Sheet automatically. You can review them, see their scores, and the basic outreach workflow is ready to send.

Phase 1 gate

We don't move to Phase 2 until you've reviewed the test outreach batch and confirmed the lead quality meets your expectations. If adjustments are needed to scoring weights or source targeting, those happen before any AI-generated emails go out.

Phase 2: AI + Follow-Up ($250)

Days 5-7

Phase 2 replaces the template-based outreach with AI-personalized emails and adds the automatic follow-up sequence. This is where the system goes from "functional" to "impressive."

DayDeliverableYou Can Verify
