About the Budget

Your posting is listed at $10 fixed. I want to be straightforward about why this proposal is $750.

This isn't a connect-two-apps job. It's a multi-workflow system with lead sourcing from 3+ channels, deduplication, AI-powered scoring, personalized outreach, and a 3-step follow-up sequence with reply detection. The scope is roughly 20-25 hours of design, development, and testing.

I could build something for $10 that dumps Google Maps results into a spreadsheet. But that's a list, not a system. It wouldn't score leads, personalize outreach, or follow up automatically. And it wouldn't give you the "minimal manual input" you asked for.

The $750 price reflects the full scope you described: "source leads, store and organize, send personalized outreach, automatically follow up, basic filtering and qualification." Every one of those is a distinct workflow with its own logic, error handling, and testing.
