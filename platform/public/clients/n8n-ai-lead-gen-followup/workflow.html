Workflow Architecture

How leads flow through the n8n pipeline, from sourcing to follow-up. Plus a live AI demo you can try right now.

n8n Workflow Structure

Workflow 1: Lead Sourcer

Runs on a cron schedule (configurable: daily at 6am, twice daily, or weekly). Hits Google Maps API, Yelp Fusion, and any configured directory feeds in parallel. Each source runs as a separate branch so a failure in one doesn't block the others. Output is a batch of normalized lead records.

Workflow 2: Qualify + Store

Receives the normalized leads. For each one: checks for duplicates against your Google Sheet, runs the scoring formula (online presence, reviews, business type, location, growth signals), and writes qualified leads to your sheet with their score. Leads scoring below a configurable threshold (default: 30) are stored but flagged as low-priority.

Workflow 3: AI Outreach

Picks up new leads above the outreach threshold (default: 50). For each lead, sends the profile to the AI with instructions to write a personalized email. The email is sent through your email provider with rate limiting. Each sent email is logged in your sheet with a timestamp and status.

Workflow 4: Follow-Up

Runs daily. Checks your sheet for leads that were contacted but haven't replied within the configured window. Sends follow-up messages according to the sequence (Day 3, Day 7, Day 14). If a reply is detected or the lead opts out, the sequence stops.

Data Flow: A Lead's Journey

Here's what happens to a single lead as it moves through the system. Take "Rosie's BBQ" as an example: a restaurant in Champaign that showed up in Google Maps results.

Step 1: Sourced

Google Maps API returns Rosie's BBQ with name, address, phone, rating (4.3), review count (187), and website URL. The Lead Sourcer normalizes this into the standard schema and passes it to Workflow 2.

Step 2: Deduplicated + Stored

Workflow 2 checks your Google Sheet for any existing record with "Rosie's BBQ" + "Champaign" (fuzzy match) or the same phone number (exact match). No match found, so it's a new lead. The enrichment sub-workflow visits rosiesbbq.com and extracts a contact email from the footer.

Step 3: Scored

The scoring formula runs: website exists (10), has social profiles (10), site loads (5) = 25/25 online presence. 187 reviews above threshold (15), 4.3 rating above 3.5 (10) = 25/25 review activity. Restaurant matches target category (15) = 15/20 type fit. Within primary radius (15) = 15/15 location. No growth signals detected (0) = 0/15. Total: 80/100. Strong lead.

Step 4: Outreach

Score is above the outreach threshold (50). The AI receives Rosie's profile: BBQ restaurant, 4.3 stars, 187 reviews, Champaign location, has a website. It writes an email that mentions their BBQ menu, their strong review presence, and proposes a specific next step relevant to your business.

Step 5: Follow-Up

No reply after 3 days. Follow-up 1 goes out: short, references the original email, adds one new piece of value. On Day 7, if still no reply, follow-up 2 tries a different angle. On Day 14, the final low-pressure message goes out and the sequence completes.
