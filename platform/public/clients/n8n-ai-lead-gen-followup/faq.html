Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers about how the system works, lead quality, compliance, and how we'd collaborate.

System and Reliability

What happens if a lead source goes down?

Each source runs as a separate branch in the n8n workflow. If Google Maps API is temporarily unavailable, the Yelp and directory sources keep running normally. The failed source logs an error and retries on the next scheduled run. You'll see a "last successful pull" date per source in your monitoring sheet, so you'll know immediately if something's been down for more than one cycle.

How do I know the system is actually working?

Three ways. First, your Google Sheet updates with new leads on schedule, so you can see activity in real time. Second, the monitoring tab in your sheet shows lead counts, outreach stats, and error rates at a glance. Third, if the error rate exceeds a threshold (default: 3 consecutive failures), n8n sends you an alert via email or Slack. The system is designed to be visible, not a black box.

What are the ongoing costs after setup?

n8n hosting: $20/month for n8n cloud, or free if you self-host. AI API costs (Claude or OpenAI): typically $5-10/month for a volume of 100-200 personalized emails per month. Google Maps API: free tier covers about 100 requests/day, which is more than enough. Yelp Fusion API: free tier covers 5,000 calls/day. Total ongoing cost for a typical setup: under $30/month.

Can I adjust the system after handoff?

Yes. You'll have full access to the n8n workflows, and the handoff documentation covers how to change scoring weights, add or remove lead sources, modify outreach templates, and adjust follow-up timing. The workflows use n8n's visual editor, so you don't need to write code. For more complex changes (adding a new API integration, changing the AI prompt logic), I'm available for follow-up work.

Lead Quality and Compliance

How do you prevent the system from sending spam?

Several layers. Rate limiting caps outreach to 30 emails/hour by default (adjustable per provider). Every email includes an opt-out mechanism. When someone opts out, their record is permanently flagged and excluded from all future outreach. Reply detection stops follow-ups the moment someone responds, even if the response is "not interested." The system also respects email provider sending limits to protect your sender reputation.

On the legal side, every outreach email includes your physical address (or a registered agent address) and a clear unsubscribe option, per CAN-SPAM requirements.

What if the AI writes something I wouldn't say?

That's why Phase 2 starts with a test batch. Before AI-generated emails go out at scale, you'll review 5 sample emails and confirm the tone, content, and approach feel right. If something's off, I adjust the AI prompt until the output matches your voice. You can also set up a manual approval step where emails queue for your review before sending, though this adds a bottleneck and defeats the "minimal manual input" goal.

How accurate is the lead scoring?

The scoring is rule-based with configurable weights, so its accuracy depends on how well the rules match your actual customer profile. The initial weights are based on general F&B patterns (online presence, review activity, business type, location), but after a week or two of data, you'll see which score ranges produce the best responses. Then we adjust the weights to reflect reality. It's designed to improve over time, not be perfect on Day 1.

Working Together

How do we communicate during the project?

Upwork messaging for day-to-day updates. I'll send a brief status update at the end of each day during the 7-day build. For the milestone reviews (Day 2, Day 4, Day 5, Day 7), I'll share a recorded walkthrough or detailed message showing what's been built and what needs your feedback. I'm in the CET timezone (Germany), which is 7 hours ahead of Champaign. I'll work during hours that overlap with your morning.

What do you need from me to get started?

Five things: (1) your target audience description (what kinds of F&B businesses you want to reach), (2) whether you prefer Google Sheets or Airtable, (3) email provider access (Gmail or other), (4) your n8n instance or a willingness to set one up on Day 1, and (5) examples of outreach emails you like. The onboarding form collects all of this.

What happens after the 7 days?

You own the system. The n8n workflows run in your instance. The Google Sheet is in your account. Post-go-live support is included for adjustments, troubleshooting, and questions. If you want to expand the system later (add more sources, build a dashboard, integrate with a CRM), that's a separate project we can scope together.
