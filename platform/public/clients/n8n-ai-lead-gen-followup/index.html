The Real Problem

Finding leads in Food & Beverage isn't the hard part. Restaurants, caterers, and food service businesses are everywhere. Google Maps, Yelp, industry directories, social media. The information is out there.

The hard part is what happens after you find them. You spend 20 minutes writing a personalized email, send it, get busy with other things, and never follow up. The lead goes cold. Multiply that by 50 leads a week and you've got a pipeline that leaks faster than you can fill it.

Most lead generation "systems" solve the easy part. They'll scrape a list of businesses and dump them into a spreadsheet. That's a list, not a system. A system decides which leads are worth pursuing, writes outreach that references something specific about that business, sends it at the right time, and follows up automatically when there's no response.

That's what this project builds.

Why this is a direct fit

Your posting asks for n8n, lead sourcing, outreach automation, and follow-up sequences. That's exactly what I build. I've designed production n8n workflows with this kind of multi-stage pipeline logic and built marketing systems that generated 600K+ weekly impressions. I understand lead generation strategy, not just the automation plumbing.

Four Outcomes

Leads Found Automatically

n8n pulls from Google Maps API, Yelp, and industry directories on a recurring schedule. New businesses in your target area show up in your pipeline without you searching for them.

Personalized Outreach at Scale

AI reads each lead's profile and writes an email that references their business specifically. Not a mail merge with {first_name}. An actual message that sounds like you wrote it after looking at their website.

Follow-up That Doesn't Depend on You

2-3 follow-up emails sent automatically over a configurable window. Reply detection stops the sequence immediately. No double-sends, no awkward "just checking in" after they've already responded.

Qualified, Not Just Collected

Every lead is scored before outreach begins. The score factors in business type, online presence, location fit, and growth signals. You see which leads are worth a phone call and which ones are long shots.
