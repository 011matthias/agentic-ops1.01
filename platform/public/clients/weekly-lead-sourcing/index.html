1. The weekly cycle

Every delivery runs the same repeatable process:

  1. Source from your three designated sources; each lead is tagged with where it came from
  2. Research and verify contact information for each lead before it enters the sheet
  3. Enter into your template exactly as provided, no improvised columns or formats
  4. Sample-check the batch against the template before handoff
  5. Deliver on your set day with a short completion note
Starting is low-risk
The first weekly list runs at the posted $20, so you can check accuracy, consistency, and data entry against your template before any ongoing commitment. The recurring weekly rate gets agreed after that, based on actual list size and research depth per cycle.

Availability: EU-based, overlapping your morning through early afternoon, roughly 9am to 2pm Pacific, every weekday.

2. What clean means

Clean data entry means the sheet needs no cleanup on your side:

  • Every contact field verified before it enters the template; unverifiable entries are flagged, never silently included
  • Duplicates checked against prior weekly deliveries, not just within the batch
  • Each delivery note lists lead count per source and anything off: duplicates removed, dead contacts, a source running dry
  • Questions and blockers get raised when they appear, not bundled into the deadline
