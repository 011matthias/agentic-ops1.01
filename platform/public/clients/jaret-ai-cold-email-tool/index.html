The Opportunity

I looked at your other open positions before writing this proposal. You've got the AI Cold Email Tool posting, a Sales Representative role for lead generation, and a WordPress CRM recommendation request. These aren't three separate problems. They're one pipeline: find leads, personalize outreach, and manage responses.

The system I'd build handles the first two stages automatically. Apollo.io finds the contacts, AI researches each company and writes personalized emails, Instantly.ai sends them on schedule. Your sales team (or that new sales rep) only steps in when someone replies. Two jobs, one workflow.
