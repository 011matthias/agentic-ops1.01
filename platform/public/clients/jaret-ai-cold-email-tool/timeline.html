Phase 4: Testing + Handoff

Checkpoint: Full pipeline runs without intervention. You've tested it yourself before we close out.

What Happens After

After handoff, the workflow is yours. You can adjust Apollo search filters, modify email templates, change sending schedules, and add new Instantly campaigns without touching the n8n nodes.

If something breaks or you want to extend the pipeline (reply detection, CRM integration, lead scoring), that's a separate conversation. No lock-in.
