Common Questions

Covering the technical details, pricing, and what happens after the pipeline is handed off.

Technical

What do I need to provide to get started?

Three API keys (Apollo.io, OpenAI, Instantly.ai) and a Google account for Sheets access. If you're running n8n on your own server, I'll also need SSH access. If you prefer n8n Cloud, I'll set it up there instead. The onboarding page collects all of this.

Can I use a different email tool instead of Instantly.ai?

Yes. The workflow is modular. The email delivery stage is one node group that can be swapped for Smartlead, Lemlist, or any tool with an API. The rest of the pipeline (scraping, AI research, email writing) stays the same.

How does the AI personalization actually work?

For each lead, OpenAI reads the company's website and extracts what they do, their industry, and their approximate size. It then writes a unique opening paragraph that references something specific about the company. No two leads get the same email. The prompts are tunable, so you can adjust the tone, length, and angle after handoff.

What about email deliverability?

Instantly.ai handles domain warm-up and sending limits automatically. I'll configure the initial warm-up schedule and set daily sending caps based on your domain age. The workflow also filters out invalid emails before they reach Instantly, which protects your sender reputation. If a lead bounces, they're automatically removed from the sequence.

How does this relate to our sales team?

This system handles prospecting and initial outreach at scale. Your sales team focuses on conversations with interested leads, not cold outreach. When someone replies to a cold email, the reply shows up in Instantly. From there, your sales rep takes over the conversation. The pipeline feeds them warm responses instead of cold lists.

Pricing

Why fixed-price instead of hourly?

Fixed-price means the scope is defined upfront. You know exactly what you're getting and what it costs. I've built similar pipelines before, so I can estimate accurately. If something takes me longer than expected, that's my problem, not yours.

Can I start with just one phase?

Yes. Phase 1 (Apollo.io lead scraping to Google Sheets) works as a standalone deliverable for $260. You'd get leads flowing into your sheet, and we can add the AI personalization and Instantly.ai stages later.

What's included in the 7 days of support?

Bug fixes only. If something I built breaks or doesn't work as documented, I'll fix it at no extra cost. Feature requests, new integrations, or changes to the pipeline scope are separate work.

After Delivery

Do I need technical skills to run this?

No. Once the workflow is set up, you run it by clicking a button in n8n (or it runs on schedule). Changing Apollo search filters is editing a text field. Updating email templates is editing a prompt. The Loom walkthrough shows all of this step by step.

Can I extend the pipeline later?

Yes. Common extensions: reply detection and routing to a CRM, lead scoring based on engagement, multi-channel outreach (LinkedIn plus email), and webhook triggers from your website. Each is a separate n8n node group that plugs into the existing pipeline.

What if I want to change the AI model later?

The OpenAI node is one step in the workflow. Swapping it for Claude, Gemini, or a local model is a configuration change, not a rebuild. The input and output format stays the same.
