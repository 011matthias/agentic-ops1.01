The three paths a message can take

Happy path: auto-resolved

Clear German "where is my order" message, order reference found, ERP returns clean tracking data, confidence high. The customer gets a correct German reply in seconds with the carrier, number, link, and status. Logged and done. No human touched it.

Caution path: drafted for review

The intent is one of the two supported types but something is uncertain: low extraction confidence, an ambiguous order match, or an Amazon message that the policy filter wants a person to confirm. The bot does all the work, then parks a ready-to-send German draft in the human queue. A person clicks send or edits first. Nothing wrong goes out automatically.

Escalation path: straight to a human

Out of scope, no order match after lookup, or a correction your ERP cannot safely apply. The customer gets a short honest German acknowledgement, and your team gets a structured handoff with the message, the detected intent, and what the bot already tried.

The Amazon difference

Amazon Message Center is not just another inbox. Buyer-seller messaging has rules: a response window, restrictions on links and contact details, no promotional content. The Amazon branch has its own policy filter so the bot never sends something that puts the seller account at risk. This is detailed on the FAQ page.
