What you asked for

An experienced developer to build an AI customer-support bot for your online shop. It should start by handling two request types: "Where is my shipment?" and the correction or update follow-ups on shipment tracking. You already have an ERP interface that returns the tracking link, tracking numbers, and shipping carrier. The bot answers customers through email (you use All-Inkl Webmail) and the Amazon Message Center. Customer communication has to be exclusively German. You want a reliable long-term partner for more AI automations after this one.

The one decision that makes this safe

A support bot that talks about shipments is only useful if it is never wrong about a shipment. So the architecture splits cleanly in two. A small focused language model call handles judgment: which order is this about, what is the customer actually asking, and how to phrase a natural German reply. Everything factual, the tracking number, the link, the carrier, the status, is pulled from your ERP by deterministic code and injected into the reply word for word. The model never writes a tracking number. If the ERP has no answer, the bot does not improvise, it hands the message to a person with full context.
