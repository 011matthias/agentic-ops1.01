Accuracy and trust

How do you guarantee the bot never gives a wrong tracking number?

By never letting the model produce one. The tracking number, link, carrier, and status are read from your ERP by plain code and inserted into the reply as exact strings. The model only writes the surrounding German sentence. It is structurally unable to invent a tracking number because it is never asked to generate one.

If the ERP returns nothing or something unexpected, the bot does not fill the gap with a guess. It stops the auto-reply and hands the message to a person.

What happens when the bot is not confident?

Every extraction returns a confidence score. Below a threshold you control, the bot still does all the work, drafts the German reply, then parks it in a human queue instead of sending. A person clicks send or edits first. The default posture is: when unsure, draft for a human, never auto-send a guess.

What if a customer writes in English or another language?

Customer communication stays exclusively German as you require. A non-German message is detected and, depending on your preference, either answered in German or routed to a person. This is a setting we agree on in phase 1, not a guess the bot makes silently.

Channels and Amazon

Why is Amazon Message Center treated differently from email?

Amazon buyer-seller messaging has rules that normal email does not: a response-time expectation, restrictions on links and contact details, and no promotional content. Sending the wrong thing there can affect the seller account, not just one customer.

So the Amazon branch has its own policy filter before anything is sent, and more cases there default to human review than on email. It is the part of this job most proposals will not mention, and it is exactly where a careless bot causes real damage.

Does All-Inkl Webmail need anything special?

All-Inkl provides standard IMAP and SMTP access behind the webmail. The bot connects over those with a dedicated mailbox or alias, so it never interferes with how your team reads mail. No plugin, no change to your webmail habits.

Scope, handoff, and the partnership

Which correction requests can the bot actually action?

That depends entirely on what your ERP interface lets an external system write back. Read-only tracking lookups are straightforward. Applying a correction (for example a delivery address change before dispatch) is only safe to automate if your ERP exposes a controlled endpoint for it. Where it does not, the bot acknowledges the customer in German and routes a structured handoff. Confirming exactly which corrections are in scope is the first onboarding step.

What similar work have you done?

Grounded LLM systems where a wrong answer has a real cost, the same judgment-vs-facts split used here. Document extraction with confidence scoring and human review queues. Email and marketplace message automation with safe handoff. The pattern on this project, small model for judgment plus deterministic code for anything that must be exact, is the pattern I use in production rather than a one-off idea for this proposal.

You want a long-term partner. What does that look like?

Every build ships with documentation, a runbook, and a handoff so you are never dependent on me to keep it running. After go-live, an optional monthly retainer covers monitoring, German phrasing tuning, and budgeted hours toward the next automation. The audit log makes the bot debuggable, which is what makes a real partnership possible instead of a black box you cannot inspect.
