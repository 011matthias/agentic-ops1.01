GDPR

How customer personal data is handled

A German shop processing customer and Amazon buyer data under the GDPR. This is how the bot stays inside that.

What data the bot touches

To answer "where is my shipment" the bot necessarily processes personal data: a customer name, an email address, an order reference, and a delivery-related address held in your ERP. This is normal order-fulfilment processing, but it has to be deliberate, not accidental.

The principles built into the design

Data minimisation

The bot only requests from the ERP what it needs to answer the specific message. It does not bulk-pull customer records. The model receives only the message text and the minimum fields needed for the judgment call, never your full ERP dataset.

Processing location and sub-processors

The model call is the one place data leaves your own systems. Both candidate providers offer data-processing terms suitable for business use, and the model can be set so message content is not used for training. The exact provider, region, and data-processing agreement are confirmed with you in phase 1, before any live customer message is processed. If you require EU-only processing, that constraint is set at the start, not retrofitted.

Retention

The audit log exists to make the bot debuggable and to meet your own record-keeping needs. Its retention period is your decision and is configurable. Old records are purged on the schedule you set rather than kept indefinitely by default.

Right to erasure and access

Because every interaction is logged against an order reference, a customer data request can be answered: their bot interactions can be found and removed on request. This is easier with the audit log than without it.

Honest scope note

I build the system to respect these principles and to give you the controls (retention, processing region, training opt-out, erasure support). I am not your data protection officer and this page is not legal advice. The data-processing agreement with the model provider and your own GDPR documentation are things we confirm together during phase 1.

Amazon buyer data, the extra rule

Amazon buyer data carries Amazon's own handling rules on top of the GDPR. The bot uses Amazon buyer messages only to answer the buyer through Amazon's own channel, never exporting that data elsewhere or reusing it for anything outside answering that specific message. This keeps both the GDPR position and the Amazon seller-account position clean.
