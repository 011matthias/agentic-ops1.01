How long this takes

You asked roughly how long implementation would need. Here is the honest version, split so you see value before the full commitment.

Two phases, not one big bang

The build is deliberately split. Phase 1 puts a working bot on your real email tickets in about two weeks, so you judge reply quality on actual customers before paying for the rest. Phase 2 adds the Amazon channel, the correction write-back, and the audit log. The estimate below assumes your ERP interface is reachable and documented at kickoff.

Phase 1, Core bot About 2 weeks
Goal: both intents answered in correct German over the All-Inkl email channel, grounded in your ERP, with human handoff working.
  • Days 1 to 2: ERP interface review, sample-ticket review, German template drafting, environment and credentials setup
  • Days 3 to 5: All-Inkl IMAP ingestion, message normalization, extraction call (order plus intent plus confidence)
  • Days 6 to 8: deterministic ERP lookup client, grounded German reply composition, confidence gate
  • Days 9 to 10: human handoff queue, audit logging, test pass on a real German ticket set, runbook and handoff doc
Phase 2, Full build About 2 to 3 more weeks
Goal: the Amazon Message Center channel live under its policy rules, safe correction write-back where the ERP allows it, and an audit view your team can read.
  • Amazon Selling Partner API messaging integration with the buyer-seller policy filter
  • Correction write-back for the correction types your ERP exposes a safe path for
  • Audit and handoff view so your team sees what the bot did and why
  • Tuning on real traffic from phase 1, threshold adjustment, German phrasing refinement
After the build

You mentioned wanting a long-term partner for more AI automations. Once the bot is live, an optional monthly retainer covers monitoring, German phrasing tuning as ticket patterns shift, and a budgeted slice of hours for the next automation. The shape is on the Investment page.
