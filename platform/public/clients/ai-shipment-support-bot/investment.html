You said you want a reliable partner for future AI automations. After go-live, an optional retainer of $600 per month covers monitoring, German phrasing tuning as ticket patterns change, and four hours toward the next automation. Month to month, cancel anytime, not required to start.

Language, LLM, and model

You asked which programming language, LLM, and model I would use. Direct answer: Python and n8n for the system, and a small instruction-following model used only for extraction and German phrasing, never for facts.

The model is set in one config value, so switching providers later is a one-line change, not a rebuild. This matters for a long-term relationship: you are not locked to one vendor's pricing.

Custom build vs a hosted helpdesk AI

Worth being honest about the alternative. Hosted support-AI products exist. They are a poor fit here for one reason: they cannot ground answers in your specific ERP interface and guarantee the tracking number is real. They generate plausible text. This build does the opposite, the facts are yours and exact, the model only phrases them. For "where is my shipment" that distinction is the whole product.
