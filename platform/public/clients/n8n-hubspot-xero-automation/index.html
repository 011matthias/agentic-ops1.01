What Gets Automated

Deal Won to Invoice

When a HubSpot deal moves to "Won", n8n picks up the deal details, maps the correct pricing tier from your custom field, finds or creates the contact in Xero, and sends the invoice automatically.

No more manual invoice creation. No more copy-pasting between HubSpot and Xero.

Form Submission to Google Drive

When someone fills out a form on the client's website (HubSpot Forms), n8n creates a new folder in Google Drive with a consistent naming convention and links it back to the HubSpot contact record.

Every new lead gets an organized workspace from day one.
