Workflow

Step-by-step data flow for both automations, including decision points and error paths.

Automation 1: Deal Won to Xero Invoice

Trigger: HubSpot Deal Stage Change

n8n listens for deal updates via webhook (Professional+) or polls the HubSpot deals API every 5 minutes (Starter). Filters for deals moving to the "Won" stage only.

Extract Deal Data

Reads the deal amount, associated contact (email, name, company), and the custom pricing tier field. Validates that all required fields are present.

Decision: Is the pricing field valid? If empty or unrecognized, the workflow flags the deal in HubSpot and sends a notification. No invoice is created until the field is corrected.

Map Pricing Tier

Routes to one of three pricing configurations based on the custom field value. Each tier defines its own line items, descriptions, and unit prices in Xero.

Lookup or Create Xero Contact

Searches Xero contacts by email. If found, uses the existing record. If not, creates a new contact with the name, email, and company from HubSpot.

Decision: Does the contact already have an invoice for this deal? If yes, skip creation to prevent duplicates. This catches scenarios where a deal is moved back to Won after being re-opened.

Create and Send Invoice

Builds the invoice in Xero with the mapped line items, tax settings, and a 30-day due date. Sends it to the contact email automatically.

Log to HubSpot

Writes the Xero invoice number, amount, and link back to the HubSpot deal as a note. Your team can see the invoice status without leaving the CRM.

Automation 2: Form Submission to Google Drive Folder

Trigger: HubSpot Form Submission

n8n receives a webhook when a form is submitted on the client's website. The payload includes all form fields and the submitter's details.

Extract Contact Details

Pulls the contact name, email, company name (if provided), and any custom fields relevant to folder naming.

Decision: Does a folder already exist for this contact? Checks the parent directory to prevent duplicate folders from repeat submissions.

Create Google Drive Folder

Creates a new folder in the designated parent directory. Naming follows a consistent convention: CompanyName - ContactName - YYYY-MM-DD

Create Subfolders

Sets up standard subfolders inside the new folder: Assets, Deliverables, Correspondence. Keeps every client workspace organized from the start.

Update HubSpot Contact

Writes the Google Drive folder URL to a custom property on the HubSpot contact record, making it one click away from the CRM.
