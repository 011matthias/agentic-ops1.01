System Architecture

Every support signal follows the same path: ingest, classify, route. The AI classification layer sits in the middle, reading each ticket, determining intent and urgency, and deciding whether to auto-resolve, escalate to support, or flag engineering.

How routing works

The confidence threshold gate is the key decision point. When Claude is 90%+ confident and the category matches a known resolution, the ticket auto-resolves. Below that threshold, it routes to human review with a pre-built summary so your team starts with full context instead of a blank ticket.
