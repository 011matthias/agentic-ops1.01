How It Works

Customer tickets arrive through your existing channels. An AI classification layer reads each ticket, determines intent and priority, and routes it to the right handler. Tier 0 issues get auto-resolved. Everything else gets enriched with context and sent to the right team with a structured summary.

📥
Triggers In
Where customer signals enter the system
  • Support ticket webhook
  • Email reply detection
  • Form submission
  • Monitoring alert
🤖
AI Classification
Claude API classifies intent, priority, and action
  • Intent detection (billing, technical, feature request)
  • Priority scoring (P1 through P4)
  • Confidence threshold gate
  • Context enrichment from knowledge base
✅
Actions Out
Automated responses and routing
  • Auto-resolve (Tier 0, high confidence)
  • Slack alert with structured summary
  • CRM ticket update
  • Engineering bug report (Tier 2)
Why this matters

Manual ticket triage doesn't scale. Response time directly predicts resolution satisfaction. This system classifies tickets in seconds, not hours, and your team walks into every issue already briefed on context, history, and suggested action.

What We Build

Three AI agents that share a common classification engine and output layer. Designed as one system, not three disconnected automations.

1
Tier 0: Auto-Resolver
Known issues with high confidence get resolved automatically. Generated response sent to customer, ticket tagged and closed.
2
Tier 1: Agent-Assisted
Medium-confidence tickets get enriched with CRM history and knowledge base context. Structured summary pushed to support team via Slack.
3
Tier 2: Engineering Escalation
Bug reports and infrastructure issues generate structured reports with repro steps, affected systems, and severity. Routed to engineering.
∞
Shared Infrastructure
Error handling, retry logic, confidence calibration, monitoring dashboards, and structured logging across all agents.
