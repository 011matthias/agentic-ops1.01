Delivery Schedule

4-Day Timeline

From DNS records to a fully working Zoom signup flow. Each day has a clear deliverable so you can track progress.

Day 1: Domain Authentication + Account Setup

1

Foundation

Estimated: 1-2 hours of work
  • Review your MailerLite account and current plan level
  • Add DKIM, SPF, and DMARC records to your domain DNS
  • Verify domain authentication in MailerLite
  • Connect Stripe account to MailerLite
  • Create Stripe product for Zoom session payment
What you need ready

Access to your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap, etc.) and your Stripe account credentials. If you do not have a Stripe account yet, creating one takes about 10 minutes.

Day 2-3: Landing Page + Form + Payment

2

Build

Estimated: 3-4 hours of work
  • Design landing page with your branding (logo, colors, copy)
  • Add signup form with name, email, and custom fields
  • Configure GDPR consent checkbox with terms link
  • Build terms popup (inline overlay, no page redirect)
  • Add Stripe payment block to the page
  • Save page as reusable template
  • Test form submission and payment flow end-to-end

Day 4: Email Automation + Testing + Handoff

3

Automation + Handoff

Estimated: 2-3 hours of work
  • Create email automation workflow (form submit trigger)
  • Design confirmation email with Zoom link and session details
  • Add subscriber grouping for session tracking
  • Full end-to-end test: signup, pay, receive email, verify Zoom link
  • Record Loom walkthrough showing how to duplicate the template
  • Handoff with written summary of what was set up and where
After handoff

7 days of support for questions. If something is not working or you need help duplicating the template for your first real session, I am available.
