The Signup Flow

Your users go through five steps, all handled automatically inside MailerLite. No manual intervention between signup and Zoom link delivery.

1
User visits your landing page
Clean, branded page with session details, date, and a clear call-to-action. Hosted on your authenticated domain.
↓
2
Fills out the signup form
Name, email, and any custom fields you need. Required terms checkbox with link to your full terms and a popup preview.
↓
3
Pays through Stripe
Native MailerLite-Stripe integration. The payment block is embedded directly on the page. No redirect to an external checkout.
↓
4
GDPR consent captured
MailerLite automatically records IP address, timestamp, subscription source, and consent text. Fully compliant consent trail.
↓
5
Zoom link delivered by email
MailerLite automation fires immediately after signup. Branded confirmation email with your Zoom link, session details, and calendar add link.

Reusable for Every Session

The landing page is saved as a MailerLite template. For each new Zoom session, you duplicate it and change three things:

  • Session title and date -- update the headline and any date references
  • Zoom link -- paste your new meeting link into the automation email
  • Stripe product -- select the correct product if pricing differs between sessions
5-minute setup per session

Once the template exists, launching a new Zoom signup page takes about 5 minutes. No design work, no code, no waiting on a developer.
