Common Questions

Everything you might want to know before we get started.

Setup Questions

Why $200 instead of $150? ▼

The $150 budget covers a basic landing page. Your project also includes Stripe payment integration, GDPR-compliant consent forms with a terms popup, domain authentication, and email automation. These add about 2 extra hours of careful configuration. The $50 difference pays for itself after your second Zoom session since you will not need to rebuild anything.

Do I need to upgrade my MailerLite plan? ▼

Stripe integration requires the Growing Business plan ($18/month) or higher. If you are on the Free plan, this is the only upgrade needed. The Growing Business plan also removes MailerLite branding from your landing pages and gives you more automation steps.

What do I need to provide before you start? ▼

Four things: (1) login access to your domain registrar for DNS records, (2) your MailerLite account login, (3) your Stripe account credentials, and (4) your terms and conditions text. The onboarding page collects all of this in one form.

Payment Questions

Do I need a Stripe account already? ▼

If you do not have one, creating a Stripe account takes about 10 minutes. You will need a bank account for payouts and basic business information. I can walk you through it if needed.

Can I change the price per session later? ▼

Yes. You create products in Stripe's dashboard. Each Zoom session can have a different price. When you duplicate the landing page template, you select which Stripe product to use for that session.

Compliance Questions

Is this GDPR compliant? ▼

The signup form meets GDPR active consent requirements: the checkbox is not pre-checked, consent text is clearly visible, and MailerLite automatically logs the IP address, timestamp, and subscription source for every signup. Each subscriber's consent history is stored in their profile and is downloadable for audits. However, I am not a lawyer. If you need formal legal review of your terms and conditions, consult a legal professional.

Do you write the terms and conditions? ▼

No. You provide the terms text and I configure the checkbox, link, and popup display. If you do not have terms written yet, I can point you to template resources or free generators, but drafting legal language is outside this project scope.

After Delivery

Can I reuse the template for different Zoom sessions? ▼

That is the whole point. The landing page is saved as a MailerLite template. For each new session, you duplicate it and change the session title, date, and Zoom link. The form, payment block, and email automation stay connected. The Loom walkthrough I record covers this exact process step by step.

What if I need changes after delivery? ▼

You get 7 days of post-delivery support for questions and minor adjustments. After that, MailerLite's drag-and-drop builder makes most changes easy to do yourself. If you need more substantial changes later, we can discuss a follow-up project.

What about Zoom registration? Does this replace it? ▼

This setup replaces Zoom's built-in registration. Instead of sending people to Zoom's generic registration page, they sign up on your branded landing page, pay through Stripe, and receive the Zoom link by email. You use a regular Zoom meeting link (not a registration link) in the confirmation email. This gives you full control over the signup experience and payment flow.
