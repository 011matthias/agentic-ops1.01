Why $200

Your posted budget was $150. I am proposing $200 because the combination of Stripe payment integration, GDPR-compliant consent forms, and a properly reusable template requires careful configuration that goes beyond basic landing page setup.

The extra $50 covers the Stripe connection, payment testing, GDPR consent chain, and the template architecture that makes future sessions a 5-minute duplication instead of a rebuild. This setup pays for itself after your second Zoom session.

What Is Included

  • Domain authentication -- DKIM, SPF, and DMARC configured and verified
  • Landing page template -- branded, mobile-responsive, saved as reusable template in MailerLite
  • Signup form -- name, email, custom fields, required terms checkbox with popup
  • Stripe payment block -- native integration, product created, embedded on page
  • GDPR consent setup -- required checkbox, terms link, popup display, automatic consent logging
  • Email automation -- form-triggered email with Zoom link, session details, and calendar add link
  • Loom walkthrough -- video showing how to duplicate the template for new sessions
  • 7 days post-delivery support -- questions, adjustments, help with your first template duplication

What Is Not Included

  • ✕Ongoing management -- this is a one-time setup with handoff, not a retainer
