Domain Authentication

Domain authentication tells email providers that MailerLite is authorized to send emails on behalf of your domain. Without it, your Zoom confirmation emails are more likely to land in spam.

What Gets Configured

Record TypePurposeWhere It Goes
DKIMCryptographic signature proving the email came from your domainCNAME record at your DNS registrar
SPFDeclares MailerLite as an authorized sender for your domainTXT record at your DNS registrar
DMARCPolicy telling receivers what to do with unauthenticated emailsTXT record at your DNS registrar
How it works

MailerLite generates the exact record values. I copy them into your DNS settings, click "Verify" in MailerLite, and confirm all three pass. DNS propagation usually takes 5-30 minutes, sometimes up to 24 hours.

Stripe Payment Integration

MailerLite has a native Stripe integration on the Growing Business and Advanced plans. No Zapier, no Make.com, no middleware.

How It Connects

  1. Connect your Stripe account to MailerLite via the Integrations page (one-click OAuth)
  2. Create a product in Stripe with the session price (e.g., $29.99 per session)
  3. Add a Stripe payment block to the landing page
  4. Select the product from the dropdown

Stripe handles all payment processing, receipts, and refunds through their dashboard. MailerLite records the payment event and triggers the automation.

Plan requirement

Stripe integration requires MailerLite Growing Business ($18/mo) or Advanced plan. If you are on the Free plan, this is the one upgrade needed. The Growing Business plan also removes MailerLite branding from your landing pages.

GDPR Consent Setup

MailerLite has built-in GDPR compliance tools. The signup form includes a required consent checkbox that cannot be pre-checked, meeting the "active consent" requirement.

What Gets Configured

ElementHow It Works
Terms checkboxRequired field. User cannot submit without checking. Label text links to your terms page.
Terms popupClicking the terms link opens a popup overlay with the full terms text, so users do not leave the page.
Consent recordMailerLite automatically stores: IP address, timestamp, subscription source, and the exact consent text shown.
Consent auditEach subscriber's profile shows their full consent history, downloadable for compliance audits.
Your responsibility

You provide the terms and conditions text. I configure the checkbox, popup display, and link. If you do not have terms written yet, I can point you to template resources, but drafting legal text is outside this scope.
