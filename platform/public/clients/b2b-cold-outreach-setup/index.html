2. How the tools fit together

One pipeline, in this order. Each tool has exactly one job:

Sales Navigator
Defines the ICP as saved searches, so the target list is reproducible, not a one-off export.
PhantomBuster
Exports those results and sends connection requests and DMs on a schedule that stays inside LinkedIn's limits.
Apollo
Enriches the same people with verified work emails and company data.
Email verification
Tiers every address (deliverable / catch-all / invalid) before it's saved. This is what makes the list clean.
Instantly
Separate sending domains, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, warm-up from day one, so the email channel is ready when you want it.
About 500 DMs in two weeks
A healthy LinkedIn account sustains roughly 100 connection requests and 50 to 80 automated DMs per week. If your 500 targets are already first-degree connections, two weeks works (about 35 a day). If they're cold, connections ramp first and the 500 completes over three to four weeks. Pushing past those limits gets accounts restricted, which costs more than the extra time.
